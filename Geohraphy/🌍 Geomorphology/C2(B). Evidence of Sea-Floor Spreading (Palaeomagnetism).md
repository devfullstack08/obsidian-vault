
# Why Was More Evidence Needed?

Harry Hess proposed Sea-Floor Spreading in 1962.

Although his theory explained many unanswered questions, scientists asked:

> "Where is the proof that the ocean floor is actually moving?"

To answer this question, geologists studied the Earth's magnetic field and volcanic rocks on the ocean floor.

This led to one of the strongest pieces of evidence in geology:

> **Palaeomagnetism**

---

# What is Geomagnetism?

The Earth behaves like a giant magnet.

It has:

- Magnetic North Pole
- Magnetic South Pole

A freely suspended compass always points toward the Earth's magnetic poles because of Earth's magnetic field.

```
        Magnetic North

              ↑

              │

         Earth's Magnetic Field

              │

              ↓

        Magnetic South
```

This magnetic field is called **Geomagnetism**.

---

# Important Fact

The Earth's magnetic poles are **not permanent**.

Over geological time, they have reversed many times.

This phenomenon is called

> **Magnetic Reversal** or **Geomagnetic Reversal**.

---

# Magnetic Reversal

Normally,

```
North → North
South → South
```

After reversal,

```
North → South
South → North
```

Scientists discovered that this reversal has happened **hundreds of times** in Earth's history.

---

# What is Palaeomagnetism?

"Palaeo" means **ancient**.

Therefore,

**Palaeomagnetism** means

> The study of ancient magnetism preserved in rocks.

---

# How Do Rocks Record Magnetism?

At Mid-Oceanic Ridges,

hot basaltic lava erupts continuously.

When the lava is molten,

its iron-rich minerals behave like tiny compass needles.

They align themselves with the Earth's magnetic field.

As the lava cools,

these minerals become permanently fixed.

Thus,

each basalt rock preserves the direction of Earth's magnetic field at the time it formed.

```
Molten Lava

↓

Iron Minerals Align

↓

Cooling

↓

Solid Rock

↓

Magnetic Direction Locked Forever
```

This process creates a natural magnetic record.

---

# Magnetometer

Scientists developed an instrument called a

> **Magnetometer**

It measures the magnetic properties of rocks.

By surveying the ocean floor, scientists discovered alternating magnetic bands.

---

# Discovery of Magnetic Stripes

The ocean floor contains long parallel bands of magnetized basalt.

```
MOR

||||||||||||||||||||

++++ Normal Polarity

---- Reverse Polarity

++++ Normal

---- Reverse

++++ Normal

---- Reverse
```

These magnetic stripes are:

- Parallel to the Mid-Ocean Ridge
- Equal on both sides
- Perfectly symmetrical

This was an extraordinary discovery.

---

# Why Are the Stripes Symmetrical?

Imagine:

1. Lava erupts at the ridge.
2. It cools and records the current magnetic polarity.
3. Ocean floor moves away.
4. New lava erupts again.
5. Earth's polarity has changed.
6. New lava records the new polarity.

Result:

Alternating magnetic bands are created.

```
Old ---- ++++ ---- ++++

           MOR

++++ ---- ++++ ---- Old
```

The pattern is identical on both sides.

This is known as **Magnetic Striping**.

---

# Vine-Matthews Hypothesis

Scientists

- Drummond Matthews
- Frederick Vine

explained these magnetic stripes.

Their hypothesis states:

> New basalt formed at Mid-Oceanic Ridges records the Earth's magnetic polarity. As sea-floor spreading continues and the magnetic field periodically reverses, symmetrical magnetic stripes develop on both sides of the ridge.

This became one of the strongest proofs of Sea-Floor Spreading.

---

# Age of Ocean Floor

Scientists measured the age of oceanic rocks.

They discovered:

```
Near MOR

↓

Youngest Rocks

Far Away

↓

Oldest Rocks
```

This perfectly matched Harry Hess's prediction.

---

# Rate of Sea-Floor Spreading

Scientists calculate the spreading rate using:

- Age of rocks
- Distance from Mid-Ocean Ridge

Formula:

```
Rate = Distance / Time
```

Average Rates

| Ocean    | Approximate Rate |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Atlantic | ~1 cm/year       |
| Indian   | ~1.5 cm/year     |
| Pacific  | ~6 cm/year       |

The Pacific Ocean spreads the fastest.
