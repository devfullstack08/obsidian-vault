# Introduction

Plate Tectonic Theory is the most accepted theory explaining the evolution of the Earth's surface.

However,

plates never remain fixed.

They continuously

- Break apart
- Move
- Collide
- Form mountains
- Create oceans
- Destroy oceans

This continuous process is called the

> Wilson Cycle.

---

# Wilson Cycle

## Definition

The Wilson Cycle is the life cycle of an ocean basin from its birth to its closure.

It was proposed by

> **J. T. Wilson**

# Modern Scientific Evidence

Scientists now have direct evidence supporting Plate Tectonic Theory.

---

## 1. GPS Measurements

Measure real-time plate movement.

---

## 2. Satellite Imaging

Shows continental movement.

---

## 3. Ocean Floor Mapping

Confirms Mid-Ocean Ridges.

---

## 4. Magnetic Stripes

Confirm sea-floor spreading.

---

## 5. Earthquake Distribution

Most earthquakes occur along plate boundaries.

---

## 6. Volcano Distribution

Most volcanoes occur near convergent boundaries.

# Why Plate Tectonic Theory is Accepted?

It successfully explains

✔ Continental Drift

✔ Ocean Formation

✔ Mountain Building

✔ Volcanoes

✔ Earthquakes

✔ Mid-Oceanic Ridges

✔ Ocean Trenches

✔ Island Arcs

✔ Sea-floor Spreading

✔ Plate Motion

No previous theory could explain all of these together.
