
# Introduction

A volcano is one of the most powerful natural phenomena on Earth.

It acts as a natural vent through which molten rocks, gases, ash and steam escape from the Earth's interior.

Volcanoes are closely related to Plate Tectonic Theory.

Most active volcanoes occur along plate boundaries.

---

# Definition

A volcano is an opening or vent in the Earth's crust through which magma, gases, ash and other volcanic materials are expelled onto the Earth's surface.

---

# Hotspots

Not all volcanoes occur at plate boundaries.

Some form in the middle of plates.

These are called

> Hotspots.

Definition

A hotspot is a fixed plume of hot mantle material rising from deep inside the Earth.

As plates move,

the hotspot remains fixed.


# Chapter Summary

✔ Volcanoes are openings through which magma reaches the surface.

✔ Magma becomes lava after eruption.

✔ Most volcanoes occur along convergent plate boundaries.

✔ Shield volcanoes are broad and quiet.

✔ Composite volcanoes are explosive.

✔ Hotspots create volcanoes away from plate boundaries.

✔ Hawaii formed due to a hotspot.

✔ Deccan Traps were formed by massive basaltic eruptions.

✔ The Pacific Ring of Fire contains the largest concentration of active volcanoes.
