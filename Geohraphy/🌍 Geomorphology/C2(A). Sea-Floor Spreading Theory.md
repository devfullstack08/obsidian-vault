
# Chapter 2A : Sea-Floor Spreading Theory

---

# Why Was a New Theory Needed?

Alfred Wegener's Continental Drift Theory was revolutionary because it proved that continents move.

However, scientists did not fully accept his theory because it had several unanswered questions.

### Major Problems

- What is the driving force behind continental movement?
- How do continents move?
- How are oceans formed?
- Why do earthquakes occur?
- How are volcanoes formed?
- How are mountains formed?

Scientists agreed that continents move.

But **they did not know HOW.**

This mystery remained unsolved for nearly 50 years.

---

# Ocean Exploration Changed Everything

During World War II (1939–1945),

scientists developed SONAR technology to detect submarines.

After the war,

the same technology was used to study the ocean floor.

Scientists expected the ocean floor to be flat.

Instead, they discovered an entirely different world.

---

# Major Discoveries

Scientists found:

✔ Long mountain ranges inside oceans.

✔ Deep trenches(ખાઈઓ, A long, narrow ditch.).

✔ Underwater volcanoes.

✔ Earthquakes concentrated along specific zones.

✔ Young rocks near ocean ridges.

✔ Old rocks far away from ridges.

These discoveries completely changed geology.

# Basic Idea

Harry Hess proposed that

> The ocean floor is continuously created and continuously destroyed.

Unlike continents,

the ocean floor is **not permanent.**

It is constantly renewed.

---

# What Causes Sea-Floor Spreading?

Harry Hess used Arthur Holmes' Mantle Convection Theory.

```
Heat inside Earth

↓

Mantle Convection

↓

Magma rises

↓

Creates new Oceanic Crust

↓

Ocean Floor spreads

↓

Old Crust moves away
```

Thus,

Mantle Convection became the driving force.

---

# Mid-Oceanic Ridge (MOR)

One of the greatest discoveries was

## Mid-Oceanic Ridge

Definition

A Mid-Oceanic Ridge is a long underwater mountain chain where new oceanic crust is continuously formed.

---

# Formation of MOR

```
        Ocean Water

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Old Crust ← ← ← ↑ → → → Old Crust

               Magma

          ↑ Rising Mantle

==============================

        Asthenosphere
```

Step-by-step

1. Mantle becomes hot.

2. Hot material rises.

3. Oceanic crust cracks.

4. Basaltic magma erupts.

5. Lava cools.

6. New oceanic crust forms.

7. Ocean floor moves away from the ridge.

This process is called

> Sea-Floor Spreading.

---

# Why Basalt?

Oceanic crust mainly consists of

> Basalt.

Basalt is formed when lava cools rapidly under seawater.

Therefore,

new ocean floor is always basaltic.

# Age of Ocean Floor

Scientists discovered

Ocean floor is surprisingly young.

Maximum age

≈ 200 million years.

No oceanic crust is older than this.

Question

Why?

Answer

Because old ocean floor is continuously destroyed.

---

# Trenches

If new crust is continuously formed,

then why is Earth not becoming larger?

Harry Hess answered this question.

---

# Ocean Trenches

Definition

A trench is a long, narrow, extremely deep depression on the ocean floor.

Example

- Mariana Trench
- Java Trench
- Diamantina Trench
- Tonga Trench

---

# Formation of Trenches

Oceanic crust becomes older.

↓

Older crust becomes colder.

↓

Colder crust becomes denser.

↓

Denser crust sinks into the mantle.

↓

Melts again.

```
Ocean

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Old Crust

↓

↓

↓

Trench

↓

↓

Mantle

↓

Melting
```

Thus,

new crust is created at MOR

and

old crust is destroyed at trenches.

# Why Continents Move

Continents are not moving independently.

Instead,

they sit on large lithospheric plates.

As new oceanic crust forms,

the entire plate slowly moves.

Therefore,

continents also move.

This solved one of the biggest problems left by Wegener.

---

# Success of Sea-Floor Spreading Theory

Harry Hess successfully explained

✔ Formation of new oceanic crust.

✔ Expansion of oceans.

✔ Driving force of continental movement.

✔ Formation of Mid-Oceanic Ridges.

✔ Formation of Ocean Trenches.

✔ Recycling of ocean floor.

---

# Limitations

Sea-Floor Spreading Theory explained

movement of ocean floor,

but it still could not completely explain

- Fold mountain formation.
- Earthquake distribution.
- Plate interaction.
- Continental collision.

These questions were finally answered by

> Plate Tectonic Theory.
