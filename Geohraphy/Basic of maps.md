#Africa
Africa is the only continent crossed by the Equator, the Tropic of Cancer, and the Tropic of Capricorn.
1. Tropic of Cancer passes through:Western Sahara, Mauritania, Mali, Algeria, Niger, Libya, Egypt.
2. Equator passes through:São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of Congo, Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda, Kenya, Somalia.
3. Tropic of Capricorn passes through:Namibia, Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar.
The **Congo River** flows in Central Africa. It is one of the largest rivers in the world and is the **second-longest river in Africa** after the Nile. Its path curves in such a way that it crosses the Equator two times.
The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram.

## longitudes (vertical) : connect to north pole to the south pole

**Prime Meridian** = **0° longitude**  
It passes through **Greenwich, London, United Kingdom**. That is why it is called the **Greenwich Meridian**.
The International Date Line is an imaginary line near 180° longitude where the calendar date changes by one day.
