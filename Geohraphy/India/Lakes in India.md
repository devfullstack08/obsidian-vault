# Special Lakes for Exams

## Chilika Lake

Chilika Lake is located in Odisha.

It is the largest brackish water lagoon in India.

It is famous for migratory birds and Irrawaddy dolphins.

## Loktak Lake

Loktak Lake is located in Manipur.

It is famous for floating islands called phumdis.

Keibul Lamjao National Park, the only floating national park in the world, is located on Loktak Lake.

## Sambhar Lake

Sambhar Lake is located in Rajasthan.

It is the largest inland saltwater lake in India.

It is important for salt production.

## Wular Lake

Wular Lake is located in Jammu and Kashmir.

It is one of the largest freshwater lakes in India.

It helps in flood control of the Jhelum River.

## Vembanad Lake

Vembanad Lake is located in Kerala.

It is the longest lake in India.

It is important for Kerala backwaters and tourism.

## Lonar Lake

Lonar Lake is located in Maharashtra.

It is a crater lake formed by a meteorite impact.

It has both geological and ecological importance.

## Pulicat Lake

Pulicat Lake lies on the border of Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.

It is the second-largest brackish water lagoon in India.

Sriharikota Island separates Pulicat Lake from the Bay of Bengal.
