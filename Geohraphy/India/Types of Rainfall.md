In Indian geography, rainfall is mainly classified into **three types according to the mechanism that causes moist air to rise and cool**.

1. **Convectional Rainfall** (संवहनीय वर्षा)
2. **Orographic / Relief Rainfall** (पर्वतीय वर्षा)
3. **Cyclonic / Frontal Rainfall** (चक्रवाती / वाताग्री वर्षा)

All three occur in India. However, **the southwest monsoon is the main source of India's annual rainfall**. Monsoon rainfall itself can involve several of these mechanisms.
