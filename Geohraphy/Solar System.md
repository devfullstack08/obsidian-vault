The Solar System is the family of the Sun and all the objects that move around it.

It includes:
- The Sun
- Eight planets
- Dwarf planets
- Moons
- Asteroids
- Comets
- Meteoroids
- Dust and gas

The Sun is at the center of the Solar System, and the planets revolve around it.


The Solar System consists of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, meteoroids, dust, and gas.

The Sun is at the center of the Solar System.

The eight planets in order are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

The inner planets are rocky planets, while the outer planets are gas giants and ice giants.

Pluto was earlier considered the ninth planet, but it is now classified as a dwarf planet.

## Basic Structure

Sun → Planets → Moons → Asteroids → Comets → Meteoroids

## Important Point

The Solar System is part of the **Milky Way Galaxy**.

# Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the Solar System.

It gives heat and light to the planets.

Life on Earth depends on the Sun’s energy.

The Sun is mainly made of hydrogen and helium gases.

## Importance of the Sun

| Point                          | Explanation                               |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Heat and light                 | Provides energy to Earth                  |
| Gravity                        | Holds planets in their orbits(ભ્રમણકક્ષા) |
| Photosynthesis(પ્રકાશસંશ્લેષણ) | Helps plants make food                    |
| Climate                        | Controls weather and seasons              |

# Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.

It is the smallest planet in the Solar System.

It has no major atmosphere, so temperatures change greatly between day and night.

## Pluto

Pluto was earlier called the ninth planet.

In 2006, it was reclassified as a dwarf planet.

So today, the Solar System has eight planets.

# Asteroids, Comets, and Meteoroids

| Object    | Meaning                                         |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Asteroid  | Small rocky object that revolves around the Sun |
| Comet     | Icy body that forms a tail near the Sun         |
| Meteoroid | Small rock or dust particle in space            |
| Meteor    | Meteoroid burning in Earth’s atmosphere         |
| Meteorite | Part of meteoroid that reaches Earth’s surface  |

## Asteroid Belt

The asteroid belt is located between Mars and Jupiter.

It contains many rocky objects called asteroids.

## Comet

A comet is made of ice, dust, and rock.

When it comes near the Sun, it forms a glowing tail.

# Rotation and Revolution

| Term       | Meaning                             | Example                                         |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Rotation   | Spinning of a planet on its axis    | Earth rotates once in about 24 hours            |
| Revolution | Movement of a planet around the Sun | Earth revolves around the Sun in about 365 days |

## Result of Rotation

Rotation causes day and night.

## Result of Revolution

Revolution causes a year.

Earth’s tilted axis and revolution together cause seasons.
