| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Western Europe

| Country            | Description                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| France             | Known for its culture, gastronomy, and the capital city of Paris.                                  |
| Germany            | The most populous country entirely within Central/Western Europe, featuring major industrial hubs. |
