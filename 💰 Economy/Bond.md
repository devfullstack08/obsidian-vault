In any financial 
- plan,
- investment scheme, 
- government scheme,
- insurance scheme, or membership plan, terms such as Bond, Bond Number, Issue Date, Bond Amount, and Bond Document are used to maintain records of a financial commitment or legal obligation.

However, their exact meaning depends on the type of scheme.

## 1. Meaning of each term

|Term|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Bond|A financial or legal instrument representing an obligation, such as repayment of money or fulfilment of a guarantee.|Investment bond|
|Bond Number|A unique identification number assigned to a bond.|BND-2026-000125|
|Issue Date|The date on which the bond is officially issued.|16 October 2026|
|Bond Amount|The monetary value associated with the bond, such as its principal or guaranteed amount.|₹50,000|
|Bond Document|A certificate or agreement containing the bond's terms and details.|PDF certificate|

## 3. Why is a bond document used in a scheme?

There are several important reasons:

1. Proof of agreement: Records the financial obligation between the issuer and bondholder.
    
2. Unique identification: The bond number helps identify a particular bond in the company's records.
    
3. Verification: The company can check the bondholder, amount, issue date and terms.
    
4. Payment and maturity tracking: Helps track interest, repayment dates and outstanding obligations.
    
5. Legal evidence: Can support a claim or dispute, depending on the document's validity and terms.
    
6. Record keeping: Helps companies maintain financial and audit records.
    

Important: Not every plan or scheme needs a bond. A simple membership or subscription plan may only require an invoice or receipt. Also, issuing a bond certificate does not automatically make an investment scheme legitimate, secure or government-approved.

## 4. Difference between Bond, Certificate, and Agreement

| Document        | Purpose                                                              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bond            | Creates or records a financial/legal obligation.                     |
| Certificate     | Confirms that a bond, investment, or membership exists.              |
| Agreement       | Defines the rights, responsibilities, and conditions of the parties. |
| Payment Receipt | Confirms that a payment was made.                                    |

A bond certificate may also serve as evidence of an agreement, but the two are not always interchangeable.
