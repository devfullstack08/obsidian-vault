
**1947 is one of the most important years in modern Indian history** because India:

- became independent from British rule;
- was partitioned into **India and Pakistan**;
- faced one of the largest migrations in modern history;
- began integrating more than 500 princely states;
- converted the Constituent Assembly into a sovereign constitution-making body;
- started building the political and administrative foundations of the Republic.

> [!summary]  
> **1947 = Independence + Partition + Princely State Integration + Beginning of Nation-Building**
>


## Political Situation Before Independence

By the beginning of 1947, British withdrawal from India had become almost certain.

Important reasons included:

- weakening of Britain after World War II;
- growth of the Indian national movement;
- INA trials and public unrest;
- Royal Indian Navy uprising of 1946;
- communal tensions;
- failure of Congress and Muslim League to agree on a constitutional arrangement;
- increasing difficulty of governing India.

India became independent on:

> **15 August 1947**

Jawaharlal Nehru became:

> **India's first Prime Minister**

Lord Mountbatten became:

> **First Governor-General of independent India**

Dr Rajendra Prasad continued as:

> President of the Constituent Assembly

## Nehru's "Tryst with Destiny" Speech

On the night of **14–15 August 1947**, Jawaharlal Nehru delivered his famous speech:

> **"Tryst with Destiny"**

The speech emphasised:

- freedom;
- responsibility;
- service to India;
- removal of poverty;
- ending inequality;
- national unity.

It became one of the most famous speeches in Indian political history.


## Partition of India

British India was divided into:
### Dominion of India
and
### Dominion of Pakistan

Pakistan initially consisted of:
- West Pakistan;
- East Pakistan.

East Pakistan later became:

> **Bangladesh in 1971**
> 

## Radcliffe Line

The boundaries between India and Pakistan were determined by Boundary Commissions chaired by:

> **Sir Cyril Radcliffe**

The resulting boundary became known as:

> **Radcliffe Line**

It divided:

- Punjab;
- Bengal.

The boundary award was publicly announced shortly after independence.


## Partition Violence

Partition was accompanied by extensive communal violence.

Major affected areas included:

- Punjab;
- Bengal;
- Delhi;
- parts of northern India.

Communities affected included:

- Hindus;
- Muslims;
- Sikhs.

Large numbers of people were:

- killed;
- injured;
- displaced;
- separated from families;
- forced to abandon property.

## Refugee and Migration Crisis

Partition caused one of the largest population movements of the twentieth century.

Millions of people moved:

```
Muslims
India → Pakistan

Hindus and Sikhs
Pakistan → India
```

The new Indian government had to deal with:

- refugee camps;
- rehabilitation;
- housing;
- abandoned property;
- employment;
- food;
- law and order.

> [!mains-point]  
> Independence brought political freedom but was simultaneously accompanied by enormous humanitarian suffering.

## Mahatma Gandhi in 1947

While national leaders celebrated independence in Delhi, **Mahatma Gandhi was not present at the main independence celebrations**.

He concentrated on:

- ending communal violence;
- Hindu-Muslim unity;
- peace efforts.

He spent important periods in:

- Bengal;
- Delhi.

Gandhi considered communal harmony more important than political celebration.

##  Integration of Princely States

At independence, India contained hundreds of princely states.

British paramountcy over them ended with independence.

The princely rulers were encouraged to accede to either India or Pakistan based mainly on:

- geographical location;
- political realities;
- interests of their people.

## Role of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Sardar Patel became:

> **Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister of India**

He played the central political role in integrating princely states.

Patel worked closely with:

> **V. P. Menon**

Their strategy included:

- diplomacy;
- persuasion;
- Instruments of Accession;
- political pressure;
- administrative negotiation.

> [!exam-note]  
> **Sardar Patel = Architect of Political Integration of India**


## Instrument of Accession

Princely rulers could join India by signing an:

> **Instrument of Accession**

Initially, accession commonly transferred authority over subjects such as:

- defence;
- external affairs;
- communications.

Later, integration became more complete.

## Formation of Saurashtra – Connection with 1947

The actual **United State of Saurashtra** was formed in:

> **1948**

But the groundwork began during **1947**, when princely rulers began negotiating accession and integration with India.

So remember:

```
1947 → Accession process

1948 → Formation of Saurashtra State
```
