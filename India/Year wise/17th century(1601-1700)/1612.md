Prince Khurram (later Shah Jahan) married Arjumand Banu Begum (later known as Mumtaz Mahal) in 1612.
(Khurram later became Emperor Shah Jahan. Mumtaz Mahal was the woman in whose memory the Taj Mahal was built.)
She was the daughter of Asaf Khan, brother of Nur Jahan. The marriage strengthened Khurram's connections with an influential family at Jahangir's court.
