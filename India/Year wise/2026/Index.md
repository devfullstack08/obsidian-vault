
### FIFA World Cup 2026

The **FIFA World Cup 2026** is the 23rd edition of the men’s international football championship organized by FIFA. It is being jointly hosted by **Canada, Mexico and the United States**, making it the first World Cup hosted across three countries.

The tournament runs from **11 June to 19 July 2026**. The opening match is in Mexico City, while the final will be played in the New York–New Jersey area.
#### Key features

- **48 national teams**, increased from 32 in previous editions
- **12 groups of four teams**
- The top two teams from each group, plus the eight best third-placed teams, advance to the knockout stage
- **104 matches** across 16 host cities
- Argentina entered the tournament as the defending champion after winning the 2022 World Cup

The 2026 tournament is the largest FIFA World Cup so far in terms of participating teams, matches and host locations.

**Spain won the FIFA World Cup 2026.** Spain defeated **Argentina 1–0 after extra time** in the final on **19 July 2026**. Ferran Torres scored the winning goal in the 106th minute. It was Spain’s **second men’s World Cup title**, after their first victory in 2010.

### 100 Years Since the General Strike in Britain, 1926

#### Background
The General Strike was organised by the Trades Union Congress in support of coal miners.

#### Causes
- Reduction in miners’ wages
- Longer working hours
- Decline of the coal industry
- Conflict between workers and mine owners
- Government economic policy

#### Importance
- It was one of Britain’s largest coordinated labour actions.
- It demonstrated the strength and limits of trade union power.
- The strike lasted approximately nine days.
- The miners continued their struggle after the general strike ended.

#### 2026 Significance
The year 2026 marks the **centenary of the British General Strike**.

### India Connection
It can be compared with the growth of India’s trade union movement and the Trade Unions Act, 1926.
