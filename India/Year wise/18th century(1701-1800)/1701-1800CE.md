
| Date / Year     | Event and significance                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1707            | Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb died on 3 March, accelerating political struggles within the Mughal Empire.                                                                                                  |
| 1709            | Two rival English East India Companies completed their merger into the United Company of Merchants of England Trading to the East Indies.                                                             |
| 1717            | Mughal Emperor **Farrukhsiyar** issued a Farman granting significant trading privileges to the English East India Company, including customs concessions in Bengal.                                   |
| 1746–1748       | First Carnatic War between the English and French in India, linked to the War of Austrian Succession in Europe.                                                                                       |
| 1749–1754       | Second Carnatic War — English and French companies supported rival claimants in the Carnatic and Hyderabad.                                                                                           |
| 1751            | Robert Clive captured Arcot, strengthening the English position during the Second Carnatic War.                                                                                                       |
| 1756            | Siraj-ud-Daulah, Nawab of Bengal, captured Calcutta from the English East India Company.                                                                                                              |
| 23 June 1757    | Battle of Plassey: Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah, establishing decisive Company political influence in Bengal.                                                                                |
| 1758–1763       | Third Carnatic War: English and French rivalry continued during the Seven Years' War.                                                                                                                 |
| 22 January 1760 | Battle of Wandiwash: English forces under Eyre Coote defeated the French under Count de Lally.                                                                                                        |
| 22 October 1764 | Battle of Buxar: Company forces under Hector Munro defeated the combined armies of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daula and Shah Alam II.                                                                        |
| 12 August 1765  | Treaty of Allahabad: Mughal Emperor Shah Alam II granted the Company Diwani (revenue collection) rights over Bengal, Bihar and Orissa.                                                                |
| 16 August 1765  | Treaty of Allahabad with Shuja-ud-Daula: Robert Clive restored Awadh to its Nawab under conditions, including a war indemnity(ક્ષતિપૂર્તિ) of ₹50 lakh, strengthening Company influence.0             |
| 1765            | Dual Government introduced in Bengal: Robert Clive established an arrangement in which the Company controlled revenue (Diwani) while the Nawab nominally retained administrative functions (Nizamat). |
| 1767–1769       | First Anglo-Mysore War: Hyder Ali of Mysore fought the East India Company and its changing allies in southern India.                                                                                  |
| 29 March 1769   | Treaty of Madras: Ended the First Anglo-Mysore War, with provisions for mutual assistance.                                                                                                            |
| 1769–1770       | Great Bengal Famine: A devastating famine caused mass deaths and exposed serious problems in Bengal's economy and administration.                                                                     |
| 1772            | Warren Hastings abolished Dual Government in Bengal, bringing revenue administration more directly under Company control.                                                                             |
| 1773            | Regulating Act: First major British parliamentary attempt to regulate the East India Company's administration in India.                                                                               |
| 1774            | Supreme Court established at Calcutta; Warren Hastings became the first Governor-General of Bengal under the new arrangement.                                                                         |
| 1775–1782       | First Anglo-Maratha War: Conflict between the Company and the Marathas, ending with the Treaty of Salbai.                                                                                             |
| 1780–1784       | Second Anglo-M`ysore War: Hyder Ali and later Tipu Sultan fought the Company; ended with the Treaty of Mangalore.                                                                                     |
| 1781            | Act of Settlement: Clarified and restricted the Calcutta Supreme Court's jurisdiction.                                                                                                                |
| 1784            | Pitt's India Act: Established a Board of Control, strengthening British government supervision over the Company.                                                                                      |
