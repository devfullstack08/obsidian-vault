9 January:
- **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (પ્રવાસી ભારતીય દિવસ)** 🇮🇳
	- **First celebrated:** 9 January 2003.
	- **Why 9 January?** On this day in **1915, Mahatma Gandhi returned to India from South Africa**.
	- **Organised by:** Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India.
	- **Purpose:** Recognise the contributions of overseas Indians (Indian diaspora) to India's development.
	- **Frequency:** The Pravasi Bharatiya Divas Convention has been held **biennially (every two years)** since 2015.

15 January: 
- Indian Army Day 🇮🇳 (15 January 1949)
	- **Why 15 January?** On this day in 1949, **General K. M. Cariappa** became the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army.
	- **Succeeded:** General Sir Francis Butcher, the last British Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army.
	- **Significance:** Marks the transfer of Indian Army leadership from British to Indian command.
