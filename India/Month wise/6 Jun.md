
5 June:
- World Environment Day (Since 1973)

21 June:
- International Yoga Day (Since 2015)
	- **First celebrated:** 21 June 2015 
	- **Proposed by:** India (PM Narendra Modi)
	- **Declared by:** United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)
	- **UN Resolution adopted:** 11 December 2014
	- **Why 21 June?** It coincides with the summer solstice, the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.
