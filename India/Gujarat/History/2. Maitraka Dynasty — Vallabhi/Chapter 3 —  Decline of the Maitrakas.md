
This is one area where you should avoid an oversimplified statement.

Maitraka power declined during the **8th century**.

Possible factors included:

- weakening central authority
    
- repeated external attacks
    
- regional political competition
    
- Arab pressure from Sindh and western coastal routes
    

Arab incursions affected western India during this period.

However, historians do **not have one perfectly documented event** that clearly explains the exact destruction of Vallabhi.

The Maitraka dynasty disappeared by approximately the **late 8th century**, around 775–780 CE. 

So for exam notes avoid:

> “Arabs definitely destroyed Vallabhi in one specific invasion.”

Better:

> **Arab invasions probably contributed to Maitraka decline, but the exact circumstances of the dynasty's end remain uncertain.**

## Historical Importance of the Maitrakas

The Maitrakas are important because they:

1. filled the political vacuum after Gupta decline in Gujarat
    
2. established Vallabhi as a major western Indian capital
    
3. maintained a strong regional kingdom for about three centuries
    
4. promoted higher education
    
5. patronized several religious traditions
    
6. strengthened Gujarat's commercial importance
    
7. preserved administrative traditions through inscriptions and grants
    
8. played a major role in Gujarat's transition from ancient to early medieval history

## Most Important Facts

Memorize these:

> **Founder → Bhatarka**

> **Capital → Vallabhi**

> **Region → Saurashtra / Gujarat**

> **Period → approximately 5th–8th centuries CE**

> **Origin → after decline of Gupta authority**

> **Bhatarka's title → Senapati**

> **Dronasimha → Maharaja**

> **Most rulers → Shaivites**

> **Vallabhi → major centre of education**

> **Chinese traveller → Xuanzang**

> **Another traveller → I-Tsing**

> **Important religions → Shaivism, Buddhism, Jainism**

> **Political contemporary → Harshavardhana**

> **Decline → late 8th century; Arab pressure among contributing factors**

## Common Traps

Be careful:

**Vallabhi was NOT the capital of the Solankis.**

Solanki capital:

> **Anahilavada Patan**

Maitraka capital:

> **Vallabhi**

---

**Bhatarka was NOT originally an independent emperor.**

He began as:

> **Senapati under the declining Gupta system**

---

**Maitrakas were NOT exclusively Buddhist.**

Most rulers were primarily:

> **Shaiva**

but they patronized different religions.

---

**Vallabhi was NOT only a religious monastery.**

It was simultaneously:

> political + commercial + educational + religious centre.

## One-Minute Revision

Remember this story:

> Gupta Empire weakens → military commander **Bhatarka** establishes himself in Saurashtra → **Maitraka dynasty** develops → **Vallabhi** becomes capital → Maitrakas expand through Gujarat → Vallabhi becomes famous for learning → Xuanzang and I-Tsing describe its importance → dynasty maintains relations/conflicts with other powers including Harsha → Shaivism dominates royal symbolism but Buddhism and Jainism flourish → political and external pressures weaken the dynasty → Maitraka rule disappears in the late 8th century.
