
The **Maitrakas** were a major ruling dynasty of western India, especially Gujarat, from roughly the **late 5th century to the late 8th century CE**. Their capital was **Vallabhi** in present-day Gujarat.

The dynasty emerged after the weakening of the **Gupta Empire**.

The founder was:

> **Bhatarka**

He had been a **Senapati, or military commander/governor, in Saurashtra under the Guptas** and gradually established an independent regional power.

### Memory line

> **Gupta decline → Bhatarka → Maitraka Dynasty → Vallabhi**

---

## Founder: Bhatarka

**Bhatarka** is regarded as the founder of the Maitraka dynasty.

Approximate period:

> c. 475 CE onward

Initially he did not immediately use a grand imperial title.

He used the title:

> **Senapati**

This suggests that Maitraka power initially developed out of the administrative-military structure of the declining Gupta Empire. His successor **Dharasena I** also used the title _Senapati_. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maitraka_dynasty?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Maitraka dynasty"))

Later rulers gradually adopted higher royal titles.

That gives us an important political progression:

```text
Gupta military governor
        ↓
Bhatarka — Senapati
        ↓
Regional autonomy grows
        ↓
Later Maitrakas become independent kings
```

## Capital: Vallabhi

The capital of the Maitraka dynasty was:

> **Vallabhi**

It was located in the Saurashtra region of Gujarat.

Vallabhi developed into an important:

- political centre
    
- commercial centre
    
- religious centre
    
- educational centre
    

The city eventually became famous far beyond Gujarat because of its institutions of learning


> **Maitraka → Vallabhi**

Never confuse it with:

> **Chaulukya/Solanki → Anahilavada Patan**
