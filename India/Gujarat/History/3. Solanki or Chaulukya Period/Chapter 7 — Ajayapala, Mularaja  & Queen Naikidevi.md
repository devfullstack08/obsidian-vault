This chapter marks the **beginning of the decline phase after Kumarapala**, but it also contains one of the most important military events in Gujarat history: the **defeat of Muhammad of Ghor's invasion in 1178 CE**.

## 1. Ajayapala — c. 1171/72–1175 CE

Ajayapala succeeded **Kumarapala**.

His reign was short.

He is generally remembered as a ruler who continued Chaulukya authority, but the kingdom had already entered a more unstable phase after the long reigns of Siddharaja and Kumarapala.

### Quick facts

|Point|Detail|
|---|---|
|**Ruler**|Ajayapala|
|**Reign**|c. 1171/72–1175 CE|
|**Predecessor**|Kumarapala|
|**Successor**|Mularaja II|
|**Capital**|Anahilapataka / Patan|

### Memory line

> **Kumarapala → Ajayapala → Mularaja II**

---

# 2. Mularaja II — c. 1175–1178 CE

After Ajayapala, **Mularaja II** became ruler.

He was still a **minor/child ruler**.

Because of his young age, his mother:

> **Queen Naikidevi**

played an important role as regent.

This is the most important point of the chapter.

> **Mularaja II = child ruler**  
> **Naikidevi = regent**

---

# 3. Who was Queen Naikidevi?

Naikidevi was the mother of Mularaja II.

She is remembered because Gujarat faced a major invasion during her son's minority.

Her name is especially associated with the defeat of:

> **Muhammad of Ghor / Muhammad Ghori**

in **1178 CE**.

This is one of the most famous episodes in medieval Gujarat history.

---

# 4. Muhammad Ghori's Gujarat Invasion — 1178 CE

Muhammad of Ghor had begun expanding into northwestern India.

In 1178, he advanced toward Gujarat.

At that time:

> **Mularaja II was the Chaulukya ruler, but he was a child.**

The Chaulukya forces confronted the invading army in the region near **Mount Abu**.

The battle is generally associated with:

> **Kasahrada / Kayadara**

The invading Ghurid army was defeated and forced to retreat.

### Core exam line

> **1178 CE → Muhammad Ghori invaded Gujarat → defeated by Chaulukya forces during the reign of Mularaja II, under the regency of Queen Naikidevi.**

---

# 5. Battle of Kasahrada / Kayadara

The battlefield is referred to in sources by names such as:

- **Kasahrada**
    
- **Kayadara**
    
- sometimes associated with the Mount Abu region
    

Different sources vary in the exact place-name.

For exam purposes, remember:

> **Battle of Kasahrada/Kayadara — 1178 CE**

---

# 6. Did Naikidevi personally fight Muhammad Ghori?

Popular retellings often describe Naikidevi as personally leading the army into battle.

The safer historical wording is:

> **Naikidevi acted as regent during the invasion, and Chaulukya forces defeated Muhammad Ghori.**

Some later traditions give her a more direct battlefield role, but the exact nature of her personal command is less certain.

So for GPSC:

> **Naikidevi = regent + resistance to Ghori**

is enough.

---

# 7. Why was this defeat important?

Muhammad Ghori's defeat in Gujarat was significant because:

- it stopped his immediate expansion into Gujarat
    
- he had to retreat toward the northwest
    
- he later changed the direction of his Indian campaigns
    
- Gujarat remained outside Ghurid control at that time
    

This was not the end of Ghori's ambitions in India.

Later he turned toward the Punjab and the Gangetic plains.

That eventually led to:

> **Battles of Tarain — 1191 and 1192**

So you can remember the sequence:

```text
1178
Ghori defeated in Gujarat
↓
later shifts focus northward
↓
1191
First Battle of Tarain
↓
1192
Second Battle of Tarain
```

---

# 8. Important historical distinction

Do not confuse:

> **Muhammad Ghori's Gujarat invasion — 1178**

with:

> **Mahmud of Ghazni's Somnath raid — 1025/26**

These are two different invaders and two different Solanki rulers.

### Comparison

|Invader|Date|Gujarat ruler|
|---|--:|---|
|**Mahmud of Ghazni**|1025–26|Bhima I|
|**Muhammad Ghori**|1178|Mularaja II / Regent Naikidevi|

This is a very common exam trap.

---

# 9. Death of Mularaja II

Mularaja II's reign was very short.

He died around:

> **1178 CE**

He was succeeded by:

> **Bhima II**

Bhima II then ruled for a very long period, though during his reign the real power of the Chaulukya kings gradually weakened.

---

# 10. Political significance of this phase

The sequence is:

> **Kumarapala = high point**  
> **Ajayapala = short reign**  
> **Mularaja II = child ruler**  
> **Naikidevi = regency**  
> **Ghori defeated**  
> **Bhima II = long but increasingly weak rule**

So even though the dynasty was entering decline, it was still capable of defeating a major external invasion in 1178.

# 12. Common GPSC Traps

### Trap 1

**Who defeated Muhammad Ghori in Gujarat in 1178?**

Best answer:

> **The Chaulukya forces during the reign of Mularaja II, under the regency of Queen Naikidevi.**

---

### Trap 2

**Was Mularaja II an adult king?**

No.

> He was a **minor**.

---

### Trap 3

**Mahmud Ghazni = Muhammad Ghori?**

No.

> **Mahmud Ghazni → 1025/26 → Bhima I**  
> **Muhammad Ghori → 1178 → Mularaja II**
