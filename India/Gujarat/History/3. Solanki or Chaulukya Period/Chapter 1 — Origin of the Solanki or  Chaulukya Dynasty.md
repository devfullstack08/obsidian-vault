

The **Chaulukyas of Gujarat**, commonly called the **Solankis**, rose to power in Gujarat around the **mid-10th century CE**.

Their first important ruler and founder was:

> **Mularaja I — c. 940–995 CE**

The dynasty ruled mainly from:

> **Anahilapataka / Anahilavada**, present-day **Patan**

### 1. Background before the Solankis

Before the Chaulukyas became powerful, different regional powers controlled parts of Gujarat.

By the 10th century, political conditions allowed a new dynasty to establish itself in north Gujarat.

Mularaja emerged as the founder of this new ruling house.

### 2. Who was Mularaja I?

Mularaja I is generally regarded as the **founder of the Gujarat Chaulukya dynasty**.

He established control over **Anahilapataka**, which became the political centre of the dynasty.

A simple memory line is:

> **Mularaja = Founder + Patan**

### 3. Why Patan was important

Anahilapataka, later known as **Anahilavada Patan**, became one of the most important cities of medieval Gujarat.

It developed as a centre of:

- administration
    
- trade
    
- religion
    
- architecture
    
- education
    
- royal patronage
    

Later Solanki rulers continued to rule from this capital.

So remember:

> **Maitraka → Vallabhi**  
> **Solanki/Chaulukya → Anahilavada Patan**

### 4. Expansion under Mularaja

Mularaja did not inherit a large empire.

His importance lies mainly in establishing a stable territorial base.

Over time, Chaulukya power expanded into areas of:

- north Gujarat
    
- Saurashtra
    
- parts of Rajasthan-facing regions
    

Later rulers such as **Bhima I, Siddharaja Jayasimha and Kumarapala** expanded the kingdom much further.

So Mularaja is best understood as:

> **the foundation-builder rather than the ruler of the dynasty’s maximum expansion.**

### 5. Religion and patronage

The early Chaulukyas participated in the broader religious culture of western India.

Over the course of the dynasty, rulers patronized:

- Shaivism
    
- Jainism
    
- temple construction
    
- Sanskrit scholarship
    

Jain influence became especially significant under later rulers, particularly **Kumarapala** and through the influence of **Acharya Hemachandra**.

### 6. Architectural importance

The Solanki period later became famous for what is often called the **Maru-Gurjara style** of architecture.

Some of Gujarat’s most celebrated temples and stepwells belong to this broader Chaulukya age.

For example, **Rani-ki-Vav at Patan** was built in the 11th century as a memorial to a king and reflects the highly developed architectural culture of the Solanki period. 

Do not connect Rani-ki-Vav directly with Mularaja, though—it belongs to a later generation.

### 7. Importance of Mularaja in Gujarat history

Mularaja matters because he:

- founded the Gujarat Chaulukya/Solanki line
    
- established Anahilavada Patan as the dynastic centre
    
- laid the political foundation for one of Gujarat’s strongest medieval kingdoms
    
- began a dynasty that later produced rulers such as Bhima I, Siddharaja Jayasimha and Kumarapala
    

### 8. Exam-ready summary

> **Founder:** Mularaja I  
> **Approx. reign:** c. 940–995 CE  
> **Dynasty:** Chaulukya / Solanki of Gujarat  
> **Capital:** Anahilapataka / Anahilavada Patan  
> **Importance:** Established the dynasty and laid the foundation for later Solanki expansion
> **Mularaja planted the root; later rulers built the empire.**
