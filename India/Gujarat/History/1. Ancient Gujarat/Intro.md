
The earliest major civilization in Gujarat was the **Indus Valley Civilization**.

Important Harappan sites in Gujarat include:

- **Dholavira** — Kutch
- **Lothal** — near Ahmedabad
- **Rangpur**
- **Surkotada**
- **Rojdi**

**Lothal** is especially famous for its dockyard and maritime trade.

**Dholavira** is important for its advanced water-management system and urban planning.

Later, Gujarat became part of several major kingdoms and empires, including the **Mauryan Empire**.

Emperor **Ashoka** left inscriptions at **Girnar near Junagadh**.

After the Mauryas, Gujarat came under different rulers including the **Western Kshatrapas**.

One important ruler was **Rudradaman I**, whose famous **Junagadh inscription** records the repair of Sudarshana Lake.
