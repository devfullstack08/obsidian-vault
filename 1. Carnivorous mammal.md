A **carnivorous mammal** is a **mammal that eats mainly meat or other animals**.

- **Carnivorous** = meat-eating / માંસાહારી
- **Mammal** = an animal that feeds its young with milk / સ્તનધારી પ્રાણી
**Lion, tiger, leopard, wolf** are carnivorous mammals.
