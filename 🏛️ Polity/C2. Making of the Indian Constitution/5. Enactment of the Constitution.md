The Constitution of India was **adopted** on:

> **26 November 1949**

The Preamble begins:

> “WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC…”

26 November is observed as:

> **Constitution Day / Samvidhan Divas** (since 2015)

## Signing

The Constitution was signed on:

> **24 January 1950**

**284** members signed it.

It was signed in:

- Hindi;
- English.

| Work | Person |
| ---- | ------ |
| English calligraphy | Prem Behari Narain Raizada |
| Hindi calligraphy | Vasant Krishan Vaidya |
| Artistic decoration | Nandalal Bose and Shantiniketan artists |

## Provisions that came into force on 26 November 1949

Some provisions commenced immediately on the date of adoption, not on 26 January 1950.

They related to:

- citizenship;
- elections;
- provisional Parliament;
- temporary and transitional provisions;
- short title;
- commencement;
- repeal.

Articles commonly listed:

> 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 60, 324, 366, 367, 379, 380, 388, 391, 392, 393

> [!exam-note]
> Adopted = **26 November 1949**. Signed = **24 January 1950**. Came into force = **26 January 1950**.
