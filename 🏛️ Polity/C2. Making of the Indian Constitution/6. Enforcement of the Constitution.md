The Constitution came into force on:

> **26 January 1950**

India became a:

> **Sovereign Democratic Republic**

Dr Rajendra Prasad became the first President of India.

The date honours:

- the Lahore Session of Congress, 1929;
- **Purna Swaraj Day — 26 January 1930**.

## Final session — 24 January 1950

On this day the Assembly:

- signed the Constitution;
- adopted **Jana Gana Mana** as the National Anthem;
- recognised **Vande Mataram** as the National Song;
- elected Dr Rajendra Prasad as the first President;
- adjourned.

## Provisional Parliament

After 26 January 1950, the Constituent Assembly functioned as the **Provisional Parliament** until the first general elections of **1951–52**.

The first elected Parliament met in **1952**.
