## Time and cost

The Constituent Assembly took:

> **2 years, 11 months and 18 days**

It held:

- **11 sessions**;
- about **165 days** of sitting;
- about **114 days** on the Draft Constitution.

The approximate cost of constitution-making was:

> **₹63.96 lakh**

## Original Constitution (1950)

As originally adopted, the Constitution contained:

- **395 Articles**;
- **22 Parts**;
- **8 Schedules**.

Later amendments added Articles, Parts and Schedules. Exam questions on “original Constitution” mean this 1950 figure, not the present one.

## Quotations

**Nehru** — the Constitution of free India must be framed, without outside interference, by a Constituent Assembly elected on adult franchise. (This was the ideal. The actual Assembly was indirectly elected.)

**Ambedkar** — political democracy must become social democracy; liberty, equality and fraternity must go together.

**Rajendra Prasad** — the success of the Constitution depends on the people who work it, especially the administration.

**Granville Austin** — the Constituent Assembly was “India in microcosm.”

## Exam traps

| Trap | Correct point |
| ---- | ------------- |
| Directly elected by adult franchise | Indirectly elected by provincial assemblies; princely members were nominated |
| All members were elected | Princely-state representatives were nominated |
| Adopted on 26 January 1950 | Adopted **26 November 1949**; commenced **26 January 1950** |
| Signed on 26 November 1949 | Signed on **24 January 1950** |
| Ambedkar wrote the whole Constitution | He chaired the Drafting Committee; the Assembly as a whole made it |
| Sovereign from 9 December 1946 | Sovereign from **15 August 1947** |
| Created by the Indian Independence Act | Created by the **Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946** |
| 395 Articles is the present strength | That is the **original** figure |
| Nehru Report was written by Jawaharlal Nehru | Chairman was **Motilal Nehru** (1928). Jawaharlal was the secretary, and later (1938) he demanded a Constituent Assembly |
| Swaraj Bill 1895 is the first demand for a Constituent Assembly | 1895 is the first Indian **draft**. The first clear demand for a **Constituent Assembly** is **M. N. Roy, 1934** |
| Tilak certainly wrote the Swaraj Bill | The author is **not known**. It is only linked with Tilak |

## Mains frames

1. **Was the Constituent Assembly representative?**  
   Yes in a broad sense — provinces, communities, princely states — but not elected by universal adult franchise.

2. **Role of Ambedkar**  
   Chairman of the Drafting Committee; defender of the draft; not the sole author.

3. **Importance of the Objectives Resolution**  
   Philosophical foundation of the Constitution and the basis of the Preamble.

4. **Was the Constituent Assembly sovereign?**  
   Not at the first meeting; yes after the Indian Independence Act, from 15 August 1947.
