The Indian Independence Act was passed by the British Parliament on **18 July 1947**.

It came into effect on **15 August 1947**.

## Main Provisions

- British India was divided into two independent dominions:
  1. India
  2. Pakistan
- British rule in India ended.
- The office of the Viceroy was abolished.
- Each dominion was to have a Governor-General.
- The Governor-General was appointed by the British monarch on the advice of the Dominion Cabinet.
- The office of the Secretary of State for India was abolished.
- The Constituent Assemblies of India and Pakistan became sovereign legislative bodies.
- British control over the princely states ended.
- Princely states were free to join India or Pakistan.
- The British monarch could no longer veto Indian laws.
- The title “Emperor of India” was removed from the British Crown.
- Civil servants appointed before independence continued to receive their existing benefits.

## Importance

- It ended British rule in India.
- It made India an independent and sovereign country.
- It gave full legislative authority to the Constituent Assembly.
- It marked the beginning of constitutional government by Indians.
