# Crown Rule: 1858–1947

After the Revolt of 1857, the British government ended the rule of the East India Company and took direct control over India.

---

## Government of India Act of 1858

### Background

The Revolt of 1857 exposed the weaknesses of Company rule. As a result, the British Parliament passed the Government of India Act of 1858.

### Main Provisions

- The rule of the East India Company was abolished.
- The administration of India was transferred to the British Crown.
- The Governor-General of India was also designated as the **Viceroy of India**.
- Lord Canning became the first Viceroy of India.
- The Board of Control and Court of Directors were abolished.
- The office of the **Secretary of State for India** was created.
- The Secretary of State was assisted by a Council of India consisting of fifteen members.
- The Secretary of State was a member of the British Cabinet and was responsible to the British Parliament.

### Importance

- It ended Company rule in India.
- It established direct rule by the British Crown.
- It created a highly centralised administrative system.
