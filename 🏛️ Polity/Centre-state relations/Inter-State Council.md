# Inter-State Council

The Inter-State Council is a non-permanent constitutional body established to facilitate coordination and resolve disputes between the Union and States, or among the States.

---

## Constitutional Basis (Article 263)

* **Article 263** states that if the President feels that the public interest would be served by the establishment of an Inter-State Council, the **President may, by order, establish such a Council**.
* The President also defines the nature of the duties to be performed by the Council, its organization, and its procedure.

---

## Origin and Sarkaria Commission

* Although the provision existed since 1950, no permanent Council was formed for decades.
* **Sarkaria Commission (1983–1988):** Highly recommended the establishment of a permanent Inter-State Council under Article 263 to strengthen cooperative federalism.
* **Establishment:** The Government of India accepted this recommendation and established the Council through a **Presidential Order dated May 28, 1990** (under Article 263).

---

## Composition of the Inter-State Council

The Council consists of:
* **Chairman:** The Prime Minister of India.
* **Members:**
  * Chief Ministers of all States.
  * Chief Ministers of Union Territories having a Legislative Assembly (Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu & Kashmir).
  * Administrators of UTs not having a Legislative Assembly.
  * Governors of States currently under President's Rule (Article 356).
  * Six Union Cabinet Ministers (including the Union Home Minister) nominated by the Prime Minister.

---

## Duties & Functions

Under Article 263, the Council is charged with:
1. **Inquiring into and advising** upon disputes which may have arisen between States.
2. **Investigating and discussing** subjects in which some or all of the States, or the Union and one or more of the States, have a common interest.
3. **Making recommendations** upon any such subject, particularly for the better coordination of policy and action on that subject.

*Note: The Council’s function is advisory in nature. Its recommendations are not binding.*
