It may:

- add new provisions;
- remove old provisions;
- replace or correct certain words or sections;
- change punishments, procedures, or definitions; or
- update a law according to current needs.

### Example

The **Information Technology Act, 2000** was later changed by the **Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008**.

The 2008 Amendment Act updated the original IT Act by adding or changing provisions related to cybercrime, electronic records, data protection, and online impersonation.

So:

- **Principal Act:** the original law
- **Amendment Act:** a later law that modifies the original law
- **Amended Act:** the original law after those changes are included

In simple words:

> An Amendment Act updates or modifies an existing Act; it does not always create a completely separate legal system.
