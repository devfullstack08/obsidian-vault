# Article 393: Short Title

Article 393 is the first article of **Part XXII** of the Constitution of India.

---

## Constitution Text

> **393. Short title** — This Constitution may be called the **Constitution of India**.

---

## Detailed Analysis & Key Points

### 1. Placement in the Constitution
* Unlike modern acts where the "Short Title" is usually placed in **Section 1** (at the very beginning), the framers of the Indian Constitution placed it at the very end in **Part XXII (Articles 393 to 395)**.
* This follows the older British legislative drafting tradition where the title and repeals were positioned at the end of the document.

### 2. Legal Significance
* This article officially names the document. 
* Any legal, official, or constitutional reference to this document must be made using the name **"Constitution of India"**.

### 3. Part XXII Overview
Article 393 belongs to Part XXII, which consists of the following articles:
* **Article 393:** Short Title.
* **Article 394:** Commencement (defines when the Constitution came into force).
* **Article 394A:** Authoritative text in the Hindi language.
* **Article 395:** Repeals (repealed the Indian Independence Act 1947 and the Government of India Act 1935).
