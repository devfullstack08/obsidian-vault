# Part XIV: Services under the Union and the States

Part XIV of the Indian Constitution contains the legal framework for the Civil Services and Public Service Commissions in India.

---

## Key Structure of Part XIV

Part XIV is divided into two chapters:
1. **Chapter I: Services** (Articles 308 to 314)
2. **Chapter II: Public Service Commissions** (Articles 315 to 323)

---

## Chapter I: Services

* **Article 309:** Recruitment and conditions of service of persons serving the Union or a State.
* **Article 310 (Doctrine of Pleasure):** Every member of defense, civil service of the Union, or all-India service holds office during the **pleasure of the President**, and state civil servants hold office during the **pleasure of the Governor**.
* **Article 311 (Constitutional Safeguards for Civil Servants):**
  * Protects civil servants from arbitrary dismissal, removal, or reduction in rank.
  * No civil servant can be dismissed by an authority subordinate to that which appointed them.
  * Provides the right to a reasonable opportunity of being heard (inquiry).
* **Article 312 (All-India Services):**
  * Gives the Parliament the power to create new **All-India Services** common to the Union and the States.
  * **Pivot Role of Rajya Sabha:** A new All-India Service can only be created if the **Rajya Sabha** passes a resolution supported by not less than **two-thirds** of the members present and voting, declaring it necessary in the national interest.
  * Currently, there are three All-India Services:
    1. Indian Administrative Service (IAS)
    2. Indian Police Service (IPS)
    3. Indian Forest Service (IFS) - *created in 1966*

---

## Chapter II: Public Service Commissions

* **Article 315:** Establishment of a Public Service Commission for the Union (UPSC) and for each State (SPSC), or a Joint State Public Service Commission (JSPSC).
* **Article 316:** Appointment and term of office of members.
  * UPSC members hold office for **6 years or until age 65**.
  * SPSC members hold office for **6 years or until age 62**.
* **Article 320:** Functions of Public Service Commissions (conducting examinations, advising government on recruitment and disciplinary matters).
