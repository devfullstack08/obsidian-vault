### 2. Amy Bradley Is Missing
#### Caribbean Sea — 24 March 1998

- **Amy Lynn Bradley** was a 23-year-old American woman from Virginia, United States.

- She disappeared during a family cruise aboard Royal Caribbean’s ship:
  - **Rhapsody of the Seas**

- The cruise began in:
  - **San Juan, Puerto Rico**
  - on 21 March 1998

- The ship then travelled to Aruba.

- On 23 March, it departed Aruba and sailed toward:
  - **Curaçao, Netherlands Antilles**

- Amy disappeared during the early morning of **24 March 1998**, while the ship was approaching Curaçao. 
#### Before her disappearance

- Amy was travelling with:
  - Her father, Ron Bradley
  - Her mother, Iva Bradley
  - Her brother, Brad Bradley

- On the night of 23 March, Amy and Brad attended a party and danced at the ship’s nightclub.

- Amy was seen dancing with **Alister “Yellow” Douglas**, a musician in the ship’s band.

- Amy and her brother later returned to the family cabin.

- According to the family, Amy’s father saw her resting or sleeping on a chair on the cabin balcony at approximately **5:15–5:30 a.m.**

- At around **6:00 a.m.**, he discovered that she was missing.

- Her cigarettes and lighter were also reportedly gone.

#### Search aboard the ship

- Amy’s family immediately began searching for her.

- They asked the cruise staff to prevent passengers from leaving the ship until Amy was found.

- However, passengers were permitted to disembark after the ship reached Curaçao.

- A search of the ship and surrounding waters failed to find her.

- The Netherlands Antilles Coast Guard conducted an aerial and maritime search, but no confirmed trace of Amy was discovered.

#### Main theories

##### Theory 1: Accidental fall overboard

- One possibility is that Amy accidentally fell from the ship.

- However:
  - She was a trained lifeguard and a strong swimmer.
  - Her family believed she was afraid of open water.
  - No body or confirmed evidence of a fall was recovered.

- An accidental fall remains possible but has never been proven.

##### Theory 2: Suicide

- Some authorities initially considered whether Amy had deliberately gone overboard.

- Her family strongly rejected this theory.

- No confirmed evidence has established that she died by suicide.

##### Theory 3: Abduction or human trafficking

- Amy’s family believes she may have been abducted from the ship and taken ashore.

- This theory is partly based on:
  - Alleged sightings after her disappearance
  - Claims that some crew members paid unusual attention to her
  - The delay in restricting passengers from leaving the ship

- No court or official investigation has proven that she was trafficked.

#### Alister “Yellow” Douglas

- Alister Douglas was a musician who was seen dancing with Amy before she disappeared.

- Some members of Amy’s family considered his behaviour suspicious.

- He was questioned by investigators.

- He denied involvement, and no evidence has established that he was responsible for Amy’s disappearance.

- Therefore, he should not be described as her kidnapper or killer.

#### Reported sightings

Several people later claimed to have seen Amy, but none of these sightings has been conclusively verified.

##### Curaçao taxi-driver report

- A taxi driver claimed that a young woman resembling Amy approached him near the port and asked to use a telephone.

- The report was not independently confirmed.

##### Canadian tourist’s report

- A Canadian tourist later claimed to have seen a distressed woman resembling Amy on a beach in Curaçao.

- He said the woman appeared to be accompanied by two men.

- This sighting was never conclusively verified.

##### U.S. Navy serviceman’s account

- A former U.S. Navy serviceman stated that he encountered a woman in a Curaçao brothel who identified herself as Amy Bradley and said she was being held against her will.

- He did not report the incident until years later.

- Authorities could not conclusively confirm the account.

##### Photograph of a woman called “Jas”

- In 2005, Amy’s family received photographs from an adult-services website showing a woman using the name “Jas.”

- The woman resembled Amy, and some facial experts reportedly considered a connection possible.

- However, the woman’s identity was never conclusively established as Amy.

#### FBI investigation

- The FBI continues to list Amy Lynn Bradley as missing.

- She has several distinguishing tattoos, including:
  - A Tasmanian Devil spinning a basketball
  - A sun tattoo on her lower back
  - A Chinese symbol on her ankle
  - A gecko tattoo near her navel

- The FBI has published age-progressed images to show what Amy might look like today. 
#### Current status

- Amy Bradley has never been found.

- No body has been recovered.

- No person has been charged with causing her disappearance.

- Reported sightings and theories involving an accident, suicide, abduction or trafficking remain unconfirmed.

> The disappearance of Amy Lynn Bradley remains an open and unresolved missing-person case.


### 3. Lover, Stalker, Killer
### Case: Cari Farver / Liz Golyar / Dave Kroupa

#### Main People

- **Dave Kroupa**
  - Auto mechanic / auto-shop manager in Omaha, Nebraska.
  - Had previously been in a long-term relationship with **Amy Flora**.
  - Dave and Amy had **two children together**.
  - After their breakup, Dave began using online dating.

- **Amy Flora**
  - Dave Kroupa’s former long-term partner.
  - Mother of his two children.
  - Later became a target of harassment and impersonation connected to the case.

- **Shanna “Liz” Golyar**
  - Met Dave through an online dating site in 2012.
  - Their relationship was casual/on-and-off.
  - Later convicted of murdering Cari Farver and committing arson.

- **Cari Farver**
  - Met Dave in late 2012.
  - Began dating him shortly afterward.
  - Disappeared on **13 November 2012**.
  - Her body has never been found.

---

#### Timeline

#### 2012 — Dave meets Liz

Dave Kroupa met **Liz Golyar** through an online dating site.

Dave told Liz that he did not want a serious or exclusive relationship and that he was dating other women.

Later, Dave met **Cari Farver** near his workplace and started dating her.

On one of Dave and Cari’s early dates, Liz appeared at Dave’s apartment building while Cari was there.

Cari and Liz briefly crossed paths.

---

#### 13 November 2012 — Cari disappears

Cari had spent the previous night at Dave’s apartment.

Dave left for work early in the morning.

Cari was still at the apartment when he left.

Later that day, Dave began receiving unusual messages apparently from Cari.

Cari’s mother also received messages suggesting that Cari was leaving and did not want people to look for her.

However:

- Cari suddenly stopped going to work.
- She stopped having normal contact with her family.
- Her behaviour in the messages did not match how her family knew her.
- She was never physically seen again.

This eventually raised suspicion that **someone else was using Cari’s phone/accounts**.

---

#### Harassment begins

After Cari disappeared, Dave and Liz began receiving large numbers of:

- text messages
- emails
- phone calls
- threats
- social-media messages

The messages appeared to come from **Cari**.

Many were extremely jealous or aggressive.

Dave initially believed Cari had become obsessed with him.

Liz also presented herself as a victim of Cari’s harassment.

The harassment continued for years.

---

#### Property damage and vandalism

Various acts of vandalism were also blamed on “Cari.”

Messages such as **“whore”** appeared during some incidents.

After certain acts of vandalism, Dave or Liz would receive messages from someone claiming to be Cari and taking responsibility.

This strengthened the false impression that Cari was alive and stalking them.

---

#### 17 August 2013 — Liz’s house fire

Liz’s rented house caught fire.

The fire was determined to have been deliberately set.

Liz and her children were not inside, but her pets died.

Dave and Liz received messages supposedly from Cari claiming responsibility for the fire.

This further reinforced the belief that Cari was still alive and dangerous.

Later, investigators concluded that **Liz herself had set the fire**.

Liz was ultimately convicted of **second-degree arson** for this incident. 

---

#### Amy Flora becomes a target

Dave’s former partner **Amy Flora** also began receiving threatening and harassing messages supposedly from Cari.

Some messages suggested that Amy was being watched.

This made it appear as if Cari was targeting not only Dave and Liz, but also people close to Dave.

---

#### 2015 — Investigators begin to suspect Liz

By 2015, investigators began examining the digital evidence more closely.

A major breakthrough came from analysing devices, accounts, IP addresses, phone records, photographs and deleted data.

Investigators discovered that:

- Liz had access to accounts used to impersonate Cari.
- Some online activity supposedly belonging to Cari was connected to networks/devices associated with Liz.
- Liz’s phone contained a photograph of Cari’s Ford Explorer taken **after Cari had disappeared**.
- Liz’s fingerprints were found inside Cari’s vehicle.
- Cari’s vehicle had been unusually cleaned.
- Blood evidence was later discovered beneath the seat covering in Cari’s vehicle.

These findings began to undermine the idea that Liz was a victim. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

#### December 2015 — Liz reports Amy Flora

Liz told police that she now suspected **Amy Flora** was responsible for the harassment.

Police pretended to take this theory seriously.

In reality, investigators had begun to suspect Liz.

They allowed Liz to continue communicating with them while they gathered more evidence.

This was a major investigative strategy.

---

#### 5 December 2015 — Shooting incident

Liz was found with a gunshot wound at a park.

She claimed she had been attacked.

Around this time, a firearm belonging to Dave was missing.

Investigators became suspicious that Liz may have **shot herself in order to frame Amy Flora** and strengthen the false stalking story.

Police deliberately told Liz that they suspected Amy, hoping Liz would continue creating evidence.

Soon afterward, messages supposedly from “Amy” began appearing and included alleged confessions to:

- killing Cari
- setting Liz’s house on fire
- shooting Liz

Those messages contained details about Cari’s disappearance that investigators believed only the real killer would know. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

#### Digital impersonation

One of the most important features of this case was **digital identity impersonation**.

Liz created or controlled multiple:

- email accounts
- social-media identities
- phone identities
- fake personas

She impersonated Cari for years after Cari was already dead.

The purpose appears to have been to:

1. Make police and family believe Cari was still alive.
2. Make Cari appear mentally unstable and dangerous.
3. Harass Dave and others.
4. Present Liz herself as another victim.
5. Redirect suspicion toward other women.

This is why the case is especially important in discussions of **cyberstalking and digital forensics**.

---

#### Murder of Cari Farver

The prosecution argued that Liz killed Cari shortly after Cari began dating Dave.

Cari disappeared on **13 November 2012**.

Her body was never recovered.

Evidence presented at trial suggested that Cari was killed and that Liz then took over her digital identity.

Some of the fake messages later sent under other identities contained descriptions suggesting Cari had been stabbed in her vehicle and that efforts were made to dispose of her remains.

Even without recovering Cari’s body, prosecutors were able to build a strong circumstantial and digital-evidence case. 

---

#### Conviction

Liz Golyar was convicted in **2017** after a bench trial of:

- **First-degree murder** of Cari Farver
- **Second-degree arson**

She received:

- **Life imprisonment for murder**
- A consecutive **18–20 year sentence for arson**

The Nebraska Supreme Court later upheld the convictions.

---

#### Important Case Lessons

#### 1. A missing body does not always prevent a murder conviction

Cari’s body was never found.

However, prosecutors relied on:

- digital evidence
- phone records
- email records
- photographs
- fingerprints
- blood evidence
- impersonation evidence
- circumstantial evidence
- Liz’s own communications

to prove the case.

---

#### 2. Digital evidence can reveal identity

The killer attempted to create the illusion that Cari was alive for years.

However, investigators eventually linked online activity back to Liz through digital-forensic analysis.

---

#### 3. The offender created herself as a “victim”

Liz’s strategy was unusual because she did not merely stalk Dave.

She created a fictional enemy — “Cari” — and then portrayed herself as one of Cari’s victims.

This helped keep Dave emotionally connected to her.

---

#### 4. Framing other people

When suspicion increased, Liz attempted to redirect attention toward Amy Flora.

Investigators exploited this by pretending to believe her story while continuing to collect evidence against her.

---

#### 5. Psychological manipulation

The case involved:

- jealousy
- obsession
- deception
- impersonation
- stalking
- manipulation
- victim-playing
- controlling relationships

However, psychological motives should be distinguished from clinically diagnosing Liz without a formal assessment.

### Girl in the Picture / Suzanne Marie Sevakis / Franklin Delano Floyd Case

#### Basic Case

- **Victim's real name:** Suzanne Marie Sevakis
- **Born:** 6 September 1969
- **Died:** April 1990
- **Place of death:** Oklahoma City area, Oklahoma, USA
- At the time of her death, she was known as **Tonya Hughes**.
- For more than 20 years, authorities did not know her true identity.
- Her real identity was finally confirmed in **2014**.

---

#### Suzanne's Different Identities

Suzanne lived under several names because Franklin Delano Floyd repeatedly changed identities.
 
Important names connected to Suzanne:

- **Suzanne Marie Sevakis** → real name
- **Sharon Marshall** → identity used while growing up
- **Tonya Dawn Tadlock**
- **Tonya Hughes** → name used around the time of her death

Franklin Floyd also used several aliases, including:

- Franklin Delano Floyd
- Warren Marshall
- Clarence Hughes / Clarence Marcus Hughes
- Brandon Cleo Williams

---
#### Biological Family

#### Father
**Clifford / Cliff Sevakis**

#### Mother
**Sandra "Sandi" Chipman**
Later known as Sandra Brandenburg / Sandra Willett.

Suzanne was born when her parents were very young.
Her parents later separated.

Sandra later had other children, including:

- Allison
- Amy
- Phillip Brandenburg

---

#### How Franklin Floyd Entered the Family

In the 1970s, Sandra met a man calling himself **Brandon Williams**.

His real identity was:

> **Franklin Delano Floyd**

Floyd married Sandra.

At that time, Floyd already had a serious criminal history and was a fugitive.

Sandra was later jailed for approximately 30 d ays over a bad-check offence.

When she was released:

> Floyd had disappeared with her children.

Sandra eventually recovered Allison and Amy.

However, she could not find:

- Suzanne
- Phillip

Suzanne remained with Floyd.

Floyd raised Suzanne for years while pretending to be her father.

---
#### Suzanne's Childhood

Floyd renamed Suzanne **Sharon Marshall**.

They moved through different states and used false identities.

Suzanne grew up believing Floyd was effectively her father.

According to investigators and people who knew her, Suzanne was highly intelligent.

She graduated from **Forest Park High School in Georgia**.

She reportedly received a scholarship opportunity connected with studying aerospace engineering.

However, her life changed dramatically because of Floyd's control over her.

---

#### Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Evidence and witness accounts indicate that Floyd sexually abused Suzanne.

Friends later reported that Suzanne told them Floyd had sexually assaulted her.

Floyd eventually took her to strip clubs and exploited her financially.

The relationship therefore changed from:

> alleged father/daughter

to

> abuser/victim

and eventually, extraordinarily:

> husband/wife under false identities.

---
#### Floyd Marries Suzanne

Floyd eventually married Suzanne even though he had raised her as his daughter.

They were using different identities at this stage.

He was known as **Clarence Hughes**.

Suzanne was known as **Tonya Hughes**.

This is one of the most disturbing features of the case:

> Floyd kidnapped Suzanne as a child, raised her as his daughter, sexually abused her, and later married her.

---
#### Suzanne's Children

Suzanne became pregnant several times.

There is some complexity in reconstructing her pregnancies, but the two most important known children are:

#### Michael Anthony Hughes

- Born in 1988.
- Raised for a period with Suzanne and Floyd.
- DNA testing later established that **Floyd was NOT Michael's biological father**.

#### Megan Dufresne

- Suzanne's daughter.
- Born in New Orleans.
- Placed for adoption.
- Years later, Megan's DNA became important in identifying Suzanne.

Do NOT write that Michael was adopted before papers were completed.

That sequence belongs to a later event involving his foster family.

---

#### April 1990 — Suzanne Is Found Injured

In April 1990, Suzanne was found critically injured near a roadway in the Oklahoma City area.

At the time she was known as **Tonya Hughes**.

She was taken to hospital around midnight.

Doctors found a serious **closed-head injury** and other trauma.

She died shortly afterward.

Initially, authorities believed she may have been the victim of a hit-and-run.

However, investigators found aspects of the incident suspicious.

Her death has never been conclusively solved.

---

#### Did Franklin Floyd Kill Suzanne?

This is important:

> **There has never been sufficient proof to convict Floyd of Suzanne's death.**

Floyd was considered a major person of interest.

Investigators strongly suspected him because of:

- his history of violence
- his control over Suzanne
- his sexual abuse of her
- inconsistent circumstances surrounding her death
- later crimes involving her son

But Floyd was **never convicted of murdering Suzanne**.

Therefore, for notes write:

> Suzanne's death remains officially unresolved/suspicious; Floyd was a person of interest but was never convicted of killing her.

---

#### What Happened to Michael After Suzanne Died?

After Suzanne's death, Michael entered state custody.

He was eventually placed with foster parents.

The foster family wanted to adopt him.

DNA testing showed:

> Franklin Floyd was NOT Michael's biological father.

This seriously weakened Floyd's claim to custody.

---

#### 1994 — Michael Hughes Kidnapping

On **12 September 1994**, Floyd went to Michael's elementary school in **Choctaw, Oklahoma**.

Michael was approximately six years old.

Floyd used a firearm and kidnapped Michael.

He also forced school principal **James Davis** to accompany them.

Later Floyd released/abandoned the principal and escaped with Michael.

Michael was never seen again.

Floyd was later arrested.

---

#### Floyd's Confession About Michael

Years later, FBI agents repeatedly interviewed Floyd.

Eventually, Floyd confessed that he had killed Michael.

According to Floyd:

- he shot Michael
- he buried/disposed of his body near the Oklahoma–Texas border

The FBI searched the area Floyd described.

However:

> Michael's remains were never recovered.

So Michael Anthony Hughes remains one of the tragic unresolved physical-evidence aspects of the case.

---

#### FBI Investigation

The FBI reopened and developed the case over many years.

One major problem remained:

> Who was "Sharon Marshall" / "Tonya Hughes" really?

Investigators knew she had lived under false identities.

But they did not know her birth name or biological family.

---

#### 2014 — Suzanne's True Identity Discovered

The breakthrough came through:

- genealogy
- DNA
- interviews
- records
- investigative journalism
- cooperation with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children

Investigators finally established that:

> **Sharon Marshall / Tonya Hughes was actually Suzanne Marie Sevakis.**

Her biological parents were identified as:

- **Cliff Sevakis**
- **Sandra Chipman**

This solved an identity mystery lasting more than two decades.

---

#### What Happened to Suzanne's Brother Phillip?

For decades, Phillip Brandenburg's fate was also unclear.

Eventually DNA testing showed that Phillip had survived.

He had been adopted and raised under another identity.

This was another major breakthrough in reconstructing Suzanne's childhood history.

---

#### Franklin Floyd's Earlier Criminal History

Floyd's crimes began long before Suzanne's death.

He had convictions involving:

- kidnapping
- sexual violence
- child abduction
- armed offences

In **1962**, he was convicted in connection with abducting and sexually assaulting a young girl.

He later became a federal fugitive.

This history became extremely important when investigators reconstructed Suzanne's life.

---
#### Cheryl Ann Commesso Murder

Floyd was also connected to another woman:

**Cheryl Ann Commesso**

She had worked at the same strip club environment connected with Suzanne.

Commesso disappeared in 1989.

Her remains were later discovered.

Photographs recovered from Floyd's possession became important evidence.

Floyd was ultimately convicted of **Commesso's murder** and sentenced to death in Florida.

This murder conviction is separate from Suzanne's death.

---
#### Important Distinction

Franklin Floyd was:

✅ convicted of serious crimes  
✅ convicted in Cheryl Ann Commesso's murder  
✅ convicted in Michael Hughes's kidnapping  
✅ admitted killing Michael

But:

❓ Suzanne Sevakis's death was never conclusively solved.

---

#### Why Suzanne's Case Was So Difficult

The case involved:

- repeated aliases
- identity theft
- constant movement between states
- child kidnapping
- family separation
- forged identities
- sexual abuse

- delayed investigation
- weak historical records
- deaths occurring under different names

Suzanne effectively disappeared twice:

1. **As a child**, when Floyd took her from her biological family.
2. **As an adult**, because she died under a false identity.

The eventual answer was:

> **Suzanne Marie Sevakis**

Finding her real name connected:

- her childhood kidnapping

#### Core Lessons

#### 1. Identity can become part of the crime

Floyd did not merely conceal himself.

He repeatedly changed Suzanne's identity too.

This made it extremely difficult for police to connect her with her biological family.

#### 2. A victim can remain unidentified for decades

Suzanne's death investigation could not be fully understood until investigators discovered who she actually was.

#### 3. DNA transformed the investigation

DNA helped connect Suzanne with her biological family and resolve decades-old identity questions.

#### 4. Criminal history matters

Once investigators reconstructed Floyd's earlier offences, Suzanne's apparently isolated story became part of a much larger pattern.

#### 5. Separate suspicion from proof

It is reasonable to say Floyd was suspected in Suzanne's death.

It is NOT correct to write:

> "Floyd murdered Suzanne"

as an established legal fact.

Better:

> "Floyd was a major suspect/person of interest in Suzanne's suspicious death, but her death remains unsolved."

---

#### One-Line Case Summary

> **Suzanne Marie Sevakis was abducted as a child by Franklin Delano Floyd, raised under false identities, subjected to years of abuse and exploitation, later married by her abductor, and died under suspicious circumstances in Oklahoma in 1990. Her true identity remained unknown until 2014.**
