### 1. The Disappearance of Lucie Blackman  
#### Tokyo, Japan

- Lucie Blackman was a **21-year-old British woman** and former British Airways flight attendant.
- She moved to Tokyo in May 2000 and worked as a hostess at the Casablanca nightclub in the Roppongi district.
- On **1 July 2000**, Lucie went out with a customer and disappeared.
- Her family travelled to Japan and launched a major public campaign to find her.
- Police later focused their investigation on **Joji Obara**, a wealthy Japanese businessman and property developer.
- During searches connected to Obara, investigators discovered hundreds of videotapes showing him sexually assaulting unconscious or heavily drugged women.
- The recordings and other evidence indicated that Obara had repeatedly drugged and sexually assaulted women over several years.
- Lucie’s dismembered remains were discovered on **9 February 2001** in a seaside cave near one of Obara’s properties.
- Because her remains were badly decomposed, investigators could not conclusively establish the exact cause of her death.

#### Charges against Joji Obara

- Obara was prosecuted in connection with:
  1. The abduction and death of Lucie Blackman
  2. The rape and death of Australian woman **Carita Ridgway**
  3. The rape of eight other women

#### 2007 District Court Judgment

- On **24 April 2007**, the Tokyo District Court convicted Obara of:
  - Raping eight women
  - Raping and causing the death of Carita Ridgway

- He was sentenced to **life imprisonment**.

- However, the court acquitted him of all charges relating to Lucie Blackman because it found insufficient direct forensic evidence proving that he had drugged, raped or caused her death.

#### Appeal

- Prosecutors appealed the acquittal relating to Lucie Blackman in 2007.

- The appeal trial began before the Tokyo High Court on **25 March 2008**.

- On **16 December 2008**, the Tokyo High Court found Obara guilty of:
  - Abducting Lucie Blackman
  - Dismembering her body
  - Illegally disposing of and abandoning her remains

- However, the court still did not convict him of causing Lucie’s death or raping her because the available evidence could not conclusively prove those charges.

- The High Court upheld his existing sentence of **life imprisonment**.

#### Final Outcome

- Joji Obara remained sentenced to life imprisonment for the rape of several women, the death of Carita Ridgway, and offences involving the abduction and disposal of Lucie Blackman’s body.

- The case became internationally known because of the seriousness of the crimes, the large number of suspected victims and criticism of the initial police investigation.
