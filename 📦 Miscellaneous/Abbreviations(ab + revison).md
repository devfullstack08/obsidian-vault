## 1. What is an Abbreviation?

An **abbreviation** is a shortened form of a word or phrase.

Examples:

- **Dr.** = Doctor
- **Mr.** = Mister
- **Govt.** = Government
- **Jan.** = January
- **etc.** = et cetera

### Memory

**Abbreviation = Short form**

---

# 2. Main Types of Abbreviations

## A. Initialism

A word formed from the **first letters** of several words, where we pronounce **each letter separately**.

Examples:

|Abbreviation|Full Form|
|---|---|
|BBC|British Broadcasting Corporation|
|ATM|Automated Teller Machine|
|RBI|Reserve Bank of India|
|USA|United States of America|
|UPSC|Union Public Service Commission|
|SSC|Staff Selection Commission|
|GST|Goods and Services Tax|
|FIR|First Information Report|

### Pronunciation

**BBC → B-B-C**

Not as one word.

---

## B. Acronym

An abbreviation made from initial letters but pronounced **as a word**.

Examples:

|Acronym|Full Form|
|---|---|
|NASA|National Aeronautics and Space Administration|
|UNESCO|United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization|
|NATO|North Atlantic Treaty Organization|
|ISRO|Indian Space Research Organisation|
|LASER|Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation|
|RADAR|Radio Detection and Ranging|

### Difference

**Initialism:** RBI → R-B-I  
**Acronym:** NASA → "nasa"

# 5. Time Abbreviations

## a.m.

**a.m. = ante meridiem**

Meaning: **before noon**

Example:

**8:00 a.m.**

---

## p.m.

**p.m. = post meridiem**

Meaning: **after noon**

Example:

**7:00 p.m.**

### Memory

**AM → Before midday**  
**PM → Past midday**

---

# 6. Common Latin Abbreviations

These are very important in English examinations.

## e.g.

**e.g. = exempli gratia**

Meaning: **for example**

Example:

You should eat healthy fruits, **e.g., apples and oranges**.

---

## i.e.

**i.e. = id est**

Meaning: **that is / in other words**

Example:

He works in the capital of India, **i.e., New Delhi**.

### Important Difference

**e.g. → gives examples**

**i.e. → explains exactly what you mean**

Example:

I like tropical fruits, **e.g., mangoes and bananas**.

I live in the capital of Gujarat, **i.e., Gandhinagar**.

---

## etc.

**etc. = et cetera**

Meaning:

**and other similar things**

Example:

I bought pens, pencils, notebooks, **etc.**

### Common Error

❌ and etc.

✅ etc.

Because **et cetera itself means "and the rest."**

---

## viz.

**viz. = videlicet**

Meaning:

**namely / specifically**

Example:

There are three primary colours, **viz., red, blue and yellow.**

---

## et al.

**et al. = et alii / et alia**

Meaning:

**and others**

Mostly used with names of authors.

Example:

The study was conducted by Sharma **et al.**

# 14. Grammar Rules for Abbreviations

### Rule 1: Capital Letters

Most organization names use capitals.

✅ RBI  
✅ SSC  
✅ NASA

---

### Rule 2: Full Stops

Some traditional abbreviations use periods:

**Mr., Dr., Prof., etc.**

But modern style often omits periods in acronyms:

✅ NASA  
✅ RBI  
✅ UNESCO

Not:

❌ N.A.S.A.

---

### Rule 3: Articles — A or An

Use **a/an according to pronunciation**, not spelling.

### Examples

**an RBI officer**

Why?

R = pronounced **"ar"**, beginning with a vowel sound.

✅ an RBI officer  
✅ an MLA  
✅ an FIR  
✅ an MBA graduate

But:

✅ a NASA scientist

Because NASA begins with the consonant sound **/n/** when spoken as a word.

✅ a UPSC aspirant

UPSC starts with **"you"**, a consonant /y/ sound.

---

# SSC Exam Trap

Choose the correct sentence:

A. He is a MBA graduate.  
B. He is an MBA graduate. ✅

Why?

**M = "em" → vowel sound**

Therefore:

**an MBA graduate**

# 15. Acronym vs Initialism — Must Remember

| Type               | Pronunciation             | Example |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | ------- |
| Acronym            | Spoken as one word        | NASA    |
| Initialism         | Letters spoken separately | RBI     |
| Shortening         | Part of word              | exam    |
| Title abbreviation | Shortened title           | Dr.     |

### One-line memory trick

**NASA = say the word → Acronym**

**RBI = say the letters → Initialism**

# SSC Quick Revision

**Abbreviation** → shortened form  
**Acronym** → initials pronounced as a word  
**Initialism** → initials pronounced separately  
**e.g.** → for example  
**i.e.** → that is  
**etc.** → and the rest  
**viz.** → namely  
**a.m.** → before noon  
**p.m.** → after noon

### Most important exam rule

> **A/An depends on SOUND, not the first written letter.**

**an FIR** ✅  
**an MLA** ✅  
**an MBA** ✅  
**a UPSC candidate** ✅
