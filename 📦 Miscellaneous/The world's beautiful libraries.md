### 1. The Long Room

![The Long Room of Trinity College Dublin, with its arched wooden ceiling and historic bookcases](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/IK79s0AjcdfxzZ5VcDUWM9cmN9LYicr91llV75R-bYzUW9GOzy7S9JPv5ALTvIHHWmFyYkir-k23bjR6GB43XWto15sMdu1VXToGWSNFp6uUtX2AEJf6votcZFyE2hie3KN1uGCm5haP6OpnmgkDzYiO45YuMp4TOI53GFYatJk?purpose=inline)

Trinity College · Dublin, Ireland
**Two surprising facts**
- The original room had a flat ceiling. In 1860, the roof was raised to create its famous barrel vault and make space for more books.
- Its marble bust collection began in 1743 with 14 commissioned sculptures of great thinkers and writers.

### 2. Admont Abbey Library

![The white-and-gold Baroque library hall at Admont Abbey](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Na30AVYfMzc56lEa9RwCaZDes031F4MfTdxkDp1kLfPi6Z3YBD3HxEGN6rb6e3QGavqvbGYMS1a4R-i7utSH-LD6nBf1njXDH2OrqB-OAgJI1Yvjffnk3tvp_jGxXzqFMMsNxq1LLgj1e5fFE6cUJvRAuphNJA4HH0QFJGQ4CLw?purpose=inline)

Admont, Austria
Two surprising facts
- At 70 metres long, it is the world's largest **monastic(મઠ સંબંધી)** library hall, with an interior holding around 60,000 books.
- ts seven ceiling frescoes were painted by **Bartolomeo Altomonte** when he was about 80 years old, depicting religion, art and science.

### 3.Biblioteca Joanina

![The gilded Baroque reading halls of Biblioteca Joanina in Coimbra](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/lPHQij71Kg0lH7FCFQkEAA1ue8XaJUVkR4aCfMPSJwdURSUPvbBD6eBe_JEnAZ57q03FnVZEyXr8sdNWZr45Ut154cMKp9gNqpzbNS3wIqMY5acl1wjbgs0MPmnY66Y4rKul8WHbFP95arW18hLe9CLbFHQpCnqn2xlcNrW-Uw4?purpose=inline)


University of Coimbra · Coimbra, Portugal

Two surprising facts
- Completed in 1728, the library honours **King John V** of Portugal, whose portrait dominates its grand interior.
- Its three lavish(ભવ્ય) halls feature black, red and green backgrounds, with gilded(સોને મઢેલું) carvings and collections of books dating from the 16th to 18th centuries.


## 4.Strahov Library

![The Philosophical Hall of Strahov Library in Prague](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/7Y78bzkRm2bC1tgsQm9wx8wrCSqhxlf5myDFx-MzW_lnq6XRjXzgfjLqq5Lr9vUzEL5L2Hc0xiX2dj6lQXPD8oLe1xeiiYo3LyaAPhnhkl12S2Liox634tcesY8o2KXWni41g8_bS8HlN9Xqi4ZPN2nR_Jt8tqwbpNgpanL0ljc?purpose=inline)

Strahov Monastery · Prague, Czechia
Two surprising facts
- The Philosophical Hall has hidden spiral staircases disguised behind false book spines, giving access to upper shelves.
- Its enormous ceiling fresco was painted by **Franz Anton Maulbertsch** with just one assistant in approximately six months in 1794.


### 5. George Peabody Library

![The multi-level cast-iron galleries of the George Peabody Library](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/RLkT8GCUGFsi4OVHxnSEAaGeQ9bjw6dqwsU2cMXbJ6niOMaztTQPxilSY9Sg0djBl_PQ13zZwFLqJ5d7R5o7kXa88aIMzjc_7TWjyTgKU8gRiSqBH4hW2Sn09-T31IM_qA2A9QTwoQZtHRd4b_jmdSIkLdQygh48nVHFNWSLkdM?purpose=inline)

Baltimore, Maryland, United States

Two surprising facts
- Five tiers of ornate cast-iron balconies rise beneath a skylight 61 feet above the library floor.
- Despite its palace-like appearance, the library is free to visit and contains about 300,000 volumes, mainly from the 19th century.

### 6. Austrian National Library · State Hall

![The grand State Hall of the Austrian National Library in Vienna](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/xOAxv_iBItvKiSsKjWhxRcD4RgquZT_VO7hyqECoKvoXHa-ZVMH1BlEZBi6tR--eHsDb8vuZ833CyS5RUNpcv6x_6f38sVBqH4bP1_HiFK4prXv546nBaOFcQ0sSIH4aTJG6wKDy85wrCaokSy8bFUD6_ujAlCmELu_D2D0I3ik?purpose=inline)

Hofburg Palace · Vienna, Austria

Two surprising facts

- The State Hall holds more than 200,000 historic books in 128 bookshelves and cabinets.
- Its spectacular dome fresco depicts **Emperor Charles VI** becoming godlike, and a marble statue of the emperor stands directly underneath.
