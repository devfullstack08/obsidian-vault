### 1. Nefertari (13th century BCE · Egypt)

![Rätsel um altägyptische Königin ein Stück weiter gelüftet | 1815.ch](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/JkCJjuowEQW4Me34qXWd2NPmv6ykmR36GY4YJ5SyFrunLMw-uQ7ZUQvM8_oSq2Fijrf-Z1yl0vqWRDENK7bpW4qMkzw4zCdqNKfYDLbPUp8srbVz0HsGMh9lRKsytXCxnKOs4e12_IoNdneS_OMYpX42ngr8BjWOQ5a6jFC5FHo?purpose=inline)

Queen of Ramesses II. Her vividly decorated tomb is among Egypt's finest.

Fact: Ramesses II dedicated a temple at Abu Simbel to Nefertari and the goddess Hathor. She is different from Nefertiti.

### 2. Nefertiti(14th century BCE · Ancient Egypt)

![The Enigmatic Nefertiti Bust | The Epoch Times](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/V7zXz8xrh30o3vYfHczEPj0cXW9wpF2F90YSxs0_ES2MM0xK0ZfsDJHU7Hpd_aQGIOZ73Og0YShQ9Z9aMAyjIuZCD53fYjgvYtuEXEAKlkxuArlYzg7wr0b3Hs5gdAHlJL0s8PKfJ7B0v8g8E7__nGWlps3s_vQ0xTLzZa82xOs?purpose=inline)

The wife of Pharaoh **Akhenaten**. Her painted bust(અર્ધ-પ્રતિમા), created around 1340 BCE, became one of history's most recognizable images of beauty.

Interesting fact: The bust was rediscovered in 1912 and is displayed in **Berlin**. Very little is certain about her later life.

### 3. Cleopatra VII(1st century BCE · Egypt)


![Tetradrachm - Cleopatra VII and Mark Antony (Antioch) - Antioch on the Orontes – Numista](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/CnuTg2lKFeZCmTcrffLwDm5yQzENNmBkin-SBJuwYgtHYpm7tkpuzORyEXxfq_IgicMtne0TXTjxxzw_e9nEcV5fLRqzEq-uVlYNqA8LnmwBohLtOmvOoD3wURvRSleyIRqPmPSLqXFBhzvuxZyVCaX2CGhQNyBNZJ7MKFIGzN0?purpose=inline)
