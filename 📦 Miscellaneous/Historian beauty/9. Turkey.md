### 1. Hürrem Sultan(c. 1505–1558 · Ottoman Empire)

Also known as Roxelana, she rose from enslavement to become the legal wife of Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent.

- Origin: From a region of present-day Ukraine.    
- Her marriage to Suleiman broke a longstanding Ottoman royal convention.
- She exercised considerable influence at court and supported charitable foundations.
- Her son Selim II succeeded Suleiman as Ottoman sultan in 1566.
