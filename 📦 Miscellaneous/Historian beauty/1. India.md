## 1. Nur Jahan(1577–1645 · Mughal India)

![Category:Nur Jahan - Wikimedia Commons](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/VrLHCbSnCu6ihqILtOsl1CuGdrp4bymIPWaIuNrzG2j8jj2srFx7UvuXgM2IcvwjxSVI6cNegyUzKsAnyz_l3doRQGfaxnUalbLoVdwqlv6PBqjpa_IVx2rmSslhYWUOEgYfH0xhWGC0K52dlY0x9Tg3mZ97Rf3XRKcPx39Ggek?purpose=inline)

Born **Mihr-un-Nissa**, she became Emperor **Jahangir's wife** in 1611 and one of the most influential women in Mughal history.

- Her title Nur Jahan means **Light of the World**.
- She was the only Mughal queen to **have coins** issued in her name.
- She commissioned the magnificent Tomb of **Itimad-ud-Daulah**  in Agra for her father.
- Family connection: She was the aunt of **Mumtaz Mahal**, for whom Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal.

### 2. Mumtaz Mahal(17th century CE · Mughal India)

The beloved wife of Emperor Shah Jahan, praised for her beauty and character in Mughal court traditions.

Interesting fact: Following her death in 1631, Shah Jahan commissioned the Taj Mahal as her mausoleum. She had also been his trusted companion.
