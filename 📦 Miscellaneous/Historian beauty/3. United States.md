### 3. Grace Kelly(1929–1982 · United States / Monaco)

![Grace Kelly: Jewelry Icon - Secrète Fine Jewelry](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/2YP2N1KCH__FjrWAHM9k9BjX4mM2cc6KvfPeht9hRrY2bPM06406aGR7dq7U5DIYLQJDksrIJ0eJxQW6KeGDpG8CElnp4rbhdUmb59PV1J0f-BziZpnEmwMpTmxP7NKsdIMryQRFxMPYLgUKVyKBhvKm560Y0icUgpQVqmuvxCo?purpose=inline)


A Hollywood actress celebrated for her elegant style who later became **Princess of Monaco**.

- Won the Academy Award for Best Actress for The Country Girl (1954).
- Starred in Hitchcock classics including Rear Window and To Catch a Thief.
- Married Prince Rainier III in 1956 and retired from acting.
- Her work as princess included charitable support for healthcare, education and the arts.
