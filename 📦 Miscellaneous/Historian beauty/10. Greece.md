### 1. Phryne(4th century BCE · Ancient Greece)
![These prostitutes left their mark in history](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/utp14d0HMTUKevy2K5orYiO6xmo_8oN-riYMSC_ydfn9J3G_P1-i6jAcitIy80bwwo-PEWSVLl6W_ua8_orGcbIyPYJwiugYgOiYMa4T-x9PL9NqdZt9_KklXJVl6x4GRx26UrCpkrfY44Gwn7trfMk1sOr23FnVKctmgyk0Ib0?purpose=inline)


Phryne was a famous hetaira (an elite courtesan) in ancient Athens, remembered for her beauty and association with prominent artists.
- Original name: Mnesarete.
- She was traditionally associated with the sculptor Praxiteles(The artist) and his representations of Aphrodite(The goddess).
- According to a famous legend, she was acquitted during a trial after her beauty was revealed to the judges.
- Historians question this courtroom story because the surviving accounts were written much later.
