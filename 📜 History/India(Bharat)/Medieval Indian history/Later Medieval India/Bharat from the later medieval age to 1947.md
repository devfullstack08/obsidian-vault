# Bharat from the later medieval age to 1947

Four maps. Same coastline as [[Bharat]]. Read them in order. Each one answers three questions: who came, who went, and what the previous map no longer shows.

Shades are classroom guides, not surveyed borders. Cream land is a power the shade does not cover.

## 1. About 1350 — one sultanate becomes several

![[bharat-c1350.png]]

Delhi has ruled in the north since **1206**. The five houses are Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi. Under the Tughlaqs the claim is very wide, and then it cracks.

| Came | Went |
| ---- | ---- |
| Bengal independent, about 1342, Ilyas Shah | The idea that one Delhi rules the whole north |
| Bahmani sultanate, 1347 | A single Deccan under Delhi |
| Vijayanagara, 1336, Harihara and Bukka | The old Chola south. They fight the Bahmanis until Talikota, 1565 |
| Kashmir stays outside Delhi | |

## 2. 1605 — Akbar has died. The south is not Mughal

![[bharat-c1605.png]]

**1526**, Babur, First Panipat, the Lodis end. Sher Shah Suri rules between Humayun's two reigns, **1540–1545**. Akbar, **1556–1605**, holds the north, Gujarat, Bengal, Kashmir, Sindh and Malwa. Most Rajput states are allied, not erased.

He does not hold Ahmadnagar, Bijapur or Golconda. Vijayanagara has already fallen at **Talikota, 1565**. The far south is Nayaka country. Aurangzeb's maximum is a later map, not this one.

## 3. 1765 — the Mughal name, other people's power

![[bharat-c1765.png]]

| Who | What they hold |
| --- | -------------- |
| Marathas | The widest Indian power. Shivaji died in 1680. Panipat, 1761, hurts them. They are still the large power on this map |
| Company | Bengal's diwani, **1765**, after Plassey 1757 and Buxar 1764. This is the door into modern history. It is not the whole of India |
| Delhi | The Mughal emperor is still there. His land is small |
| Also on the map | Hyderabad, Mysore under Haidar Ali, Awadh, the Sikh misls, the Rajput states |

## 4. 1947 — British rule ends. This is not today's map

![[bharat-1947.png]]

**15 August 1947.** Two dominions, India and Pakistan. Pakistan has two wings, west and east, with India between them. East Pakistan becomes **Bangladesh in 1971**. That story is modern history.

Princely states are not automatically inside either dominion. Hyderabad is still a prince in 1947 and is taken into India in **1948**. Kashmir is not settled on this map. Mysore accedes. The Republic is **1950**. The state borders you know are later, especially after **1956**. Do not draw a 2026 state map back onto 1947.
