
### KHALJI FAMILY
- **Father**: Yughrush/Yoghresh Khan
- **Mother**: not reliably recorded
- Brothers: 
	1. **Shihabuddin Mas'ud**
		1. Ali Gurshasp → Alauddin Khalji (Jalaluddin later brought Alauddin up after Shihabuddin’s death.)
		2. Almas Beg (later Ulugh Khan),
		3. Qutlugh Tign
		4. Muhammad.
		
	2. **Jalaluddin Firuz Khalji Sultan(1290-296)** (3 known sons + ≥2 daughters = at least 5 children)
		1. Malika-i-Jahan (daughter) (Alauddin Khalji married with her)
		2. Another daughter, whose personal name is not securely recorded, married **Almas Beg (Ulugh Khan)**
		3. Mahmud — given the title **Khan-i-Khan**
		4. Arkali Khan
		5. Qadr Khan — later took the royal title **Ruknuddin Ibrahim** and briefly occupied the throne after Jalaluddin's death.


### Alauddin Khalji's wives:
1. **Malika-i-Jahan**(Jalaluddin's daughter)
2. **Mahru → Khizr Khan**: She was the sister of **Malik Sanjar**, later known as **Alp Khan**. She became an important wife of Alauddin and was the mother of his eldest son **Khizr Khan**.
3. **Kamala Devi**: She had previously been the chief queen of **Karna II of Gujarat** before being taken to Alauddin's court following the Gujarat campaign.
4. **Jhatyapali**  :Daughter of **Ramachandra of Devagiri**. She was the mother of **Shihabuddin Omar**; historical summaries also identify her as the mother of **Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah**
	1. Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah(Sultan: 1316-1320)
	2. Shihabuddin Omar(Sultan: 1316)
