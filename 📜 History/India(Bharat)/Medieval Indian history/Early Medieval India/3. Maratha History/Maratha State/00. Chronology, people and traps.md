## Prelims traps

| Incorrect statement                                              | Correct understanding                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Shivaji was already Chhatrapati in 1630                          | He was born in 1630 and crowned in 1674                            |
| Shahaji was already an independent Maratha king                  | He was a powerful commander in Deccan political service            |
| The entire Pune region was independent of Bijapur                | The jagir operated under superior Sultanate authority              |
| All Maval chiefs supported Shivaji                               | Local chiefs had competing interests                               |
| Torna and Pratapgad were the same fort                           | They were different forts with distinct historical roles           |
| Shivaji peacefully inherited Javli                               | Javli was acquired through a violent political conflict            |
| Pratapgad was built by the Mughals                               | It was constructed under Shivaji's authority                       |
| Afzal Khan was a Mughal general                                  | He served Bijapur                                                  |
| Pratapgad was fought in 1665                                     | The confrontation occurred on 10 November 1659                     |
| The Pratapgad encounter has one uncontested eyewitness narrative | Accounts differ about the initial violence                         |
| Shivaji first came into conflict with the Mughals after 1659     | Important Mughal frontier conflicts had already occurred in 1657   |
| Afzal Khan was a Mughal commander                                | He served Bijapur                                                  |
| Shaista Khan was from Bijapur                                    | He was a Mughal noble                                              |
| Shivaji defeated the whole Mughal army in the Pune raid          | He carried out a surprise attack on Shaista Khan's residence       |
| Shivaji conquered and ruled Surat permanently                    | He raided it in 1664 and 1670                                      |
| Jai Singh was Shivaji's general                                  | Jai Singh I was a Rajput commander serving Aurangzeb               |
| The Treaty of Purandar was signed in 1666                        | 1665                                                               |
| Shivaji surrendered all his forts at Purandar                    | 23 ceded, 12 retained                                              |
| Shivaji escaped from Agra in 1665                                | 1666                                                               |
| Tanaji survived Sinhagad                                         | He died during the operation                                       |
| Shivaji only used guerrilla warfare                              | His forces also won conventional engagements                       |
| Shivaji's coronation occurred at Rajgad                          | Raigad, 1674                                                       |
| Aurangzeb died in 1674                                           | He died in 1707                                                    |
