**Birth:** 19 February 1630 (widely accepted official chronology)
**Birthplace:** Shivneri Fort, near Junnar, Maharashtra
**Father:** Shahaji Bhonsle
**Mother:** Jijabai Jadhav
**Grandfather:** Maloji Bhonsle
**Maternal grandfather:** Lakhuji Jadhav

### The political world into which Shivaji was born

Shivaji was born into a politically influential family, not into an established independent royal house. His father Shahaji was involved in the struggles of the Deccan Sultanates.

His father Shahaji had served competing Deccan powers. His mother Jijabai came from the Jadhav family of Sindkhed, which also possessed substantial military and aristocratic status.

The Mughal Empire was expanding towards Maharashtra.

Ahmadnagar's sovereignty was under severe pressure.

Bijapur remained powerful.

Regional chiefs exercised substantial local influence.

Western Maharashtra possessed strategically important forts, hill regions and local military networks.

Therefore, Shivaji was born into a politically fragmented but opportunity-rich environment.

### Why does his family background matter?

Shivaji inherited access to three important resources:

1. **Political connections** through the Bhonsle and Jadhav families.
2. **A territorial base** associated with his father's jagir around Pune.
3. **Military knowledge and networks** developed through decades of service under Deccan rulers.

These resources provided opportunities, but Shivaji still had to build his own alliances and authority.
