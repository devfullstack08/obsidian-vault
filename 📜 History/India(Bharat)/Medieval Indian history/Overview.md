**Medieval History(700 CE to 1750 CE)** 

You can divide **Indian Medieval History** into two major parts:

| Period                   | Approx. Time     | Main topics                                      |
| ------------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Early Medieval India** | **700–1200 CE**  | Rajputs, Palas, Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas, Cholas |
| **Later Medieval India** | **1206–1750 CE** | Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughals, Marathas |

**1. Early Medieval Kingdoms (c. 700–1200)**  
Important dynasties include **Palas**, **Gurjara-Pratiharas**, **Rashtrakutas**, **Cholas**, and various **Rajput kingdoms**. A famous topic here is the **Tripartite Struggle** among the Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas for control of **Kannauj**.

**2. Arab and Turkish invasions**  
**Muhammad bin Qasim** conquered Sindh in **712 CE**. Later came **Mahmud of Ghazni** and **Muhammad Ghori**. Muhammad Ghori's victories helped establish Turkish political power in northern India.

**3. Delhi Sultanate — 1206–1526**

Remember the five dynasties:

**M → K → T → S → L**

**M**amluk/Slave → **K**halji → **T**ughlaq → **S**ayyid → **L**odi

| Dynasty      | Period    |
| ------------ | --------- |
| Slave/Mamluk | 1206–1290 |
| Khalji       | 1290–1320 |
| Tughlaq      | 1320–1414 |
| Sayyid       | 1414–1451 |
| Lodi         | 1451–1526 |

Important rulers include **Qutb-ud-din Aibak, Iltutmish, Razia Sultan, Balban, Alauddin Khalji, Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Firoz Shah Tughlaq, Sikandar Lodi, and Ibrahim Lodi**.

**4. South Indian Kingdoms**  
Especially important are the **Vijayanagara Empire** and **Bahmani Sultanate**. **Krishnadevaraya** of Vijayanagara is particularly important for competitive exams.

**5. Bhakti and Sufi movements**  
Important personalities include **Kabir, Guru Nanak, Mirabai, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, Ramananda**, and various **Sufi saints**.

**6. Mughal Empire — 1526 onward**

The main Mughal sequence for exams is:

**Babur → Humayun → Akbar → Jahangir → Shah Jahan → Aurangzeb**

A useful mnemonic is:

**B H A J S A**

Babur defeated **Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat in 1526**, beginning Mughal rule.

**7. Sur Empire**  
Between Humayun's two periods of rule, **Sher Shah Suri** ruled much of northern India. He is important for administration, roads and currency reforms.

**8. Maratha rise**  
**Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj** established a powerful Maratha state in the 17th century. After him, the Marathas and later the **Peshwas** became major political powers.

Then comes the transition to **Modern Indian History**, where European trading companies—Portuguese, Dutch, English and French—became increasingly important.
