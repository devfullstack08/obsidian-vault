- 1498 - **vasco de Gama**(1498)  reached Calicut(Kozhikode), kerala, becoming the first Portuguese to reach India by sea from Europe.
- He arrived on 20 May 1498.
- The ruler of Calicut was the **Zamorin(Samoothiri)**.
- This opened the direct Europe-India sea route around the **Cape of Good Hope**.
- 1500 - **Pedro Alvares Cabral** come to India with a larger Portuguese fleet.
- 1505 - **Francisco de Almeida** become the first Portuguese viceroy/governor in India.
- 1510 - **Alfonso de Albuquerque** captured Goa, which become the main Portuguese base in India.


### Purpose 

| Period              | Main Purpose                         | What happened                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------------ | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Before 1498         | Find a direct sea route to India     | European countries wanted direct access to India's valuable spices and trade without relying on Middle Eastern/Venetian trades.                         |
| 1498                | Trade + find the sea route           | Vasco de gama reached Calicut. Portugal now had a direct maritime route to India.                                                                       |
| 1500-1505           | Establish trade & military presence  | Portugal sent fleets and began establishing trading posts and forts.                                                                                    |
| 1505-1509           | Control the Indian Ocean trade       | Francisco de Almeida developed the **"Blue water Policy"** - focus on naval power and controlling sea routes rather than conquering large area of land. |
| 1510                | Territorial control begins           | Albuquerque captured Goa, turning it into Portugal's major base in India.                                                                               |
| 1510-1530           | Expand territory + trade monopoloy   | Portuguese tried to control important ports and shipping routes and strengthen their monopoly over the spice trade.                                     |
| 1530 onward         | Permanent colonial empire            | Portuguese possessions expanded, including Diu, Daman, Goa, and other territories.                                                                      |
| 16th-17th centuries | Trade + religion + political control | They maintained commercial power, military forts and Catholic missionary activity.                                                                      |
| 17th-18th centuries | Decline                              | Dutch,  English and French competition weakened Portuguese commercial dominance.                                                                        |
| 1961                | End of Portuguese rule in India      | India took control of Goa, Daman and Diu in 1961                                                                                                        |
