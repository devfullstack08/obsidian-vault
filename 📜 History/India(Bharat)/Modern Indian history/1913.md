1913 — Mangarh Massacre (Mangarh Hatyakand)

- Took place in the Mangarh hills along the Rajasthan–Gujarat border.
    
- British and princely forces fired upon a gathering of Bhil tribal people associated with **Govind Guru**.
    
- Hundreds of people were killed; exact casualty estimates vary.
    
- It occurred almost six years before the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.

Important for Gujarat history, tribal movements.
