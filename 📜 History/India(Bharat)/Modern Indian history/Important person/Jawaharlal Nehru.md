Jawaharlal Nehru was born in Allahabad (present-day Prayagraj), British India.

- **Father:** Motilal Nehru
- **Mother:** Swarup Rani Nehru
- **First Prime Minister:** 15 August 1947 – 27 May 1964\
- **Popular name:** Chacha Nehru
- **Bharat Ratna:** 1955

**Why important?** Nehru was a major leader of the independence movement and played a central role in shaping India's parliamentary democracy, foreign policy, scientific institutions and planned economic development

### Nehru's important contributions

| Area             | Contribution                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Freedom struggle | Congress President at the Lahore Session, 1929, associated with the Purna Swaraj resolution |
| Economy          | Advocated planned development and a mixed economy                                           |
| Foreign policy   | Promoted non-alignment and peaceful coexistence                                             |
| Science          | Supported IITs, scientific research and technological development                           |
| Democracy        | Helped consolidate parliamentary institutions after independence                            |
| Literature       | Wrote **The Discovery of India**, Glimpses of World History and _An Autobiography           |
