Maulana Abul Kalam Azad was born in Mecca and later became an important Indian freedom fighter, scholar and political leader.

He served as independent India's first Education Minister from 1947 to 1958.

**Major contributions:**

- Promoted universal education and scientific learning.
    
- Supported the development of higher education and technical institutions.
    
- Served as Congress President during the crucial Quit India Movement period.
    
- Strongly advocated Hindu–Muslim unity and opposed India's partition.
    

**Why important?** His educational vision helped shape India's post-independence education system
