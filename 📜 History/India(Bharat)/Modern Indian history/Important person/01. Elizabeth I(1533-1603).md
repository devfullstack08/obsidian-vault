**Queen Elizabeth I** was the **Queen of England and Ireland from 1558 to 1603**. She was born in **1533** and was the daughter of **King Henry VIII** and **Anne Boleyn**. She belonged to the **Tudor dynasty** and was its last monarch. 

She is especially important for your **British-in-India timeline** because during her reign, on **31 December 1600**, she granted a royal charter to English merchants to form the **English East India Company**. That company later became extremely important in the history of British expansion in India.

Also, **Elizabeth I and Elizabeth II are different people**. Elizabeth I lived in the **16th–17th centuries**, while Elizabeth II lived from **1926 to 2022**.
