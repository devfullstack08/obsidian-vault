- During July, 1583 an english merchant, Ralph Fitch was arrested for spying at Ormuz.
- Ralph was presented before the Portuguese Viceroy in Goa where he was placed under arrest.
- **He was release on the surety provided by Jesuit priests, but escaped from Goa and wandered around India for several years. He returned to England in 1951, and become a valuable consultant for the Company**
