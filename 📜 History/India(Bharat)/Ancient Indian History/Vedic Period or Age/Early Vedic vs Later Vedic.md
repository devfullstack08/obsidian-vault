
| Topic            | Early Vedic                                    | Later Vedic                                   |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |

| Region           | North-west India<br>(Punjab, Sapta Sindhu)     | Ganga–Yamuna Doab, expansion eastward         |

| Political unit   | Jana (tribe)                                   | Larger territorial units and early kingdoms   |
| Ruler            | Rajan, tribal chief                            | Stronger hereditary monarchy                  |
| Political system | Tribal                                         | Larger kingdoms                               |
| King             | Limited chief                                  | More powerful monarch                         |
| Assemblies       | Sabha and Samiti important                     | Their political importance generally declined |
| Society          | Less rigid                                     | Varna system more structured                  |
| Religion         | Hymns and relatively less elaborate sacrifices | Increasingly elaborate sacrificial rituals    |
| Literature       | Primarily Rigveda                              | Other Vedas, Brahmanas, early Upanishads      |
| Priests          | Important                                      | Became more influential                       |
| Philosophy       | Hymns and ritual                               | Upanishadic thought developed                 |
| Technology       | Bronze/copper common                           | Iron increasingly important                   |
