A monotheistic religion founded in Punjab in the 15th century by Guru Nanak.
Sikh =  **learner / disciple / seeker of truth**
## Main beliefs of Sikhism

| Point               | Meaning                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One God**         | Sikhism believes in one formless God.                                   |
| **Equality**        | All humans are equal, regardless of caste, religion, gender, or status. |
| **Honest living**   | A person should earn by honest work.                                    |
| **Service**         | Helping others is a major duty.                                         |
| **No idol worship** | Sikhism does not support idol worship.                                  |
| **No caste system** | Sikhism strongly rejects caste discrimination.                          |
| **Naam Simran**     | Remembering and meditating on God’s name.                               |

Three main principles
1. Naam Japna → Remember God
2. Kirat Karni → Earn honestly
3. Vand Chhakna → Share with others

The holy book = Guru Granth Sahib
It is not only a scripture, but also considered the **eternal Guru** of Sikhs.

## Khalsa

In **1699**, **Guru Gobind Singh Ji** founded the **Khalsa**.

Khalsa means a community of committed Sikhs who follow discipline, courage, equality, and service.

|Five Ks|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**Kesh**|Uncut hair|
|**Kangha**|Wooden comb|
|**Kara**|Iron/steel bracelet|
|**Kachera**|Special undergarment|
|**Kirpan**|Small sword/dagger, symbol of protection and justice|

Important place
Golden Temple / Harmandir Sahib — Amritsar, Punjab
