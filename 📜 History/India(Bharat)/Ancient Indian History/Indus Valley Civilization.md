# Indus Valley Civilization: IVC (સિંધુ ખીણની સભ્યતા)

The **Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)**, also known as the **Harappan Civilization** (after Harappa, the first site discovered), is one of the oldest urban civilizations in the world, contemporaneous with Ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia. 

---

## 1. Timeline & Phases

| Phase | Duration | Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Harappan Phase** | c. 3300 BCE - 2600 BCE | Formative stage, transition from rural farming to urban life. Sites: Kot Diji, Amri, Kalibangan (early phase). |
| **Mature Harappan Phase** | c. 2600 BCE - 1900 BCE | Peak urbanization, grand cities, standardized weights, trade, covered drainage, pictographic script. Sites: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Dholavira. |
| **Late Harappan Phase** | c. 1900 BCE - 1300 BCE | Gradual decline, de-urbanization (returning to rural life), eastward/southward migration. Sites: Rangpur, Rojdi, Cemetery H (Harappa). |

---

## 2. Geographical Extent

## 4. Key Features of IVC

### A. Town Planning & Architecture 
* **Grid System:** Cities were divided into rectangular blocks with wide streets cutting each other at 90-degree angles.
* **Citadel vs. Lower Town:** Most cities were divided into a fortified **Citadel** (west, containing public buildings/upper class) and a **Lower Town** (east, residential area for common citizens). *Exception: Dholavira has three divisions.*
* **Building Material:** Burnt bricks of uniform ratio (**4:2:1**) were used for construction. No temples or palaces have been found.
* **Drainage System:** Extremely advanced covered drainage system. Every house had its own bathroom connected to street drains via brick pipes, featuring inspection manholes.

### B. Economy & Trade
* **Domestic & Foreign Trade:** Trade existed with Mesopotamia (Iraq), Persia (Iran), and Afghanistan. Mesopotamian texts refer to the Indus region as **"Meluhha"**.
* **Standardized Weights & Measures:** Weights followed a binary system (1, 2, 4, 8, 16... up to 160) and decimal system. They were made of chert stone.
* **Seals:** Over 2,000 seals made of **steatite** (soapstone) featuring animal carvings (unicorn, bull, elephant) and pictographic script were used as identity **markers for trade**.

### C. Script & Language
* **Nature:** The script is **pictographic** and has not been deciphered yet.
* **Writing Style:** Written in **Boustrophedon** style (first line from right to left, second line from left to right, and so on).

## 5. Theories of Decline

| Theory | Scholar | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aryan Invasion** | Mortimer Wheeler, Gordon Childe | Debunked by modern historians due to lack of skeletal war evidence. |
| **Ecological/Climate Change** | Robert Raikes, Fairservis | Shift in monsoon patterns, drying up of rivers, and deforestation. *(Most accepted theory today).* |
| **Tectonic Uplift / Course Change** | M.S. Vats, H.T. Lambrick | The Ghaggar-Hakra river dried up, and the Indus shifted its course, leading to water scarcity. |

## 6. Current Situation & Recent Discoveries (Current Affairs Integration)

### A. Rakhigarhi DNA Study (2019)
* **What was found:** DNA was successfully extracted from a 4,500-year-old skeletal remain of a Harappan woman at Rakhigarhi.
* **Significance:** The study proved that Harappans had an independent indigenous ancestry. There was **no Steppe (Aryan) ancestry** in the DNA, proving that the agricultural revolution in India was indigenous and not imported from Central Asia.

### B. Dholavira: UNESCO World Heritage Site (2021)
* Dholavira was declared the **40th UNESCO World Heritage Site in India** (and the first Harappan site in India to get this tag). It was recognized for its unique water harvesting system, town planning, and stone architecture.

### C. National Maritime Heritage Complex (NMHC) at Lothal
* The Government of India is currently developing a mega **National Maritime Heritage Complex** at the historic site of Lothal. It will feature a reconstruction of the Harappan dockyard and act as an international museum to showcase India's ancient maritime heritage.
