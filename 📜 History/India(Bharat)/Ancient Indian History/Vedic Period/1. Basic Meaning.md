The **Vedic Period** is one of the most important phases of ancient Indian history because many later Hindu religious ideas, social institutions, rituals, and Sanskrit literature developed from it.

The Vedic Period is the period when the **Vedas** were composed and Vedic culture developed in the Indian subcontinent.

| Period                 | Approx. Date         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Early Vedic Period** | c. **1500–1000 BCE** |
| **Later Vedic Period** | c. **1000–600 BCE**  |
