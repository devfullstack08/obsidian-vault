#### 1.Grihya Sutras

The Āśvalāyana Gṛhyasūtra is an ancient Vedic text that describes domestic rituals and ceremonies followed in household life.
**Āśvalāyana**  = name of a Vedic sage/school|
**Gṛhya** = related to  home/domestic life|
**Sūtra** = short rule/manual/instruction text|

It includes household ceremonies such as: 
- marriage
- birth rituals 
- naming ceremony 
- initiation 
- funeral rites 
- daily household worship
