### Meaning of Veda
- **Vid** = to know -
- **Veda** = knowledge / wisdom
### Origin of the Vedas 

#### According to Hindu Tradition 
- The Vedas are considered **Apauruṣeya** = not of human origin.
- They are regarded as eternal. 
- Rishis did not "compose" them in the ordinary sense; they are traditionally said to have **heard / realized** them. 
- At the beginning of each cosmic cycle, Vedic knowledge is revealed again. 
- Sage **Vyasa** is traditionally believed to have organized the Vedas into four collections. 
- So, according to Hindu tradition: 
- The Vedas do not have a specific date of origin.

---

### According to Modern Historical Scholarship 

Historians and linguists date the Vedic texts(Vedic Literature) approximately as follows: 

- **Rigveda** → c. 1500–1200 BCE ->  hymns(સ્તોત્ર,ભજન) and prayers
- **Samaveda** → later than the Rigveda -> chants and melodies
- **Yajurveda** → later Vedic development ->  sacrificial formulas
- **Atharvaveda** → later Vedic development ->  prayers, charms, rituals, everyday concerns
-  Itihasas and puranas (Itihasas-Samhita or Purana-Samhita of vyasa)):
The **Rigveda** is generally considered the oldest.


--- 
### Are the Vedas the Oldest Texts in the World? 

The Vedas are: 
- among the oldest surviving religious texts 
- among the oldest preserved works in an Indo-European language 
- extremely important because of their highly accurate oral preservation 
 
But they are not the oldest known written texts. 
Earlier examples include: 
- **Instructions of Shuruppak** 
- **Pyramid Texts** 
- early versions of the **Epic of Gilgamesh** 

So: 
**Hindu tradition:** Vedas are eternal.
**Modern historical scholarship:** the oldest parts of the Rigveda are usually dated to around 1500–1200 BCE.
