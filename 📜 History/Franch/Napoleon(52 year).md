Napoleon Bonaparte (1769–1821)

- Symbol of self-confidence and aplomb(5'2 hight).
- Born: 15 August 1769, Corsica.
- 1785: His father died in February (Napoleon was 15).
- 1785: Graduated from military school at age 16 and became a Second Lieutenant in the artillery.
- 1793: Promoted to Brigadier General at age 24 after the Siege of Toulon.
- 1–2 August 1798: Battle of the Nile (Aboukir Bay, Egypt).
    
    - British Navy: Admiral Horatio Nelson.
        
    - French Navy: Admiral François-Paul Brueys.
        
    - Winner: British Navy.
        
    - Napoleon was leading the French campaign in Egypt but did not personally fight Nelson in this naval battle.
        
    
- 1804: Became Emperor of the French.
    
- 1815: Defeated at the Battle of Waterloo.
    
- Died: 5 May 1821, Saint Helena, aged 51.

Battle of the Nile (1798) → Nelson defeated the French fleet associated with Napoleon's Egyptian expedition.

Battle of Waterloo (1815) → Napoleon was defeated by the allied armies led by the Duke of Wellington and Gebhard Leberecht von Blücher.
