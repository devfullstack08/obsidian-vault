Noah is a major figure in the **Book of Genesis**.

According to Genesis, humanity became deeply violent and corrupt, and God decided to bring a great flood. Noah is described as a righteous man who “walked with God.”

God told Noah to build an **ark** and bring into it:

- Noah
- his wife
- his three sons
- their wives
- animals, to preserve life

The flood then came and covered the land.

After the waters went down, the ark came to rest in the mountains of **Ararat**.

Noah later sent out birds to test whether the land had dried. A dove eventually returned with an **olive leaf**, which became a famous symbol of peace and hope.

After leaving the ark, Noah built an altar and offered sacrifices to God.

God then made a **covenant** with Noah and promised never again to destroy the earth by a flood in the same way.

The sign of that covenant was the **rainbow**.

# What happened during the Flood?

God instructed Noah to build a large ark.

The basic sequence is:

```
Human wickedness increases
↓
God chooses Noah
↓
Noah builds the ark
↓
Noah's family + animals enter
↓
Rain falls for 40 days and 40 nights
↓
Flood waters rise
↓
Ark survives
↓
Waters gradually decrease
↓
Ark rests on mountains of Ararat
↓
Raven and dove are sent out
↓
Noah leaves the ark
↓
Noah offers sacrifice
↓
God makes covenant with Noah
↓
Rainbow becomes covenant sign
```

One detail: **40 days of rain** does not mean the whole Flood story lasted only 40 days. The waters remained for much longer before Noah and his family finally left the ark.

---

## Why did God save Noah?

Genesis describes Noah as:

- righteous
- blameless among his generation
- someone who walked with God

That does not mean Noah was considered absolutely perfect.

Later in the story, Noah becomes drunk, showing that even after the Flood he was still a flawed human being.

---

## God's Covenant with Noah

After the Flood, God made a covenant with:

- Noah
- Noah's descendants
- every living creature

God promised that never again would a flood destroy all life in this way.

The sign was:

🌈 **Rainbow**

This is usually called the **Noahic Covenant**.

---
## Noah and the animals

People often say:

> “Noah took two of every animal.”

That is partly correct, but Genesis gives a little more detail.

For many animals, it describes **male and female pairs**.

For certain “clean” animals and birds, Genesis also describes additional numbers because some would later be used for sacrifice.

---

## Noah after the Flood

After leaving the ark, Noah became a farmer and planted a vineyard.

He drank wine and became drunk.

Then an incident happened involving his sons **Ham, Shem, and Japheth**.

Noah later pronounced:

- a curse involving **Canaan**, Ham's son
- blessings connected with Shem
- a blessing involving Japheth

This becomes important later in Genesis because these sons are presented as ancestors of different peoples.
