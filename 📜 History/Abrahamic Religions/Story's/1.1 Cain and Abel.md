Cain and Abel are brothers from the **Bible**, described as the sons of **Adam and Eve**.

- **Cain** was a farmer.
- **Abel** was a shepherd.
- Both offered gifts to God.
- God favored Abel’s offering, and Cain became jealous.
- Cain then killed Abel, making Abel the first murder victim described in the Bible and Cain the first murderer.

The story is mainly found in **Genesis 4**.

So if someone says **“Cain and Abel”**, it often refers to **brothers in conflict, jealousy, or rivalry**.
