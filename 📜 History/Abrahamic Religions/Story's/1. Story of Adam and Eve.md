## Basic Introduction

Adam and Eve are considered the first human beings in the Abrahamic religions.

In Islam, Adam is known as **Prophet Adam**, and Eve is known as **Hawwa**.

In Christianity and Judaism, Adam and Eve are described as the first man and first woman.

Their story explains:
- Creation of human beings
- Life in Paradise / Garden of Eden
- Temptation
- Disobedience
- Fall of humanity
- Human life on Earth
- Repentance and divine guidance

## Creation of Adam

God created Adam as the first human being.

In Islamic tradition, Adam was created from clay.

God gave Adam knowledge and honored him.

Adam was taught the names of things, showing his special position among creation.

---

## Angels and Iblis

In Islamic tradition, God commanded the angels to bow before Adam as a sign of respect.

The angels obeyed.

But Iblis refused.

Iblis became arrogant(ઘમંડી) and said that he was created from fire while Adam was created from clay.

Because of his pride and disobedience, Iblis was rejected.

## Important Lesson

The story of Iblis teaches that pride and arrogance can lead to downfall.

---

## Creation of Eve / Hawwa

Eve, known as Hawwa in Islam, was created as the companion of Adam.

Adam and Hawwa lived together in Paradise(સ્વર્ગ).

In Biblical tradition, Eve was created from Adam’s rib(પાંસળી).

In Islamic tradition, the Qur’an says that human beings were created from a single soul and its mate.

---

## Garden of Eden / Paradise

Adam and Eve were placed in Paradise.

In Biblical tradition, this place is called the **Garden of Eden**.

They were allowed to enjoy the blessings of Paradise.

But they were told not to approach or eat from a particular forbidden tree.

---

## Temptation(લાલચ)

Iblis / Satan tempted Adam and Eve.

He deceived them and encouraged them to eat from the forbidden tree.

Adam and Eve ate from the tree and realized their mistake.

---

## Disobedience and Fall

Because of their mistake, Adam and Eve were sent down to Earth.

This event is called the **Fall of Man** in Christian tradition.

In Islam, Adam and Hawwa made a mistake, but they repented sincerely.

God accepted Adam’s repentance.

---

## Islamic View

In Islam, Adam is a prophet.

Adam and Hawwa made a mistake, but they repented.

Allah forgave Adam.

Islam does not teach that all humans inherit Adam’s sin.

Every person is responsible for his or her own actions.

## Christian View

In Christianity, the story is connected with the idea of **Original Sin**.

Many Christian traditions believe that Adam and Eve’s disobedience affected all humanity.

Jesus Christ is understood in Christianity as the savior who brings salvation(મુક્તિ) from sin.

## Jewish View

In Judaism, Adam and Eve’s story explains human free will, temptation, moral responsibility, and life outside Eden.

Judaism generally does not understand original sin in the same way as Christianity.
