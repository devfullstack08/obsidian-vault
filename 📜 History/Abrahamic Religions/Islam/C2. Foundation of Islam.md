## Basic Introduction

Islam is one of the major Abrahamic religions.

It began in **7th century CE** in Arabia.

Muslims believe that Islam was revealed by **Allah** to **Prophet Muhammad** through Angel **Jibril**.

Prophet Muhammad is regarded by Muslims as the **final prophet and messenger of Allah**.

The foundation of Islam is based on:

- Belief in one God
- Prophethood
- Qur’an
- Moral life
- Worship
- Justice
- Equality
- Brotherhood
- Accountability before God

---

## Meaning of Islam

The word **Islam** comes from an Arabic root connected with **submission, peace, and surrender**.

In religious meaning, Islam means **submission to the will of Allah**.

A follower of Islam is called a **Muslim**.

## Simple Meaning

| Term             | Meaning                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| Islam            | Submission to Allah      |
| Muslim           | One who submits to Allah |
| Allah            | God in Islam             |
| Qur’an           | Holy book of Islam       |
| Prophet Muhammad | Final prophet in Islam   |

---

## Arabia Before Islam

Before Islam, Arabia was mostly a tribal society.

This period is called **Jahiliyyah**, meaning the **Age of Ignorance**.

## Features of Arabia Before Islam

| Feature | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Tribal society | People were loyal to tribes and clans |
| Polytheism | Many people worshipped idols |
| Kaaba | Kaaba was an important religious and trade center |
| Trade | Makkah was an important trade city |
| Poetry | Oral poetry and tribal pride were important |
| Social injustice | Slavery, tribal wars, and injustice existed |
| Poor treatment of women | Women often had low social status |
| Some monotheists | Some people followed the tradition of Prophet Ibrahim |

---

## Prophet Muhammad and First Revelation

Prophet Muhammad was born in **Makkah** in **570 CE**.

He belonged to the **Quraysh tribe** and the **Banu Hashim clan**.

Before prophethood, he was known as **Al-Amin**, meaning **the Trustworthy**.

At the age of **40**, he received the first revelation from Allah through Angel **Jibril**.

This happened in the **Cave of Hira**.

The first revealed word was:

**Iqra** — meaning “Read” or “Recite.”

This event marked the beginning of the foundation of Islam.

## Core Message of Islam

The main message of Islam is **Tawhid**.

**Tawhid** means belief in the oneness of Allah.

Islam teaches that there is only one God, and He alone should be worshipped.

## Main Message

| Principle      | Meaning                                        |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Tawhid         | Belief in one God                              |
| Risalah        | Prophethood / messengership                    |
| Akhirah        | Life after death                               |
| Accountability | Humans are answerable to Allah                 |
| Justice        | People should live with fairness               |
| Mercy          | Allah is merciful and people should show mercy |
| Equality       | All humans are equal before Allah              |

---

## Tawhid: Oneness of Allah

Tawhid is the most important foundation of Islam.

It rejects idol worship and polytheism.

Islam teaches that Allah has no partner, no son, and no equal.

## Simple Meaning

Tawhid means:

**There is only one God, Allah, and only He should be worshipped.**

---

## Shahada: Declaration of Faith

The first pillar of Islam is **Shahada**.

Shahada is the declaration of faith.

It says:

**There is no god except Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.**

## Importance of Shahada

Shahada is the basic entry point into Islam.

It expresses two main beliefs:

1. Belief in one God
2. Belief that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah

---

## Five Pillars of Islam

The foundation of Islamic practice is based on the **Five Pillars of Islam**.

| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Shahada | Declaration of faith |
| Salah | Prayer |
| Zakat | Charity |
| Sawm | Fasting in Ramadan |
| Hajj | Pilgrimage to Makkah |

---

## 1. Shahada

**Shahada** means declaration of faith.

It is the belief that there is no god except Allah and Muhammad is His messenger.

It is the foundation of Islamic belief.

## Memory Line

Shahada → Faith in Allah and His Messenger

---

## 2. Salah

**Salah** means prayer.

Muslims pray five times a day.

Prayer connects a Muslim with Allah and teaches discipline, humility, and remembrance of God.

## Memory Line

Salah → Five daily prayers

---

## 3. Zakat

**Zakat** means obligatory charity.

It is given to help the poor and needy.

Zakat teaches social justice, compassion, and purification of wealth.

## Memory Line

Zakat → Charity and social justice

---

## 4. Sawm

**Sawm** means fasting.

Muslims fast during the month of Ramadan.

Fasting teaches self-control, patience, discipline, compassion, and taqwa.

## Memory Line

Sawm → Fasting in Ramadan

---

## 5. Hajj

**Hajj** is the pilgrimage to Makkah.

It is required once in a lifetime for Muslims who are physically and financially able.

Hajj shows unity, equality, sacrifice, and devotion to Allah.

## Memory Line

Hajj → Pilgrimage to Makkah

## Prophets in Islam

Islam teaches that Allah sent many prophets to guide humanity.

Important prophets include:

- Adam
- Nuh / Noah
- Ibrahim / Abraham
- Musa / Moses
- Dawud / David
- Sulaiman / Solomon
- Isa / Jesus
- Muhammad

Muslims believe Prophet Muhammad is the **final prophet**.

---

## Early Preaching of Islam

After receiving revelation, Prophet Muhammad began preaching the message of Islam.

At first, the message was preached privately.

The earliest followers included:

- Khadijah
- Abu Bakr
- Ali ibn Abi Talib
- Zayd ibn Harithah

Later, Prophet Muhammad began preaching publicly in Makkah.

---

## Opposition in Makkah

The Quraysh leaders opposed Prophet Muhammad because Islam challenged their:

- Idol worship
- Tribal pride
- Social injustice
- Economic interests around the Kaaba
- Power and authority

Early Muslims faced persecution, insults, social boycott, torture, and hardship.

---

Islam brought many social and moral reforms.

| Reform | Explanation |
|---|---|

| Equality | All humans equal before Allah |

| Anti-idolatry | Rejection of idol worship |

## Hijrah: Migration to Madinah

Due to persecution in Makkah, Prophet Muhammad and his followers migrated to **Madinah** in **622 CE**.

This migration is called **Hijrah**.

Hijrah is very important because it marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar.

## Importance of Hijrah

| Point | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Migration | Muslims moved from Makkah to Madinah |
| Date | 622 CE |
| Importance | Beginning of Islamic calendar |
| Political importance | Muslim community became organized |
| Religious importance | Islam developed as a full community system |

---

## Foundation of Muslim Community in Madinah

In Madinah, Prophet Muhammad established a Muslim community called the **Ummah**.

The Ummah was based on faith, brotherhood, justice, and mutual support.

He also created agreements with different groups in Madinah.

One important agreement was the **Constitution of Madinah**.

---

## Constitution of Madinah

The Constitution of Madinah was an agreement among Muslims, Jews, and other groups in Madinah.

It organized the city as a political and social community.

It emphasized:

- Mutual protection
- Religious freedom
- Cooperation
- Justice
- Law and order
- Responsibility of different groups

---

## Ummah

**Ummah** means the Muslim community.

It is based on faith in Allah and brotherhood among believers.

The concept of Ummah joined people beyond tribe, race, wealth, and social status.

## Simple Meaning

Ummah → Muslim community based on faith and brotherhood

---

## Islam and Kaaba

The Kaaba in Makkah became the central place of worship in Islam.

Muslims believe the Kaaba is connected with Prophet Ibrahim and Prophet Ismail.

Muslims face the direction of the Kaaba during prayer.

This direction is called **Qibla**.

---

## Qibla

**Qibla** means the direction Muslims face during prayer.

Muslims face the Kaaba in Makkah while praying.

This creates **unity** among Muslims around the world.

## Qur’an

The Qur’an is the holy book of Islam.

Muslims believe it is the final revelation from Allah.

It was revealed to Prophet Muhammad over about 23 years.

The Qur’an gives guidance about:

- Faith
- Worship
- Morality
- Law
- Justice
- Family life
- Social life
- Hereafter

---

## Sunnah and Hadith

**Sunnah** means the example and way of Prophet Muhammad.

**Hadith** are reports about his sayings, actions, and approvals.

Together with the Qur’an, the Sunnah helps Muslims understand how to live according to Islam.

---

## Important Battles in Early Islam

During the early period, Muslims faced military conflict with the Quraysh.

| Battle               | Date   | Importance                       |
| -------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Battle of Badr       | 624 CE | First major Muslim victory       |
| Battle of Uhud       | 625 CE | Muslims faced loss and lessons   |
| Battle of the Trench | 627 CE | Madinah defended against enemies |

These battles were part of the struggle of the early Muslim community for survival.

---

## Treaty of Hudaybiyyah

The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was made in **628 CE** between Muslims and Quraysh.

At first, some Muslims felt it was unfavorable.

But it created peace and allowed Islam to spread more widely.

It became an important diplomatic success.

---

## Conquest of Makkah

In **630 CE**, Prophet Muhammad and the Muslims entered Makkah.

The conquest was largely peaceful.

Idols were removed from the Kaaba.

Makkah became the central sacred city of Islam.

---

## Farewell Pilgrimage and Final Sermon

In **632 CE**, Prophet Muhammad performed his Farewell Pilgrimage.

During this pilgrimage, he delivered the **Final Sermon**.

The sermon emphasized:

- Equality of all humans
- Rights of women
- Justice
- No superiority by race or tribe
- Brotherhood
- Avoiding oppression
- Following the Qur’an and Sunnah

---

## Death of Prophet Muhammad

Prophet Muhammad died in **632 CE** in Madinah.

After his death, Islam continued to spread through Arabia and beyond.

The leadership of the Muslim community passed to the caliphs.

## Short Summary

Islam began in 7th century CE Arabia through the revelation given to Prophet Muhammad. Muslims believe that Allah revealed the Qur’an to Prophet Muhammad through Angel Jibril. The foundation of Islam is based on Tawhid, or belief in one God, and on the teachings of the Qur’an and Sunnah. The Five Pillars of Islam are Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj. After facing opposition in Makkah, Prophet Muhammad migrated to Madinah in 622 CE. This migration, called Hijrah, marked the beginning of the Islamic calendar and the foundation of the Muslim community, or Ummah.
