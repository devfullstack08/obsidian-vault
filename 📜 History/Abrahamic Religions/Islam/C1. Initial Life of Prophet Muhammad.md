
## Basic Introduction

Prophet Muhammad was the **founder** of Islam and is regarded by Muslims as the final prophet and messenger of Allah.

He was born in **Makkah** in Arabia in **570 CE**.

This year is traditionally known as the **Year of the Elephant**.

His full name was **Muhammad ibn Abdullah**.

He belonged to the respected **Quraysh tribe**.

## Arabia Before Islam

Before the birth of Prophet Muhammad, Arabia was mostly tribal society.

This period is called **Jahiliyyah**, meaning the Age of Ignorance(અજ્ઞાન).

## Main Features of Arabian Society Before Islam

| Feature          | Explanation                                                                 |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tribal society   | People were loyal to their tribes and clans                                 |
| Polytheism       | Many Arabs worshipped idols                                                 |
| Kaaba            | Kaaba was an important religious and trade center                           |
| Trade            | Makkah was an important trade city                                          |
| Poetry           | Poetry and oral tradition were important                                    |
| Social problems  | Tribal wars, injustice, slavery, and poor treatment of women existed        |
| Some monotheists | Some people still believed in one God and followed the tradition of Ibrahim |

## Birth of Prophet Muhammad

Prophet Muhammad was born in **Makkah** around **570 CE**.

His father, **Abdullah**, died before his birth.

Because of this, Prophet Muhammad was born as an orphan(અનાથ).

His mother was **Aminah bint Wahb**.

He belonged to the **Banu Hashim** clan of the Quraysh tribe.

---

## Year of the Elephant

The year of Prophet Muhammad’s birth is traditionally called the **Year of the Elephant**.

According to Islamic tradition, this year is connected with **Abraha**, the ruler of Yemen, who came with an army and elephants to attack the Kaaba.

The attack failed.

This event is mentioned in the Qur’an in **Surah Al-Fil**.

## Memory Line

Year of the Elephant → Abraha tried to attack the Kaaba → Prophet Muhammad was born in the same year

---

## Early Childhood

In Arab tradition, noble families often sent their children to live in the desert with Bedouin families.

This was done so that children could grow strong, learn pure Arabic, and live in a healthy environment.

Prophet Muhammad was cared for by **Halimah Sa’diyah** during his early childhood.

---

## Death of His Mother

When Prophet Muhammad was about **six years old**, his mother **Aminah** died.

After her death, he was cared for by his grandfather **Abd al-Muttalib**.

---

## Care of His Grandfather

After the death of his mother, Prophet Muhammad came under the care of his grandfather **Abd al-Muttalib**.

Abd al-Muttalib was a respected leader of Makkah.

However, he also died when Prophet Muhammad was about **eight years old**.

---

## Care of Abu Talib

After the death of his grandfather, Prophet Muhammad was cared for by his uncle **Abu Talib**.

Abu Talib was the father of **Ali ibn Abi Talib**.

He protected Prophet Muhammad and supported him later when he began preaching Islam.

---

## Early Work

Prophet Muhammad worked as a shepherd(ભરવાડ) in his youth.

Later, he became involved in trade.

He was known for his honesty, truthfulness, and trustworthy nature.

Because of his character, people called him:

**Al-Amin**, meaning “the Trustworthy.”

---

## Title: Al-Amin

| Title    | Meaning         |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Al-Amin  | The Trustworthy |
| As-Sadiq | The Truthful    |

People of Makkah trusted him because he was honest, truthful, and reliable.

---

## Journey to Syria

As a young man, Prophet Muhammad travelled for trade.

He accompanied his uncle Abu Talib on trade journeys.

One famous journey was toward **Syria**.

These journeys gave him experience in trade and wider society.

---

## Marriage with Khadijah

Prophet Muhammad later worked for **Khadijah bint Khuwaylid**, a wealthy and respected businesswoman of Makkah.

She was impressed by his honesty and good character.

Prophet Muhammad married Khadijah when he was about **25 years old**.

Khadijah became his first wife.

She later became the first person to accept Islam.

---

## Family Life

Prophet Muhammad and Khadijah had children.

Their daughters included:

- Zainab
- Ruqayyah
- Umm Kulthum
- Fatimah

Fatimah is especially important in Islamic history because she was the wife of Ali ibn Abi Talib and the mother of Hasan and Husayn.

---

## Character Before Prophethood

Before receiving revelation, Prophet Muhammad was already respected in Makkah.

He was known for:

- Honesty
- Truthfulness
- Good manners
- Trustworthiness
- Justice
- Kindness
- Calm nature
- Helping others
- Avoiding idol worship
- Avoiding immoral practices of society

---

## Hilf al-Fudul

As a young man, Prophet Muhammad participated in **Hilf al-Fudul**.

It was an agreement among some Makkans to protect the weak and give justice to the oppressed.

This shows that even before prophethood, Prophet Muhammad supported justice and fairness.

---

## Rebuilding of the Kaaba

When Prophet Muhammad was about **35 years old**, the Kaaba was rebuilt after damage.

During the rebuilding, the tribes of Makkah argued about who should place the **Black Stone** back in its position.

To solve the dispute, Prophet Muhammad suggested placing the Black Stone on a cloth.

Representatives of each tribe lifted the cloth together, and Prophet Muhammad placed the stone in its position.

This solution avoided conflict and showed his wisdom.

---

## Spiritual Reflection

Before prophethood, Prophet Muhammad often went to the **Cave of Hira** near Makkah.

He spent time there in prayer, reflection, and meditation.

He disliked the idol worship, injustice, and moral problems of Arabian society.

---

## First Revelation

At the age of **40**, Prophet Muhammad received the first revelation(સાક્ષાત્કાર) from Allah through Angel Jibril.

This happened in the **Cave of Hira**.

The first revealed words were:

**Iqra** — meaning “Read” or “Recite.”

This marked the beginning of his prophethood.

## Short Summary

Prophet Muhammad was born in Makkah in 570 CE, in the Quraysh tribe and Banu Hashim clan. His father Abdullah died before his birth, and his mother Aminah died when he was about six years old. He was later cared for by his grandfather Abd al-Muttalib and then by his uncle Abu Talib. In his youth, he worked as a shepherd and trader and became known as Al-Amin, meaning the Trustworthy. At the age of 25, he married Khadijah. Before prophethood, he often reflected in the Cave of Hira. At the age of 40, he received the first revelation through Angel Jibril, marking the beginning of Islam.

## First Revelation and Laylat al-Qadr

Prophet Muhammad received the first revelation from Allah through Angel Jibril in the Cave of Hira.

This event happened in the month of Ramadan.

The first revealed words were:

**Iqra** — meaning “Read” or “Recite.”

This night is connected with **Laylat al-Qadr**, also called the **Night of Power** or **Night of Decree**.

Muslims believe that the Qur’an began to be revealed on Laylat al-Qadr.

The exact date of Laylat al-Qadr is not fixed with full certainty.

Many Muslims search for Laylat al-Qadr in the last ten nights of Ramadan, especially the odd nights:

- 21st night
- 23rd night
- 25th night
- 27th night
- 29th night

Many Muslims especially observe the 27th night of Ramadan, but other odd nights are also considered important.

Therefore, it is better to say that the first revelation happened in Ramadan on Laylat al-Qadr, not simply on the 21st of Ramadan.

## Important Point

21st Ramadan can be one of the possible odd nights of Laylat al-Qadr, but it is not universally accepted as the exact date of the first revelation.
