# Recall questions and exam traps

## Say the answer before opening the box

1. Who became the first caliph after Muhammad’s death in 632?
2. Put Abu Bakr, Ali, Uthman and Umar in order.
3. Which dynasty used Damascus as its capital?
4. Who was killed at Karbala in 680?
5. Was Qasim a caliph, sultan or commander?
6. Name Qasim’s governor-patron and the reigning caliph.
7. Which ruler did Qasim defeat in 712?
8. Which dynasty took over the central caliphate in 750?
9. Which city was founded as the Abbasid capital in 762?
10. Why is the Samanid background useful before studying Ghazni?
10a. Who made Ghazni his capital in 962, and who reigned there from 998 to 1030?
11. Which famous ruler at Ghazni reigned from 998 to 1030?
12. Who won First Tarain? Who won Second Tarain?
13. Which battle is associated with Jayachandra in 1194?
14. Who is conventionally the first Delhi sultan, from 1206?
15. Did the Abbasid caliphate end before Mahmud’s reign?

> [!success]- Answers
> 1. Abu Bakr.
> 2. Abu Bakr → Umar → Uthman → Ali.
> 3. Umayyads.
> 4. Husayn, Ali’s son and Muhammad’s grandson.
> 5. Commander.
> 6. Al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf; al-Walid I.
> 7. Raja Dahir of Sindh.
> 8. Abbasids.
> 9. Baghdad, under al-Mansur.
> 10. By the end of the ninth century the caliphate had declined. Turkish commanders founded regional kingdoms, and the caliph became symbolic. The Samanids, an Iranian dynasty at Bukhara, were the state Alptigin served before he took Ghazni.
> 10a. Alptigin in 962. Mahmud, 998–1030. Sebuktigin, Alptigin’s slave, ruled from 977 and built the state. Mahmud was Sebuktigin’s son.
> 11. Mahmud of Ghazni.
> 12. Prithviraj in 1191; Ghori in 1192.
> 13. Chandawar.
> 14. Qutb-ud-din Aibak.
> 15. No. The Abbasid caliphate in Iraq lasted until 1258.

## Traps to correct

- **Qasim = khalifa:** incorrect; he served the Umayyad government as a commander.
- **Mahmud = Muhammad of Ghazni:** different people; the latter was Mahmud’s son.
- **Alptigin led the Indian raids:** no. He took Ghazni in 962. Mahmud, 998–1030, led the campaigns.
- **Sebuktigin was Alptigin’s son:** no. He was Alptigin’s slave. Mahmud was Sebuktigin’s son.
- **The caliph commanded Mahmud’s army:** no. By then the caliph’s authority was symbolic. He granted titles.
- **Ghazni = Ghor:** a city and a separate mountainous region.
- **All Muslim rulers were Arabs:** religion and ethnic background are different.
- **Qasim conquered all India:** his durable conquests centred on Sindh and the lower Indus region, extending to Multan.
- **712 = first Arab contact with India:** trade was older; political conquest is a different milestone.
- **Mahmud established Delhi Sultanate:** its conventional beginning is Aibak in 1206.
- **Ghori was the first Sultan of Delhi:** he laid the base. Aibak, from 1206, is the first sultan.
- **Prithviraj won Tarain:** he won the first battle, 1191. Ghori won the second, 1192.
- **1178 Gujarat was Bhima II:** that defeat belongs to Mularaja II and the regent Naikidevi. Bhima II is Aibak’s campaign in 1197. Gujarat was not annexed in either year.
- **Abbasids → Ghaznavids means complete replacement:** the states overlapped.
- **Every religious change followed directly from a battle:** conversion and cultural exchange had longer and varied histories.

## One-minute explanation

Explain aloud: **632 → four caliphs → Umayyads → Qasim in Sindh → Abbasids → regional powers → Mahmud → Ghori → Aibak.** Then explain why this is not one continuous dynasty.
