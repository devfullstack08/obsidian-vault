# After Muhammad: the order of the chapters

One road, from **632** to **1206**. These are different states, not one family of Muhammad’s descendants.

![[arabia-to-medieval-india-poster.png]]

The pictures are symbols, not portraits and not a scale of years. Read the chapters for the dates.

1. [[01. Origins of the Schism]] — 632. Who leads? Sunni and Shia.

## Three men who are not the same person

| Man                | State             | Place        | Date                          |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Muhammad bin Qasim | Umayyad commander | Sindh        | 712                           |
| Mahmud of Ghazni   | Ghaznavid ruler   | Ghazni       | 998–1030                      |
| Muhammad Ghori     | Ghurid ruler      | toward Delhi | Second Tarain 1192; dies 1206 |

Dates are CE. “India” here means the historical subcontinent. This road is not the whole of medieval India: keep the Pratiharas, Palas, Rashtrakutas and Cholas beside it.
