## Basic Introduction

The **Day of Qiyamah** means the **Day of Resurrection** or **Day of Judgment**.

In Islam, Muslims believe that this world is temporary and one day it will end.

After death, all human beings will be raised again by Allah and judged according to their faith and actions.

This final day is called:

- Yawm al-Qiyamah
- Day of Resurrection
- Day of Judgment
- Day of Accountability
- Akhirah / Hereafter

---

## Meaning of Qiyamah

**Qiyamah** means rising or standing up.

It refers to the day when all dead people will be raised from their graves and stand before Allah for judgment.

---

## Main Belief

Muslims believe that:

- This world is temporary.
- Death is not the end.
- All people will be resurrected.
- Everyone will be judged by Allah.
- Good and bad deeds will be weighed.
- The righteous will enter Jannah.
- The wicked will be punished in Jahannam.

---

## Place in Islamic Belief

Belief in the Day of Judgment is one of the basic beliefs of Islam.

It is part of the **Six Articles of Faith**.

| Article of Faith | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Belief in Allah | One God |
| Belief in Angels | Angels created by Allah |
| Belief in Books | Divine scriptures |
| Belief in Prophets | Messengers of Allah |
| Belief in Day of Judgment | Final resurrection and accountability |
| Belief in Divine Decree | Allah’s knowledge and will |

---

## Why Day of Qiyamah Is Important

The Day of Qiyamah is important because it teaches:

- Humans are accountable for their actions.
- Good deeds will be rewarded.
- Evil actions will be punished.
- Life has a moral purpose.
- Justice will be completed by Allah.
- Death is not the final end.
- People should live with taqwa and righteousness.

## Barzakh

**Barzakh** means the intermediate stage between death and resurrection.

It is the period after death and before the Day of Qiyamah.

In this stage, a person waits until the final resurrection.

---

## Blowing of the Trumpet

In Islamic belief, Angel **Israfil** will blow the trumpet by Allah’s command.

This will mark the end of the world.

Then, by Allah’s command, the trumpet will be blown again and people will be resurrected.

---

## Resurrection

Resurrection means that all people will be raised again after death.

Every human being from the beginning of time to the end of time will be brought back to life.

They will stand before Allah for judgment.

---

## Gathering of Humanity

On the Day of Qiyamah, all humans will be gathered together.

No one will be able to hide.

Every person will be responsible for his or her own deeds.

Wealth, status, power, and family background will not save anyone.

Only faith, Allah’s mercy, and righteous deeds will matter.

---

## Book of Deeds

In Islamic belief, every person has a record of deeds.

Angels record human actions.

On the Day of Judgment, each person will receive their book of deeds.

| Receiving Book                 | Meaning                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| In the right hand              | Sign of success and reward     |
| In the left hand / behind back | Sign of failure and punishment |

---

## Weighing of Deeds

Good and bad deeds will be weighed.

This is called the **Mizan**.

If good deeds are heavier, the person will be successful by Allah’s mercy.

If bad deeds are heavier, the person may face punishment.

---

## Sirat Bridge

The **Sirat** is described in Islamic tradition as a bridge over Hell.

People will cross it according to their faith and deeds.

The righteous will cross safely by Allah’s mercy.

The wicked will fail and fall into punishment.

---

## Jannah

**Jannah** means Paradise.

It is the final reward for righteous believers by Allah’s mercy.

Jannah is a place of peace, happiness, mercy, and eternal life.

---

## Jahannam

**Jahannam** means Hell.

It is the place of punishment for evil, disbelief, hypocrisy, and serious sins.

It shows Allah’s justice against wrongdoing.

---

## Signs of Qiyamah

Islamic tradition mentions signs before the Day of Qiyamah.

They are usually divided into:

- Minor signs
- Major signs

---

## Minor Signs

Minor signs are events and conditions that appear before the end times.

Examples include:

- Increase in injustice
- Increase in dishonesty
- Loss of trust
- Spread of immorality
- Competition in building tall buildings
- Increase in killing and conflict
- People becoming careless about religion
- Spread of greed and materialism

---

## Major Signs

Major signs are great events close to the end of time.

Islamic traditions mention signs such as:

- Appearance of Dajjal
- Return of Prophet Isa
- Appearance of Ya’juj and Ma’juj
- Smoke
- Rising of the sun from the west
- Beast of the earth
- Major earthquakes or disturbances
- Great fire gathering people

Different Islamic scholars explain the order and details differently.

---

## Dajjal

**Dajjal** means the false messiah or great deceiver.

He will mislead many people before the end times.

Muslims are taught to seek Allah’s protection from the trial of Dajjal.

---

## Return of Prophet Isa

Islam teaches that Prophet Isa will return before the Day of Judgment.

He will defeat falsehood and restore justice by Allah’s will.

This belief is different from the Christian concept of the Second Coming.

---

## Ya’juj and Ma’juj

**Ya’juj and Ma’juj** are known as Gog and Magog.

They are connected with end-time events.

Islamic tradition says they will cause great disorder before being destroyed by Allah.

## Moral Message of Qiyamah

The belief in Qiyamah teaches Muslims to live responsibly.

A Muslim should:

- Worship Allah
- Avoid sins
- Speak truth
- Help the poor
- Respect parents
- Be honest
- Avoid injustice
- Repent sincerely
- Prepare for the Hereafter
- Remember that every deed is recorded

## Short Summary

The Day of Qiyamah is the Islamic belief in the final Day of Resurrection and Judgment. Muslims believe that the world will end, all people will be raised again, and every person will be judged by Allah according to faith and deeds. The good and bad actions of people will be recorded, presented, and weighed. The righteous will enter Jannah by Allah’s mercy, while the wicked may face punishment in Jahannam. Belief in Qiyamah teaches accountability, justice, moral living, repentance, and preparation for the Hereafter.
