| No. | Hebrew month | Rough Gregorian period | Easy memory                                 |
| --- | ------------ | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Nisan**    | March–April            | **N** = New beginning(first biblical month) |
| 2   | **Iyar**     | April–May              | Nisan → **I**yar                            |
| 3   | **Sivan**    | May–June               | Think **S** = summer approaching            |
| 4   | **Tammuz**   | June–July              | Hot summer                                  |
| 5   | **Av**       | July–August            | **A**ugust ≈ Av                             |
| 6   | **Elul**     | August–September       | Preparation before New Year                 |
| 7   | **Tishrei**  | September–October      | Major Jewish New Year month                 |
| 8   | **Cheshvan** | October–November       | After Tishrei                               |
| 9   | **Kislev**   | November–December      | Hanukkah begins in Kislev                   |
| 10  | **Tevet**    | December–January       | Winter                                      |
| 11  | **Shevat**   | January–February       | Tu BiShvat                                  |
| 12  | **Adar**     | February–March         | Purim month                                 |

| **Tishrei** | **Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot** |
| **Kislev**  | **Hanukkah**                          |
| **Adar**    | **Purim**                             |
