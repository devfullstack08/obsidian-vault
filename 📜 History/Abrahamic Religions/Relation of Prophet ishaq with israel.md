## Basic Introduction

Prophet Ishaq, also known as Isaac, was the son of Prophet Ibrahim and Sarah.

He is an important prophet in Islam and also an important patriarch in Judaism and Christianity.

## Family Line

| Person                    | Relation                                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Prophet Ibrahim / Abraham | Father of Prophet Ishaq                           |
| Sarah                     | Mother of Prophet Ishaq                           |
| Prophet Ishaq / Isaac     | Son of Prophet Ibrahim                            |
| Prophet Yaqub / Jacob     | Son of Prophet Ishaq                              |
| Israel (to rule)          | Another name/title of Prophet Yaqub               |
| Bani Israel / Israelites  | Children or Descendants of Prophet Yaqub / Israel |

## Relation with Israel

The word Israel is connected with Prophet Yaqub.

Prophet Yaqub was the son of Prophet Ishaq.

According to Abrahamic tradition, Prophet Yaqub was also called Israel.

Therefore, the descendants of Prophet Yaqub are called Bani Israel or Children of Israel.

## Prophets from Bani Israel

Many later prophets came from the descendants of Prophet Yaqub.  
  
These prophets were not all direct sons of Yaqub. Most of them came many generations later from Bani Israel / Israelites(English term for Bani Israel).

| Prophet          | Relation with Bani Israel               |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Prophet Yusuf    | Direct son of Prophet Yaqub             |
| Prophet Musa     | Later prophet from Bani Israel          |
| Prophet Harun    | Brother of Musa; from Bani Israel       |
| Prophet Dawud    | Later prophet and king from Bani Israel |
| Prophet Sulaiman | Son of Dawud; from Bani Israel          |
| Prophet Zakariya | Later prophet from Bani Israel          |
| Prophet Yahya    | Son of Zakariya; from Bani Israel       |
| Prophet Isa      | Later prophet from Bani Israel          |

## Important Difference  
  
| List | Meaning |  
|---|---|  
| Twelve sons of Yaqub | Direct sons of Prophet Yaqub |  
| Prophets of Bani Israel | Later prophets from the descendants of Yaqub |  
  
## Example  
  
Prophet Yusuf was a direct son of Prophet Yaqub.  
  
Prophet Musa, Harun, Dawud, Sulaiman, Zakariya, Yahya, and Isa were later prophets from Bani Israel.

## Simple Chain

Prophet Ibrahim → Prophet Ishaq → Prophet Yaqub / Israel → 12 sons → 12 tribes of Bani Israel → later prophets of Bani Israel

## Simple Explanation

Prophet Ishaq himself was not called Israel.
His son Prophet Yaqub was called Israel.

## Short Summary

Prophet Ishaq is related to Israel because his son Prophet Yaqub was also known as Israel. The descendants of Prophet Yaqub are called Bani Israel or Israelites.
