## Basic Information

- **Name:** Saint Lucy
- **Also Known As:** Saint Lucia
- **Born:** Around 283 CE
- **Died:** Around 304 CE
- **Birthplace:** Syracuse, Sicily
- **Religion:** Christianity
- **Feast Day:** 13 December (*Those whose hearts are pure are temples of the holy spirit*)
- **Known For:** Christian martyr and patron saint of the blind

---

## Who Was St. Lucy?

St. Lucy was an early Christian martyr from **Syracuse in Sicily**.

She lived during the time when Christians were persecuted under the Roman Empire.

According to Christian tradition, Lucy decided to dedicate her life to God and remain unmarried.

Her refusal to marry and her Christian faith eventually led to her persecution and death.

---
## Meaning of the Name "Lucy"

The name **Lucy** comes from the Latin word:

> **Lux = Light**

Therefore, St. Lucy is strongly associated with:

- Light
- Vision
- Hope
- Faith

---

## Why Is St. Lucy Famous?

St. Lucy is especially famous as the:

**Patron Saint of the Blind and People with Eye Problems**

She is often shown in Christian art:

- Holding a lamp
- Holding candles
- Carrying a plate with eyes
- Wearing a crown of candles

These symbols represent **light and vision**.

---

## Story of St. Lucy

According to tradition:

1. Lucy was born into a wealthy Christian family.
2. Her father died when she was young.
3. Her mother wanted Lucy to marry.
4. Lucy wanted to dedicate herself completely to Christianity.
5. She gave much of her wealth to the poor.
6. The man she was supposed to marry became angry.
7. He reported her as a Christian to Roman authorities.
8. Lucy refused to abandon her Christian faith.
9. She was tortured and eventually killed.

Because she died for her religious beliefs, she became known as a **martyr**.

---

## St. Lucy and Eyes

One famous tradition connects St. Lucy with eyesight.

Some legends say her eyes were damaged or removed during her persecution.

Because of this association, she later became the:

**Patron Saint of Blind People**

However, some parts of this story come from later Christian legends rather than early historical records.

## St. Lucy's Day in Sweden

St. Lucy's Day is very famous in Sweden.

A girl traditionally represents **Lucia** and wears:

- A white dress
- A red sash
- A crown with candles

She may lead a procession while people sing traditional songs.

The candles symbolize:

**Light during the darkness of winter.**
