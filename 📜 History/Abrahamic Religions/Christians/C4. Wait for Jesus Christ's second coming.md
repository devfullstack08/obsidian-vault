
## Basic Meaning

The **Second Coming of Jesus Christ** means the future return of Jesus to the world.

In Christianity, Christians believe that Jesus came once in history, lived on earth, was crucified, rose from the dead, and ascended to heaven.

They believe that he will come again at the end of time.

This future return is called the **Second Coming**.

---

## Simple Meaning

| Term           | Meaning                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| First Coming   | Birth and earthly life of Jesus             |
| Ascension      | Jesus going up to heaven after resurrection |
| Second Coming  | Future return of Jesus Christ               |
| End Times      | Final period before God’s judgment          |
| Final Judgment | God’s final judgment of humanity            |

---

## Why Christians Wait for the Second Coming

Christians wait for Jesus Christ’s Second Coming because they believe he will return to:

- Defeat evil
- Judge humanity
- Establish God’s kingdom fully
- Bring justice and peace
- Reward the righteous
- Complete God’s plan of salvation

---

## Connection with Resurrection and Ascension

After the crucifixion, Christians believe Jesus rose from the dead on the third day.

After appearing to his disciples for forty days, he ascended to heaven.

Christians believe that just as Jesus ascended to heaven, he will return again in glory.

---

## Main Belief

The main Christian belief is:

Jesus came once as savior, and he will come again as judge and king.

---

## Biblical Idea

In the New Testament, the Second Coming is connected with the belief that Jesus will return suddenly and gloriously.

Christians are taught to remain faithful, morally prepared, and spiritually alert.

## “Wait” Does Not Mean Doing Nothing

In Christianity, waiting for Jesus’ Second Coming does not mean sitting idle.

It means living with faith, prayer, good actions, moral purity, and service to others.

Christians are taught to:
- Follow Jesus’ teachings
- Love God
- Love neighbors
- Help the poor
- Avoid sin
- Stay faithful
- Be ready for God’s judgment

## Connection with Final Judgment

Christians believe that the Second Coming is connected with the **Final Judgment**.

At that time, God will judge humanity.

Good and evil will be separated.

The righteous will receive eternal life, while evil will face punishment.

---

## Different Christian Views

All major Christian traditions believe in the Second Coming, but they may explain the details differently.

| Tradition               | General View                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Catholic Christianity   | Jesus will return in glory and judge the living and the dead      |
| Orthodox Christianity   | Jesus will return at the end of time and establish final judgment |
| Protestant Christianity | Jesus will return; details differ among denominations(સંપ્રદાયો)  |
| Evangelical groups      | Often strongly emphasize end-time signs and readiness             |

---

## Islamic View of Jesus’ Return

Islam also has a belief in the return of Prophet Isa before the Day of Judgment.

In Islam, Prophet Isa was not killed or crucified in the Christian sense.

Muslims believe Allah raised him, and he will return before the end times.

However, the Islamic explanation is different from the Christian doctrine.

| Topic | Christianity | Islam |
|---|---|
| Jesus | Son of God, Messiah, Savior | Prophet Isa, Messiah, not Son of God |
| Crucifixion | Jesus died on the cross | Isa was not truly killed or crucified |
| Return | Jesus will return in glory | Isa will return before the Day of Judgment |
| Purpose | Final judgment and completion of salvation | Defeat falsehood and restore justice by Allah’s will |

---

## Important Difference from Islam

Christianity connects the Second Coming with Jesus’ divine role as Son of God, Savior, Judge, and King.

Islam connects the return of Isa with the signs of the Day of Judgment, but does not accept Jesus as God or Son of God.

## Short Summary

The Second Coming of Jesus Christ is the Christian belief that Jesus will return at the end of time. Christians believe that he first came as savior and teacher, and he will come again in glory as judge and king. This belief is connected with final judgment, the defeat of evil, the completion of God’s kingdom, and the hope of eternal life. Christians are taught to wait for this event with faith, moral living, prayer, and readiness.
