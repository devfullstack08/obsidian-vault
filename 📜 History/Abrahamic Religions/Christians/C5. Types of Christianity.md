# Orthodox vs Catholic vs Protestant

| Topic                                     | Eastern Orthodox                                                                                                                                                                                        | Roman Catholic                                                                                                                                   | Protestant                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Historical Origin**                     | Early Christian Church; Eastern branch formally separated from Rome in the Great Schism of **1054 AD**                                                                                                  | Early Christian Church; Western branch centered in Rome                                                                                          | Reformation beginning in the **16th century**, especially 1517                                                         |
| **Important Early Figure**                | Apostles and early Church Fathers; traditionally strong connection with St. Andrew in Constantinople                                                                                                    | Apostles and early Church Fathers; especially St. Peter in Roman tradition                                                                       | Martin Luther is a major Reformation figure, along with others such as Calvin and Zwingli                              |
| **Main Symbol**                           | Orthodox Cross                                                                                                                                                                                          | Crucifix / Latin Cross                                                                                                                           | Usually Latin Cross                                                                                                    |
| **Church Architecture**                   | Often highly decorated, domes, icons                                                                                                                                                                    | Often grand churches, cathedrals, statues, stained glass                                                                                         | Varies; often simpler, but some denominations also have grand churches                                                 |
| **Interior Style**                        | Icons, iconostasis, candles, incense                                                                                                                                                                    | Statues, images, altar, candles, stained glass                                                                                                   | Often simpler; varies greatly by denomination                                                                          |
| **Bible**                                 | Canon varies by Orthodox tradition; generally more books than Catholic and Protestant Bibles                                                                                                            | **73 books**                                                                                                                                     | **66 books** in most Protestant traditions                                                                             |
| **Main Clergy**                           | Priests, bishops, patriarchs                                                                                                                                                                            | Priests, bishops, cardinals, Pope                                                                                                                | Pastors, ministers, elders; structure varies                                                                           |
| **Highest Human Authority**               | No single universal pope; bishops and patriarchs govern together                                                                                                                                        | **Pope** is Bishop of Rome and highest earthly authority in the Catholic Church                                                                  | No single universal authority; depends on denomination                                                                 |
| **Confession**                            | Sacramental confession normally to a priest                                                                                                                                                             | Sacramental confession normally to a priest                                                                                                      | Usually direct confession to God; some denominations also practice pastoral or corporate confession                    |
| **Blessings**                             | Holy water, oil, prayer, incense, blessings by clergy                                                                                                                                                   | Holy water, blessed objects, oil, prayer                                                                                                         | Prayer, laying on of hands; practices vary                                                                             |
| **Prayer Items**                          | Prayer rope, icons                                                                                                                                                                                      | Rosary, crucifix, medals                                                                                                                         | Usually no required prayer item; some Anglicans and others use prayer beads                                            |
| **Afterlife**                             | Heaven, Hell, judgment; teachings on the intermediate state and prayers for the dead                                                                                                                    | Heaven, Hell, **Purgatory**, judgment                                                                                                            | Usually Heaven, Hell, judgment; beliefs vary by denomination                                                           |
| **Music**                                 | Chant, especially Byzantine or Slavic traditions                                                                                                                                                        | Gregorian chant, hymns, organ, classical and modern worship music                                                                                | Hymns, gospel, choir, contemporary worship; depends on denomination                                                    |
| **Sacraments**                            | Traditionally **7 Holy Mysteries**                                                                                                                                                                      | **7 Sacraments**                                                                                                                                 | Many recognize **2 main ordinances/sacraments**: Baptism and Communion, but terminology varies                         |
| **Baptism**                               | Infants and adults; usually immersion                                                                                                                                                                   | Infants and adults; usually pouring, though immersion is also possible                                                                           | Depends on denomination: some baptize infants, others only professing believers                                        |
| **Eucharist / Communion**                 | Belief in the real presence of Christ; consecrated bread and wine                                                                                                                                       | Belief in the real presence; Catholic doctrine uses **transubstantiation**                                                                       | Views vary: symbolic memorial, spiritual presence, or real presence depending on denomination                          |
| **Mary**                                  | Highly honored as **Theotokos (Mother of God)**                                                                                                                                                         | Highly honored; doctrines include Immaculate Conception and Assumption                                                                           | Honored as the mother of Jesus, but usually given less devotional emphasis                                             |
| **Saints**                                | Venerated; believers may ask saints to pray for them                                                                                                                                                    | Venerated; believers may ask saints to intercede                                                                                                 | Usually not invoked in prayer; varies somewhat among Anglicans/Lutherans                                               |
| **Icons / Images**                        | Icons are very important in worship and devotion                                                                                                                                                        | Religious statues and images are widely used                                                                                                     | Varies; some use images freely, others avoid them                                                                      |
| **Clergy Marriage**                       | Married men may become priests, but bishops are normally celibate monks                                                                                                                                 | Latin-rite priests are normally celibate; some exceptions exist                                                                                  | Ministers/pastors are generally allowed to marry                                                                       |
| **Main Worship Day**                      | Sunday                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Sunday                                                                                                                                           | Sunday                                                                                                                 |
| **Major Feast Structure**                 | **12 Great Feasts + Pascha (Easter)**, with Pascha as the highest feast                                                                                                                                 | Many liturgical feasts and Holy Days of Obligation; number varies by country                                                                     | Varies by denomination; Christmas and Easter are usually central                                                       |
| **Major Orthodox Feasts**                 | Nativity of Mary; Exaltation of the Cross; Presentation of Mary; Christmas; Theophany; Presentation of Christ; Annunciation; Palm Sunday; Ascension; Pentecost; Transfiguration; Dormition; plus Pascha | —                                                                                                                                                | —                                                                                                                      |
| **Major Catholic Feasts**                 | —                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Christmas, Epiphany, Easter, Ascension, Pentecost, Assumption, All Saints, Immaculate Conception, Mary Mother of God, Corpus Christi, and others | —                                                                                                                      |
| **Common Protestant Festivals**           | —                                                                                                                                                                                                       | —                                                                                                                                                | Christmas, Easter, Good Friday, Palm Sunday, Pentecost, Ascension, Advent; some observe Reformation Day and All Saints |
| **Largest Historic / Demographic Center** | Large populations in Russia, Greece, Romania, Serbia, etc.                                                                                                                                              | Large populations in Brazil, Mexico, Philippines, Italy, etc.                                                                                    | Large populations in USA, parts of Europe, Africa, South Korea, etc.                                                   |
| **Traditional Liturgical Languages**      | Greek, Church Slavonic, Arabic and local languages                                                                                                                                                      | Latin historically; now local languages are widely used                                                                                          | Mostly local languages                                                                                                 |
| **Worship Style**                         | Highly liturgical, incense, icons, chanting                                                                                                                                                             | Liturgical Mass; style can range from traditional to modern                                                                                      | Highly varied: liturgical, evangelical, charismatic, contemporary, etc.                                                |
| **Church Authority Basis**                | Scripture + Holy Tradition + Ecumenical Councils                                                                                                                                                        | Scripture + Sacred Tradition + Magisterium                                                                                                       | Usually emphasizes Scripture as the highest authority; interpretation varies                                           |
