## Basic Introduction

Jesus Christ is one of the most important religious figures in world history.

In Christianity, Jesus is believed to be the Son of God, the Messiah, and the central figure of Christian faith.

In Islam, Jesus is known as **Prophet Isa** and is respected as one of the great prophets of Allah.

Jesus lived in the region of **Judea and Galilee** during the time of **Roman rule**.

His life and teachings later became the foundation of Christianity.

## Names

| Tradition                   | Name             |
| --------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Christianity                | Jesus Christ     |
| Islam                       | Prophet Isa      |
| Hebrew / Aramaic background | Yeshua           |
| Title                       | Christ / Messiah |

## Meaning of Christ

The word **Christ** comes from the Greek word **Christos**, meaning “Anointed One.”

It is similar to the Hebrew word **Messiah**, meaning a chosen or anointed savior/king.

So:

Jesus Christ means **Jesus the Messiah**.

## Historical Background

At the time of Jesus, the Jewish people were living under Roman control.

Jerusalem was the religious center of the Jews because the Second Temple was located there.

The region was politically controlled by Rome and local rulers such as Herod and his successors.

## Important Regions

| Region       | Importance                                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Judea        | Region around Jerusalem                                |
| Galilee      | Northern region where Jesus spent much of his ministry |
| Bethlehem    | Traditional birthplace of Jesus                        |
| Nazareth     | Town where Jesus grew up                               |
| Jerusalem    | Religious center of Jews                               |
| Jordan River | Place connected with baptism by John the Baptist       |

# Birth of Jesus

According to Christian tradition, Jesus was born in **Bethlehem**.

His birth is generally placed around **4 BCE**.

There is no year 0 in the BCE/CE calendar. The calendar moves from **1 BCE** directly to **1 CE**.

Jesus was born into a **Jewish family**.

His mother was **Mary**.

In Islam, Mary is known as **Maryam**.

Christian tradition teaches that Jesus was born miraculously through the Virgin Mary.

Islam also accepts the miraculous birth of Prophet Isa without a father.

## Important Point

| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| Mary / Maryam | Mother of Jesus / Isa |
| Joseph | Husband of Mary in Christian tradition |
| Jesus / Isa | Central figure of Christianity; prophet in Islam |
| Bethlehem | Traditional birthplace of Jesus |

# Mary / Maryam

Mary is one of the most respected women in Christianity and Islam.

In Christianity, she is known as the Virgin Mary.

In Islam, she is known as Maryam and is highly honored in the Qur’an.

The Qur’an has a chapter named **Surah Maryam**.

## Importance

| Tradition    | Importance of Mary                                   |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Christianity | Mother of Jesus Christ                               |
| Islam        | Mother of Prophet Isa; one of the most honored women |

# Childhood of Jesus

Jesus grew up in **Nazareth**, a town in Galilee.

Because of this, he is often called **Jesus of Nazareth**.

Jesus was born and raised in a Jewish religious environment. 

According to Christian tradition, when Jesus was a child, he was taken to the **Second Temple in Jerusalem**.

One famous childhood event says that Jesus was found in the Temple discussing religious matters with teachers.

Not many details about his childhood are given in the Gospels.

Christian tradition mentions that Jesus showed wisdom from a young age.

## Important Point

Bethlehem is connected with the birth of Jesus.

Nazareth is connected with the upbringing of Jesus.

Jerusalem and the Second Temple are connected with the Jewish religious background of Jesus.
# Roman Rule and Jewish Society

During the time of Jesus, Judea was under Roman control.

The Jews were waiting for a Messiah who would save them.

Different Jewish groups existed at that time.

## Important Jewish Groups

| Group                 | Basic Meaning                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Pharisees<br>(ફરોશીઓ) | Jewish religious group focused on law and tradition          |
| Sadducees             | Priestly aristocratic group connected with the Temple        |
| Essenes               | Strict religious group, often linked with desert communities |
| Zealots               | Anti-Roman nationalist group                                 |
| Common Jews           | Ordinary people waiting for religious and social hope        |

## Importance for Understanding Jesus

Jesus preached in a society affected by:
- Roman political control
- Jewish religious expectations
- Poverty and social inequality
- Hope for the Messiah
- Temple-centered worship

# John the Baptist

Before Jesus began his public ministry, John the Baptist preached repentance.

He baptized people in the Jordan River.

John called people to leave sin and prepare for the coming of God’s kingdom.

In Christianity, John the Baptist is seen as the forerunner(પુરોગામી) of Jesus.

In Islam, John is known as **Prophet Yahya**.

## Important Point

| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| John the Baptist / Yahya | Prophet who preached repentance and baptized people |
| Jordan River | Place where John baptized people |

# Baptism of Jesus

According to Christian tradition, Jesus came to John the Baptist and was baptized in the Jordan River.

This event marks the beginning of Jesus’ public ministry.

After baptism, Jesus began preaching and teaching publicly.

## Importance

The baptism of Jesus is important because:
- It marks the beginning of his public mission
- It connects Jesus with John the Baptist
- It shows the theme of repentance and divine callinge

# Temptation in the Wilderness

After his baptism, Jesus went into the wilderness.

According to Christian tradition, he fasted for forty days and was tempted by Satan.

Jesus rejected temptation and remained faithful to God.

## Main Lesson

This event shows:
- Spiritual strength
- Control over desire
- Faith in God
- Rejection of worldly power and pride
# Beginning of Jesus’ Ministry in Galilee

Jesus began preaching mainly in Galilee.

His message focused on repentance, faith, mercy, and the Kingdom of God.

He taught that people should turn away from sin and live a righteous life.

## Core Message

Jesus preached:
- Repentance
- Love of God
- Love of neighbor
- Mercy and forgiveness
- Humility
- Justice
- Kingdom of God
  
# Calling of the Disciples

Jesus gathered disciples to follow him and spread his message.

The most important group is known as the **Twelve Apostles(પ્રેરિતો,ધર્મદૂત)**.

They learned from Jesus and later became early leaders of Christianity.

## Important Disciples

| Disciple       | Importance                                            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Peter          | One of the leading disciples                          |
| Andrew         | Brother of Peter                                      |
| James          | One of the close disciples                            |
| John           | One of the close disciples                            |
| Matthew        | Tax collector who became disciple                     |
| Judas Iscariot | Later betrayed Jesus according to Christian tradition |

## Meaning of Apostle

An apostle means a messenger or one who is sent with a mission.

# Teachings of Jesus

Jesus taught through sermons, parables, and examples.

His teachings focused on inner purity, compassion, forgiveness, and sincere worship.

## Main Teachings

| Teaching | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Love God | God should be loved with full heart and devotion |
| Love your neighbor | People should care for others |
| Forgiveness | Forgive those who wrong you |
| Mercy | Be kind to the weak and suffering |
| Humility | Avoid pride |
| Charity | Help the poor and needy |
| Inner purity | Goodness should come from the heart |
| Faith | Trust in God |
| Repentance | Turn away from sin |

# Sermon on the Mount

The Sermon on the Mount is one of the most famous teachings of Jesus in Christian tradition.

It includes moral and spiritual teachings.
## Important Teachings

Jesus taught:
- Blessed are the poor in spirit
- Blessed are the merciful
- Blessed are the peacemakers
- Love your enemies
- Do not be hypocritical in worship
- Give charity sincerely
- Pray with sincerity
- Do not be ruled by greed
- Build life on strong moral foundation
## Importance

The Sermon on the Mount shows the ethical and spiritual message of Jesus.

# Parables of Jesus

Jesus often taught through short stories called **parables**.

A parable is a simple story with a moral or spiritual lesson.

## Famous Parables

| Parable | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Good Samaritan | Help others beyond social and religious boundaries |
| Prodigal Son | God’s mercy and forgiveness |
| Lost Sheep | God cares for those who are lost |
| Sower | People receive truth in different ways |
| Mustard Seed | Small faith can grow greatly |
| Wise and Foolish Builders | Build life on strong moral foundation |

## Importance

Parables made difficult spiritual ideas easy for common people to understand.


# Miracles of Jesus

According to Christian tradition, Jesus performed many miracles.

In Islam also, Prophet Isa performed miracles by the permission of Allah.

## Important Miracles

| Miracle                   | Meaning                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Healing the sick          | Jesus healed people with diseases           |
| Giving sight to the blind | Symbol of mercy and divine power            |
| Feeding the multitudes    | Feeding many people with little food        |

In Islam, Prophet Isa performed miracles by Allah’s permission, not by independent divine power.

In Christianity, miracles show the divine authority and mission of Jesus.

# Jesus and the Poor

Jesus gave special attention to the poor, sick, weak, and rejected people.

He criticized pride, hypocrisy, greed, and injustice.

He taught that true religion must include mercy and compassion.

## Important Message

Jesus taught that helping the poor and forgiving others are signs of true faith.

# Conflict with Religious Authorities

As Jesus became popular, some religious authorities became worried.

They questioned his teachings, actions, and authority.

Jesus criticized hypocrisy and empty ritualism.

He taught that religion should be based on sincerity, mercy, justice, and love of God.

## Reason for Conflict

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Popularity | Large crowds followed Jesus |
| Interpretation of law | Jesus challenged strict or hypocritical interpretations |
| Temple authority | Religious leaders feared loss of influence |
| Messiah expectation | Some people saw Jesus as the Messiah |

# Kingdom of God

One of the central themes of Jesus’ teaching was the **Kingdom of God**.

It does not simply mean a political kingdom.

It means God’s rule over human life, society, and the heart.

## Meaning

The Kingdom of God is connected with:
- Justice
- Mercy
- Faith
- Repentance
- Righteousness
- God’s authority
- Spiritual transformation

## Important Point

Many Jews expected a political Messiah who would defeat Rome.

Jesus emphasized(ભાર મૂક્યો) spiritual transformation and God’s moral rule.

# Jesus as Messiah

In Jewish tradition, Messiah means an anointed or chosen one.

Many Jews expected the Messiah to restore Israel and bring justice.

In Christianity, Jesus is believed to be the Messiah.

The title **Christ** means Messiah.

## Important Difference

| Tradition    | View                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Christianity | Jesus is the Messiah and Son of God               |
| Islam        | Jesus is Prophet Isa, Messiah, but not Son of God |
| Judaism      | Jesus is not accepted as the Messiah              |

# Journey Toward Jerusalem

After preaching in Galilee and nearby regions, Jesus moved toward Jerusalem.

Jerusalem was the religious center of Judaism and the location of the Second Temple.

Jesus knew that going to Jerusalem would create conflict with powerful religious and political authorities.

Before reaching Jerusalem, his fame had already spread because of his teachings, miracles, and growing followers.

## Importance of Jerusalem

Jerusalem was important because:
- It contained the Second Temple
- It was the center of Jewish worship
- It was connected with kings David and Solomon
- It had religious and political importance
- It was under Roman control

# Jesus and Bani Israel

Jesus was born among the Jewish people.

He belonged to the Israelite religious tradition.

In Islam, Prophet Isa was sent to Bani Israel.

His message continued the tradition of earlier prophets such as:
- Prophet Ibrahim
- Prophet Ishaq
- Prophet Yaqub
- Prophet Musa
- Prophet Dawud
- Prophet Sulaiman
- Prophet Zakariya
- Prophet Yahya

## Simple Chain

Prophet Ibrahim → Prophet Ishaq → Prophet Yaqub / Israel → Bani Israel → Prophet Musa → Prophet Dawud → Prophet Sulaiman → Prophet Zakariya → Prophet Yahya → Prophet Isa

# Short Summary

Jesus Christ, known as Prophet Isa in Islam, was born in Bethlehem and grew up in Nazareth. He lived during Roman rule in Judea and Galilee. Before beginning his public ministry, John the Baptist preached repentance and baptized Jesus in the Jordan River. Jesus then preached mainly in Galilee, teaching about repentance, mercy, forgiveness, love of God, love of neighbor, and the Kingdom of God. He called disciples, taught through parables, performed miracles, helped the poor and sick, and criticized hypocrisy. His popularity created tension with some religious authorities. Before reaching Jerusalem, Jesus had already become known as a teacher, healer, and spiritual leader.
