
Baptism is a **Christian religious ceremony using water** that marks a person’s entry into the Christian faith.

**“A person is washed with water as a sign of new life, cleansing from sin, and belonging to Christ.”**

It is based on Jesus’ command to baptize people, and Jesus himself was baptized by John the Baptist.

Different churches practice it differently:

- **Catholic / Orthodox churches:** usually baptize infants as well as adults.
- **Many Protestant churches:** often baptize people after they personally declare faith in Jesus.
- Some churches pour water on the head.
- Some sprinkle water.
- Some fully immerse the person in water.

Baptism is **not the same as Holy Water**. Baptism is the whole sacrament/ceremony. Water is used during baptism, and in some traditions that water may be blessed.

The main ideas connected with baptism are:

- cleansing from sin
- beginning a new spiritual life
- becoming part of the Christian community
- following Jesus
