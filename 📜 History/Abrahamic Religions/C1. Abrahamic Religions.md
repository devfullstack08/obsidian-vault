## Meaning

Abrahamic religions are religions that trace their spiritual history to Prophet Ibrahim / Abraham.

The three main Abrahamic religions are:

| Religion     | Important Figure  | Main Scripture |
| ------------ | ----------------- | -------------- |
| Judaism      | Abraham, Moses    | Torah / Tanakh |
| Christianity | Abraham, Jesus    | Bible          |
| Islam        | Ibrahim, Muhammad | Qur’an         |

## Why Called Abrahamic?

They are called Abrahamic because all three religions respect Prophet Ibrahim / Abraham as an important figure.

## Common Beliefs

| Belief     | Meaning                                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| One God    | Belief in one supreme God                 |
| Prophets   | God sends prophets/messengers             |
| Revelation | God gives guidance through scripture      |
| Moral Law  | Humans should follow divine guidance      |
| Judgment   | Human actions have spiritual consequences |

## Important Difference

| Religion | View |
|---|---|
| Judaism | Follows the Israelite/Jewish tradition |
| Christianity | Centers on Jesus Christ |
| Islam | Centers on final revelation through Prophet Muhammad |
