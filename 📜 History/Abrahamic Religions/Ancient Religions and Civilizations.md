# Tradition of Prophets & Messengers

## Prophet / Nabi

A Prophet, or Nabi, is a person chosen by God to guide people.

### Main points
- Shows ideal conduct and moral life
- Spreads God’s message
- Guides people toward truth and righteousness
- May continue the teaching of an earlier messenger

## Messenger / Rasul / Paygambar

A Messenger, or Rasul, is a prophet who receives a special mission from God and delivers it to people.

### Main points
- Receives divine revelation from God, often through angels
- Brings God’s message to people
- May bring a new scripture, law, or religious system
- Often creates major religious or social change

## Difference

| Prophet / Nabi                               | Messenger / Rasul                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Chosen by God to guide people                | Chosen by God to deliver a specific divine message |
| Teaches morality and right conduct           | Brings a special mission, scripture, or law        |
| May follow earlier revelation                | May bring new revelation or reform                 |
| Every prophet is not necessarily a messenger | Every messenger is also a prophet                  |
