
The Bible is divided into two main parts:

| Part | Main Tradition | Main Language |
|---|---|---|
| Old Testament | Judaism and Christianity | Mainly Hebrew, with some Aramaic |
| New Testament | Christianity | Greek |

## Old Testament / Hebrew Bible

In Judaism, the Hebrew Bible is called the **Tanakh**.

The Tanakh has **24 books** in Jewish tradition.

The word **Tanakh** comes from three parts:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Torah | Law / Teaching |
| Nevi’im | Prophets |
| Ketuvim | Writings |

## Torah

The first five books of the Tanakh are called the **Torah**.

In tradition, the Torah is connected with **Prophet Musa / Moses**.

The five books of the Torah are:

| No. | Book | Meaning / Main Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Genesis | Creation, Adam, Noah, Ibrahim/Abraham, Ishaq/Isaac, Yaqub/Jacob, Yusuf/Joseph |
| 2 | Exodus | Story of Prophet Musa/Moses, Pharaoh, Exodus from Egypt, Ten Commandments |
| 3 | Leviticus | Religious laws and priestly rules |
| 4 | Numbers | Journey of Israelites in the wilderness |
| 5 | Deuteronomy | Final speeches and laws of Moses |

## Book of Exodus

The **Book of Exodus** is the second book of the Torah.

It contains the major story of Prophet Musa / Moses, including:
- Birth of Musa
- Pharaoh’s oppression
- Musa leaving Egypt
- Revelation at Mount Sinai / Horeb
- Confrontation with Pharaoh
- Plagues
- Exodus from Egypt
- Crossing of the sea
- Ten Commandments

## New Testament

The New Testament is the second main part of the Christian Bible.

It was written mainly in **Greek**.

It focuses on:
- Life and teachings of Jesus / Prophet Isa
- Early Christian community
- Letters of apostles
- Book of Revelation
