**Picasso** means **Pablo Picasso**, a very famous **Spanish painter and artist**.

- **Pablo Picasso** was a **Spanish painter and sculptor(શિલ્પકાર)**.
- He was one of the most important artists of the **20th century**.
- He is strongly associated with **Cubism**.
- He developed Cubism with **Georges Braque**.
- Famous works include **Guernica** and **Les Demoiselles d’Avignon**.
- **Guernica** is a famous anti-war painting connected with the bombing of the Spanish town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War.
