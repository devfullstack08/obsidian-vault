After Hitler came to power in Germany in 1933, many Jews, intellectuals, scientists, artists, and political opponents fled the country because of Nazi persecution and anti-Semitism. They were known as refugees, exiles, or émigrés.

| Person              | Why important                                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Albert Einstein** | Jewish scientist; left Germany and settled in the USA    |
| **Hannah Arendt**   | Jewish political thinker; fled Nazi persecution          |
| **Sigmund Freud**   | Jewish psychoanalyst; left Austria after Nazi annexation |
| **Thomas Mann**     | German writer; opposed Nazism and went into exile        |

**Auschwitz** also known as **Oświęcim**, was a complex of over 40 [concentration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_concentration_camps "Nazi concentration camps") and [extermination camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extermination_camp "Extermination camp") operated by [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany "Nazi Germany") in [occupied Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_areas_annexed_by_Nazi_Germany "Polish areas annexed by Nazi Germany") (in a portion annexed into Germany in 1939) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II") and [the Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust "The Holocaust"). It consisted of [Auschwitz I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp#Auschwitz_I), the main camp (_Stammlager_) in [Oświęcim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O%C5%9Bwi%C4%99cim "Oświęcim"); [Auschwitz II-Birkenau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp#Auschwitz_II-Birkenau), a concentration and extermination camp with [gas chambers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_chamber "Gas chamber"), [Auschwitz III-Monowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp#Auschwitz_III), a [labour camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbeitslager "Arbeitslager") for the chemical conglomerate [IG Farben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IG_Farben "IG Farben"), and [dozens of subcamps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_subcamps_of_Auschwitz "List of subcamps of Auschwitz"
