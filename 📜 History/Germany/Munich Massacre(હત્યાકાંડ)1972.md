The 1972 Olympics were held in **Munich, West Germany**. Germany wanted to show the world a peaceful and modern image after the dark history of Nazi Germany and World War II. Because of that, Olympic security was kept quite relaxed.

On **5 September 1972**, eight members of the Palestinian militant group **Black September** entered the Olympic Village. They went to the building where the **Israeli team** was staying. They first killed **two Israeli team members** and then took **nine others hostage**. Their demand was the release of Palestinian prisoners held in Israel.

The hostage crisis continued for many hours. The whole world was watching the Olympics, so the attack became a huge international shock. West German police tried to plan a rescue operation, but they were not properly prepared for such a terrorist hostage crisis.

Later, the terrorists and hostages were taken to **Fürstenfeldbruck airbase**, because the attackers wanted to fly out of Germany. German police attempted a rescue there, but the operation failed badly. During the shootout and explosions, the **nine remaining Israeli hostages were killed**. A **West German police officer** and **five attackers** were also killed.

## Israel’s response

After 11 Israeli Olympic team members were murdered in Munich, Israel decided to punish the people connected with the attack.
The response mainly had two parts:
## 1. Operation Wrath of God

Israel’s intelligence agency **Mossad** started a secret operation called:

```
Operation Wrath of God
```

Its aim was to find and kill people believed to be connected with **Black September** and the planning of the Munich attack.

This operation continued for many years. Israeli agents tracked suspects in different countries in Europe and the Middle East.

## 2. Operation Spring of Youth

In **1973**, Israel also carried out a military operation in **Beirut, Lebanon**, called:

```
Operation Spring of Youth
```

Israeli commandos attacked targets linked to Palestinian militant groups. Some important Palestinian leaders were killed in that raid.

## But one mistake happened

In **1973**, Mossad agents killed a man in **Lillehammer, Norway**, thinking he was a senior Black September leader.

But later it was found that he was the wrong person.

This incident became known as:

```
Lillehammer Affair
```

It damaged Israel’s image internationally.
