### Born (foam-born)
- (1) In Homer's Iliad, she is the daughter of **Zeus** and **Titaness Dione**. In Hesiod's Theogony she is born from the foam (ἀφρός, aphrós) produced by **Uranus's genitals**, (land in Cyprus)
- (2) Before the reign of zeus, the universe was rules by the primordial(આદિકાલીન) sky god, **Uranus**, whose tyranny(અત્યાચાર) over his own children eventually provoked a rebellion.
- Encourages by **Gaia**, the primordial goddess of the earth,  the titan Cronus ambushed(હુમલો કર્યો) his father and severed his genitals with as adamantine sickle. As they fell into the sea, the blood and divine essence of Uranus mixed with the waves, causing a brilliant white foam to gather up on the surface. Form that foam emerged Aphrodite Rather than being born as an infant(શિશુ), she appeared full grown, radiant goddess of extraordinary beauty rising from the sea itself. The winds carried her across the water until she reached the island of Cythera before continuing to Cyprus, where she stepped ashore. 
- According to the myth, flowers bloomed beneath her feet whenever she walked. she was welcomed with open arms. They cloth her in magnificent garments. They adorned her with gold and Jewelry and escorted her to join the immortal gods upon Mount Olympus.  
- Aphros : meaning foam or foaming of sperm.
