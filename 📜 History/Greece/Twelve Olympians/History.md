According to Greek mythology:
- Cronus ruled over the earlier generation of divine beings known as the Titans.
- His son Zeus defeated the Titans in a great conflict called the **Titanomachy**.
- Zeus became the supreme ruler of the Olympian gods.
- Hera, who was both Zeus's sister and wife, became the queen of Olympus.
