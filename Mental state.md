### What is the most dangerous mental state?
Rather than a specific diagnosis, one of the most urgent states is an acute suicidal crisis, particularly when someone:

- Feels completely hopeless or trapped.
    
- Believes others would be better off without them.
    
- Has persistent thoughts about dying.
    
- Has started planning suicide or preparing to act.
    
- Suddenly withdraws from everyone or gives away important possessions.

These are warning signs, not proof that someone will attempt suicide. But they should always be taken seriously.
