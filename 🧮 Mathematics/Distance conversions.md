
Here are the main distance conversions to remember:

- **1 kilometre (km) = 1,000 metres (m)**
- **1 metre = 100 centimetres (cm)**
- **1 centimetre = 10 millimetres (mm)**
- **1 kilometre ≈ 0.621 mile**
- **1 mile = 1,760 yards(વાર)**

Easy memory:

**km → m → cm → mm**  
**1 km = 1,000 m = 1,00,000 cm = 10,00,000 mm**

For miles, just remember:

**1 mile ≈ 1.6 km**  
**1 NM(nautical mile/ દરિયાઈ માઈલ) ≈ 1.85 km**.
So **5 miles ≈ 8 km**, and **10 miles ≈ 16 km**.

**Foot** is singular; **feet** is plural.
- **1 foot = 12 inches**
- **2 feet = 24 inches**
- **5 feet = 60 inches**
**1 yard = 3 feet**
