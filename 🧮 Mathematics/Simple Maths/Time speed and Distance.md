# Time, Speed & Distance

This topic is highly conceptual. It covers basic motion, relative speed, late-early problems, and train problems.

---

## 1. Core Formulas & Conversions

* **Basic Equation:**
  $$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$
* **Unit Conversions:**
  * **km/h to m/s:** Multiply by **$\frac{5}{18}$**
    $$90\text{ km/h} = 90 \times \frac{5}{18} = 25\text{ m/s}$$
  * **m/s to km/h:** Multiply by **$\frac{18}{5}$**
    $$20\text{ m/s} = 20 \times \frac{18}{5} = 72\text{ km/h}$$

---

## 2. Speed-Time-Distance Relationships

If one parameter is kept constant, the relationship between the other two changes:
1. **If Distance is constant:** Speed is inversely proportional to Time ($S \propto 1/T$).
   * If the ratio of speeds is $a:b$, the ratio of times taken is **$b:a$**. (Crucial for shortcuts!)
2. **If Time is constant:** Distance is directly proportional to Speed ($D \propto S$).
3. **If Speed is constant:** Distance is directly proportional to Time ($D \propto T$).

---

## 3. Key Concepts & Shortcuts

### A. Average Speed
* **General Formula:**
  $$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}$$
* **Shortcut (Two equal halves):** If a body travels equal distances at speeds $x$ and $y$, the average speed is:
  $$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x+y}$$

---

### B. Relative Speed
Relative speed is the speed of a moving body with respect to another.
* **Same Direction:** Speeds are subtracted.
  $$\text{Relative Speed} = |S_1 - S_2|$$
* **Opposite Direction:** Speeds are added.
  $$\text{Relative Speed} = S_1 + S_2$$

---

### C. The "Late-Early" Formula
Used when a person changes their speed and arrives late or early at their destination.

$$\text{Distance} = \frac{\text{Product of Speeds}}{\text{Difference of Speeds}} \times \text{Difference in Time}$$
$$\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \times S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \Delta T$$

> *Note on $\Delta T$:*
> * If one is Late and the other is Early $\rightarrow$ Add the times ($T_{\text{late}} + T_{\text{early}}$).
> * If both are Late, or both are Early $\rightarrow$ Subtract the times ($|T_1 - T_2|$).

---

### D. Trains Crossing Objects
* **Crossing a Point Object (Pole, Standing Man, Tree):** The distance covered is equal to the length of the train ($L_T$).
  $$\text{Time} = \frac{L_T}{\text{Speed}}$$
* **Crossing a Platform, Bridge, or Tunnel:** The distance covered is the sum of the length of the train ($L_T$) and the object ($L_O$).
  $$\text{Time} = \frac{L_T + L_O}{\text{Speed}}$$

---

## 4. Solved Practice Questions

### Question 1 (Late-Early Concept - SSC CGL / UPSC CSAT)
If a student walks from his house to school at $5\text{ km/h}$, he is late by $10\text{ minutes}$. If he walks at $6\text{ km/h}$, he reaches $15\text{ minutes}$ early. Find the distance to the school.
* **Solution:**
  * Speeds: $S_1 = 5$, $S_2 = 6$
  * Difference in time ($\Delta T$): 10 min late + 15 min early $= 25\text{ minutes}$.
  * Convert $\Delta T$ to hours: $\frac{25}{60} = \frac{5}{12}\text{ hours}$.
  * Using the formula:
    $$\text{Distance} = \frac{5 \times 6}{|6 - 5|} \times \frac{5}{12} = \frac{30}{1} \times \frac{5}{12} = \frac{150}{12} = \mathbf{12.5\text{ km}}$$
  * **Answer:** **$12.5\text{ km}$**.

### Question 2 (Trains Crossing Platform)
A train $150\text{ meters}$ long takes $20\text{ seconds}$ to cross a platform $250\text{ meters}$ long. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
* **Solution:**
  * Total Distance $= \text{Length of train} + \text{Length of platform} = 150 + 250 = 400\text{ meters}$.
  * Time taken $= 20\text{ seconds}$.
  * $\text{Speed (m/s)} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{400}{20} = 20\text{ m/s}$.
  * Convert to km/h:
    $$\text{Speed} = 20 \times \frac{18}{5} = 4 \times 18 = \mathbf{72\text{ km/h}}$$
