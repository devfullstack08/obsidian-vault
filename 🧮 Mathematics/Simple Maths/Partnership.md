# Partnership (ભાગીદારી)

Partnership is a direct extension of the **Ratio & Proportion** concept. It involves individuals investing capital for different durations and sharing profits accordingly.

---

## 1. Core Rule of Partnership

The profit distributed among partners depends on two factors:
1. The **Capital** invested.
2. The **Time** duration for which the capital was invested.

$$\text{Profit} \propto \text{Capital} \times \text{Time}$$
$$\text{Profit Ratio} = (\text{Capital}_1 \times \text{Time}_1) : (\text{Capital}_2 \times \text{Time}_2)$$

If partner A invests $C_A$ for time $T_A$ and partner B invests $C_B$ for time $T_B$, their profits $P_A$ and $P_B$ will be shared in the ratio:

$$\frac{P_A}{P_B} = \frac{C_A \times T_A}{C_B \times T_B}$$

---

## 2. Types of Partnerships

1. **Simple Partnership:**
   * All partners invest their capital for the **same time period**.
   * The profit is shared simply in the ratio of their capitals.
   * $\text{Profit Ratio} = C_A : C_B : C_C$
2. **Compound Partnership:**
   * Partners invest capital for **different time periods**.
   * The profit is shared in the ratio of the products of their capitals and time periods.
   * $\text{Profit Ratio} = (C_A \times T_A) : (C_B \times T_B) : (C_C \times T_C)$

---

## 3. Special Exam Cases: Working vs. Sleeping Partner

* **Working Partner:** A partner who manages the business. They often receive a specific percentage of the total profit as a "salary" or commission before the rest of the profit is divided in the investment-time ratio.
* **Sleeping Partner:** A partner who only invests capital and does not manage the business. They only get their share of the profit.

---

## 4. Solved Practice Questions

### Question 1 (Compound Partnership - SSC CGL)
A, B, and C enter into a partnership. A invests ₹$20,000$ for $12\text{ months}$, B invests ₹$30,000$ for $8\text{ months}$, and C invests ₹$40,000$ for $6\text{ months}$. Find their share in a total profit of ₹$24,000$.
* **Solution:**
  * Find the ratio of their profits using: $C \times T$
    * Profit of A $= 20,000 \times 12 = 2,40,000$
    * Profit of B $= 30,000 \times 8 = 2,40,000$
    * Profit of C $= 40,000 \times 6 = 2,40,000$
  * Ratio of Profit $= 1 : 1 : 1$.
  * Since the ratio is equal, each partner gets an equal share:
    * Share of each partner $= \frac{24,000}{3} = \mathbf{₹8000}$.
  * **Answer:** Each partner receives **₹8000**.

### Question 2 (Working Partner Case - UPSC CSAT)
A and B start a business with investments of ₹$10,000$ and ₹$15,000$ respectively. A is a working partner and receives $10\%$ of the profit as salary. The remaining profit is distributed in the ratio of their investments. If the total profit at the end of the year is ₹$5000$, find B's share.
* **Solution:**
  * Total Profit $= \text{₹}5000$.
  * A's salary $= 10\%$ of $5000 = \text{₹}500$.
  * Remaining Profit for distribution $= 5000 - 500 = \text{₹}4500$.
  * Investment Ratio A:B $= 10,000 : 15,000 = 2 : 3$.
  * Share of B in remaining profit:
    $$\text{B's share} = 4500 \times \frac{3}{2 + 3} = 4500 \times \frac{3}{5} = \mathbf{₹2700}$$
  * **Answer:** B receives **₹2700**.
