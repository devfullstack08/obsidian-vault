# Profit & Loss (लाभ અને હાનિ)

Profit & Loss is one of the most frequently asked topics in exams. It directly extends the concepts of **Percentage**.

---

## 1. Core Terms & Formulas

* **Cost Price (CP):** The price at which an article is purchased.
* **Selling Price (SP):** The price at which an article is sold.
* **Profit (P):** Occurs when $SP > CP$.
  $$Profit = SP - CP$$
  $$Profit\% = \left( \frac{Profit}{CP} \times 100 \right)\%$$
* **Loss (L):** Occurs when $CP > SP$.
  $$Loss = CP - SP$$
  $$Loss\% = \left( \frac{Loss}{CP} \times 100 \right)\%$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Profit percentage and Loss percentage are **always calculated on the Cost Price (CP)**, unless the question specifically states it is calculated on the Selling Price.

* **Direct SP and CP Formulas:**
  $$SP = CP \times \left( \frac{100 + Profit\%}{100} \right) \quad \text{or} \quad SP = CP \times \left( \frac{100 - Loss\%}{100} \right)$$
  $$CP = SP \times \left( \frac{100}{100 + Profit\%} \right) \quad \text{or} \quad CP = SP \times \left( \frac{100}{100 - Loss\%} \right)$$

## 2. Exam Shortcuts & Tricks

### A. Same Selling Price ($SP$) with Equal Profit & Loss%
When two items are sold at the **same selling price ($SP_1 = SP_2$)**, one at a profit of $x\%$ and the other at a loss of $x\%$:
* There is **always a net loss** in the overall transaction.
* The overall loss percentage is:
  $$\text{Net Loss\%} = \left( \frac{x}{10} \right)^2 = \frac{x^2}{100}\%$$

> **Example:** A man sells two houses at the same price. On one he gains $20\%$, and on the other he loses $20\%$. Find his overall gain or loss.
> * Since SP is same and profit/loss % is equal ($x = 20$):
> * Net Loss $\% = \frac{20^2}{100} = \frac{400}{100} = \mathbf{4\%}$.
> * **Answer:** Overall loss of **$4\%$**.

---

### B. Dishonest Dealer / Faulty Weights
A dishonest merchant claims to sell their goods at cost price but uses a false (lighter) weight.
* Formula for profit percentage:
  $$Profit\% = \left( \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \right) \times 100\%$$
  $$\text{OR}$$
  $$Profit\% = \left( \frac{\text{True Weight} - \text{False Weight}}{\text{False Weight}} \right) \times 100\%$$

> **Example:** A shopkeeper sells sugar at cost price but uses a weight of $900\text{ gm}$ instead of $1\text{ kg}$. Find his profit percentage.
> * True Weight $= 1000\text{ gm}$
> * False Weight $= 900\text{ gm}$
> * Error $= 100\text{ gm}$
> * Profit $\% = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = \frac{1}{9} \times 100 = \mathbf{11.11\%}$.

---

### C. The "Cross-Multiplication" Quantity Trick
Used when items are bought at a certain rate and sold at a different rate.
* **Rule:** If a person buys $m$ articles for ₹$n$ and sells $p$ articles for ₹$q$:
  1. Write columns for **Quantity** and **Price**.
  2. Cross-multiply:
     $$\text{CP} = p \times n \quad \text{and} \quad \text{SP} = m \times q$$
  3. Calculate profit/loss using the new CP and SP.

> **Example:** A boy buys lemons at $5$ for ₹$4$ and sells them at $4$ for ₹$5$. Find his profit or loss percentage.
> * Let's align:
>   * Buy (CP): Quantity $= 5$, Price $= 4$
>   * Sell (SP): Quantity $= 4$, Price $= 5$
> * Cross-multiply:
>   * $\text{CP} = 4 \times 4 = 16$
>   * $\text{SP} = 5 \times 5 = 25$
> * Since $\text{SP} > \text{CP}$, it is a profit.
> * $Profit = 25 - 16 = 9$
> * $Profit\% = \frac{9}{16} \times 100 = \mathbf{56.25\%}$.

## 3. Solved Practice Questions

### Question 1 (Cost Price of multiple items - SSC CGL)
By selling 33 meters of cloth, a merchant gains the selling price of 11 meters. Find his gain percentage.
* **Solution:**
  * Let the SP of 1 meter of cloth be ₹1.
  * SP of 33 meters $= \text{₹}33$.
  * Gain $= \text{SP of 11 meters} = \text{₹}11$.
  * Recall: $CP = SP - Gain = 33 - 11 = \text{₹}22$.
  * $Gain\% = \frac{11}{22} \times 100 = \frac{1}{2} \times 100 = \mathbf{50\%}$.
