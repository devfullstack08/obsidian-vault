# Percentage (प्रतिशत)

Percentage is the backbone of Quantitative Aptitude. Mastery over percentages allows you to quickly solve questions in Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Data Interpretation, and Mixture & Alligation.

---

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **Definition:** Percentage means "per hundred." A fraction with a denominator of 100 is called a percentage.
  $$x\% = \frac{x}{100}$$
* **Percentage to Fraction:** Divide by 100.
  $$40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{2}{5}$$
* **Fraction to Percentage:** Multiply by 100.
  $$\frac{3}{8} = \frac{3}{8} \times 100 = 37.5\%$$

## 3. Exam Shortcuts & Tricks

### A. The "AB Rule" (Product Stability / Price-Consumption)
Used when the product of two variables remains constant ($Price \times Consumption = Expenditure$).
* **Rule:** If variable $A$ increases by a fraction of $\frac{a}{b}$, then variable $B$ must decrease by **$\frac{a}{a+b}$** to keep the product constant.
* If variable $A$ decreases by a fraction of $\frac{a}{b}$, then variable $B$ must increase by **$\frac{a}{b-a}$**.

> **Example:** If the price of petrol increases by $25\%$ ($\frac{1}{4}$), by how much percentage must a car owner reduce their consumption so that their expenditure remains the same?
> * Increase: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{1}{4}$
> * Required Decrease: $\frac{a}{a+b} = \frac{1}{1+4} = \frac{1}{5} = \mathbf{20\%}$.
> * **Answer:** Reduce consumption by **$20\%$**. (Solved in 3 seconds!)

---

### B. Successive Percentage Change
If a value is changed by $x\%$ and then by $y\%$, the net percentage change is:

$$\text{Net Change} = \left( x + y + \frac{xy}{100} \right)\%$$

* *Note:* Use a **positive (+)** sign for increase/profit, and a **negative (-)** sign for decrease/loss/discount.

> **Example:** The price of a watch is increased by $20\%$ and later decreased by $10\%$. Find the net change in price.
> * Here, $x = +20$ and $y = -10$.
> * Net Change $= 20 - 10 + \frac{20 \times (-10)}{100} = 10 - 2 = \mathbf{+8\%}$ (Net increase of 8%).

---

### C. Base Change Concept
* If $A$ is $x\%$ more than $B$, then $B$ is less than $A$ by:
  $$\left( \frac{x}{100 + x} \times 100 \right)\%$$
* If $A$ is $x\%$ less than $B$, then $B$ is more than $A$ by:
  $$\left( \frac{x}{100 - x} \times 100 \right)\%$$

## 4. Solved Practice Questions (Exam Level)

### Question 1 (Election-based - UPSC/SSC CGL)
In an election between two candidates, the winner secured $60\%$ of the total valid votes and won by a majority of $4000$ votes. Find the total number of valid votes.
* **Solution:**
  * Let total valid votes be $100\%$.
  * Winner gets $= 60\%$.
  * Loser gets $= 100\% - 60\% = 40\%$.
  * Margin of victory $= 60\% - 40\% = 20\%$.
  * According to the question, $20\%$ of total votes $= 4000$.
  * Therefore, $100\%$ of votes $= 4000 \times \frac{100}{20} = \mathbf{20,000\text{ votes}}$.

### Question 2 (Population / Depreciation)
The population of a town is $1,80,000$. It increases at the rate of $10\%$ per annum. What will be its population after 2 years?
* **Solution:**
  * Initial Population $= 1,80,000$
  * Method 1 (Successive): Net increase $= 10 + 10 + \frac{10 \times 10}{100} = 21\%$
  * Population after 2 years $= 1,80,000 \times 121\% = \mathbf{2,17,800}$.
