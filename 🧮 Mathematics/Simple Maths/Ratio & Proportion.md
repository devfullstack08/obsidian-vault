# Ratio & Proportion (अनुपात और समानुपात)

Ratio & Proportion is a key tool for solving problems quickly. It is heavily used in Partnership, Ages, Mixture & Alligation, and Time & Work without resorting to long algebraic equations.

---

## 1. Core Concepts & Properties

### A. Ratio
* **Definition:** A ratio is a comparison of two quantities of the same kind by division. The ratio of $a$ and $b$ is written as $a:b$ or $\frac{a}{b}$.
* **Key Terms:** In the ratio $a:b$, $a$ is called the **antecedent** (first term) and $b$ is called the **consequent** (second term).
* **Compounded Ratio:** The compounded ratio of $(a:b)$ and $(c:d)$ is $(ac : bd)$.

| Type of Ratio | Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Duplicate Ratio** of $a:b$ | $a^2 : b^2$ |
| **Sub-duplicate Ratio** of $a:b$ | $\sqrt{a} : \sqrt{b}$ |
| **Triplicate Ratio** of $a:b$ | $a^3 : b^3$ |
| **Sub-triplicate Ratio** of $a:b$ | $\sqrt[3]{a} : \sqrt[3]{b}$ |

---

### B. Proportion
* **Definition:** When two ratios are equal, they are said to be in proportion.
  $$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \implies a:b :: c:d$$
* Here, $a$ and $d$ are called **extremes**, and $b$ and $c$ are called **means**.
* **Product Rule:** $\text{Product of Extremes} = \text{Product of Means} \implies a \times d = b \times c$

* **Mean Proportional:** The mean proportional between two numbers $a$ and $b$ is:
  $$\text{Mean Proportional} = \sqrt{a \times b}$$
* **Third Proportional:** The third proportional to $a$ and $b$ is:
  $$\text{Third Proportional} = \frac{b^2}{a}$$
* **Fourth Proportional:** The fourth proportional to $a$, $b$, and $c$ is:
  $$\text{Fourth Proportional} = \frac{b \times c}{a}$$

## 2. Exam Shortcuts & Tricks

### A. Combining Ratios (The "Block-Filling" Method)
If you are given $A:B = 2:3$ and $B:C = 4:5$, and you need to find $A:B:C$:

1. Write them in rows:
   ```text
   A   :   B   :   C
   2   :   3   :   [ ]  <- Fill with neighboring number (3)
  [ ]  :   4   :   5    <- Fill with neighboring number (4)
   ```
2. Fill the empty spaces:
   ```text
   A   :   B   :   C
   2   :   3   :   3
   4   :   4   :   5
   ```
3. Multiply vertically:
   * $A = 2 \times 4 = 8$
   * $B = 3 \times 4 = 12$
   * $C = 3 \times 5 = 15$
* **Result:** $A:B:C = 8:12:15$. (Fastest method, works for $A:B:C:D$ too!)

---

### B. Direct Value Substitution
If you are given a ratio $\frac{x}{y} = \frac{a}{b}$ and asked to find the value of a **homogeneous** expression (where the power of every term is the same), you can directly substitute $x = a$ and $y = b$.

> **Example:** If $x:y = 3:4$, find $(2x + 3y) : (3x + 4y)$.
> * Directly substitute $x = 3$ and $y = 4$:
> * $\text{Value} = \frac{2(3) + 3(4)}{3(3) + 4(4)} = \frac{6 + 12}{9 + 16} = \frac{18}{25}$
> * **Answer:** $18:25$.

## 3. Solved Practice Questions (Exam Level)

### Question 1 (Coin Bag Problem - Very Common in SSC CGL / Bank Exams)
A bag contains $50\text{p}$, $25\text{p}$, and $10\text{p}$ coins in the ratio $5:9:4$, amounting to a total value of ₹$206$. Find the number of coins of each type.
* **Solution:**
  * Ratio of number of coins $= 5x : 9x : 4x$
  * Value of each coin: $50\text{p} = \text{₹}0.50$, $25\text{p} = \text{₹}0.25$, $10\text{p} = \text{₹}0.10$
  * Total Value $= (5x \times 0.50) + (9x \times 0.25) + (4x \times 0.10) = 206$
  * $2.5x + 2.25x + 0.40x = 206$
  * $5.15x = 206 \implies x = \frac{206}{5.15} = 40$
  * **Number of coins:**
    * $50\text{p}$ coins $= 5 \times 40 = \mathbf{200}$
    * $25\text{p}$ coins $= 9 \times 40 = \mathbf{360}$
    * $10\text{p}$ coins $= 4 \times 40 = \mathbf{160}$

### Question 2 (Income & Expenditure - UPSC CSAT)
The ratio of incomes of two persons is $9:7$ and the ratio of their expenditures is $4:3$. If each of them saves ₹$2000$ per month, find their monthly incomes.
* **Solution:**
  * Let their incomes be $9x$ and $7x$.
  * Let their expenditures be $4y$ and $3y$.
  * Recall: $\text{Income} - \text{Savings} = \text{Expenditure}$
  * $\frac{9x - 2000}{7x - 2000} = \frac{4}{3}$
  * Cross-multiplying: $3(9x - 2000) = 4(7x - 2000)$
  * $27x - 6000 = 28x - 8000 \implies x = 2000$
  * **Incomes:**
    * First person $= 9 \times 2000 = \mathbf{₹18,000}$
    * Second person $= 7 \times 2000 = \mathbf{₹14,000}$
