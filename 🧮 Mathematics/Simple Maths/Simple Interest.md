# Simple Interest (સાધારણ વ્યાજ)

Simple Interest is calculated only on the principal amount. Unlike compound interest, the interest remains constant every year.

---

## 1. Core Formulas

* **Simple Interest (SI):**
  $$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$
  * Where:
    * $P = \text{Principal (Initial sum of money)}$
    * $R = \text{Rate of Interest (in \% per annum)}$
    * $T = \text{Time (in years)}$
* **Amount (A):** The total money returned after $T$ years.
  $$A = P + \text{SI} = P \left(1 + \frac{RT}{100}\right)$$

---

## 2. Exam Shortcuts & Tricks

### A. The "$n$-times" Formula
If a sum of money becomes **$n$ times** of itself in $T$ years at a rate of $R\%$:

$$R \times T = (n - 1) \times 100$$

> **Example:** A sum of money doubles itself in $8\text{ years}$ at simple interest. Find the rate of interest.
> * Here, $n = 2$ (doubles), and $T = 8$.
> * $R \times 8 = (2 - 1) \times 100$
> * $R \times 8 = 100 \implies R = \frac{100}{8} = \mathbf{12.5\%}$.
> * **Answer:** Rate of interest is **$12.5\%$**. (Solved in seconds!)

---

### B. The Double Time-Ratio Trick
If a sum of money becomes $n_1$ times in $T_1$ years, and $n_2$ times in $T_2$ years at the same rate of interest:

$$\frac{T_1}{T_2} = \frac{n_1 - 1}{n_2 - 1}$$

> **Example:** A sum of money becomes $3\text{ times}$ of itself in $10\text{ years}$ at simple interest. In how many years will it become $5\text{ times}$ of itself?
> * Given: $n_1 = 3$, $T_1 = 10$, $n_2 = 5$, find $T_2$.
> * Using the formula:
>   $$\frac{10}{T_2} = \frac{3 - 1}{5 - 1} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$$
>   $$T_2 = 10 \times 2 = \mathbf{20\text{ years}}$$
> * **Answer:** It will take **$20\text{ years}$**.

---

### C. Rate Change / Extra Interest Shortcut
If the interest rate changes from $R_1\%$ to $R_2\%$, the change in interest earned ($\Delta \text{SI}$) is:

$$\Delta \text{SI} = \frac{P \times \Delta R \times T}{100}$$

> **Example:** A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for $3\text{ years}$. Had it been put at a $2\%$ higher rate, it would have fetched ₹$360$ more. Find the principal.
> * Given: $T = 3$, $\Delta R = 2\%$, $\Delta \text{SI} = 360$
> * $$360 = \frac{P \times 2 \times 3}{100}$$
> * $$360 = \frac{6P}{100} \implies P = \frac{360 \times 100}{6} = \mathbf{₹6000}$$
> * **Answer:** Principal is **₹6000**.
