Tongue / Lingua / lingual 
- The tongue is a muscular organ whose rough upper surface contains papillae, many of which house taste buds.
- (Filiform papillae = No taste buds)
- Five Basic Tastes
	1. Sweet
	2. Sour
	3. Salty
	4. Bitter
	5. Umami (savory)
- Lingual frenulum = Connects tongue to floor of mouth

Teeth 
- Adult teeth: 32 | Milk teeth: 20
- Study: Odontology
- Upper jaw: Maxilla
- Lower jaw: Mandible
- Dentition: Arrangement of teeth in the jaws
    - Thecodont: Teeth embedded in jaw sockets (theca = socket).
    - Diphyodont: Two sets of teeth:
        
        1. Temporary (milk): ~6 months–3 years
        2. Permanent: From ~6 years (wisdom teeth may erupt at 17–25 years).
        
    - Heterodont: Four types of teeth:
        
        1. Incisors — 8 (cutting)
        2. Canines — 4 (tearing)
        3. Premolars — 8 (crushing)
        4. Molars — 12 (grinding)
        
- Tooth Structure:
- Enamel: White outer crown; hardest body substance; acellular, avascular; fluoride strengthens it.
- Dentin: Forms the major part of the tooth.
- Cementum: Covers the root.
- Pulp cavity: Contains nerves and blood vessels; does not normally close after eruption.
- Gum (Gingiva): Surrounds teeth.
- Root canal: Contains pulp tissue within the root.
- Alveolar bone: Supports tooth sockets.

- Permanent teeth replacing milk teeth: 20
- Permanent teeth erupting without replacing milk teeth: 12 (all permanent molars).
- Third molar: Wisdom tooth.

- Dental Formula:

	- Permanent: 2.1.2.3 / 2.1.2.3 × 2 = 32
	- Milk: 2.1.0.2 / 2.1.0.2 × 2 = 20
