
| Feature    | Bright                                  | Luminous                             |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Meaning    | Giving off or reflecting a lot of light | Producing or emitting light; glowing |
| Main focus | Intensity of light                      | Emission of light                    |
| Example    | The sun is very bright.                 | The stars are luminous.              |

Luminous = Giving off light
The word luminous comes from Latin lumen, meaning light.
The jellyfish is luminous.
Focus: It produces or emits light.


## 3. Important distinction

A thing can be bright without being luminous.

- A bright white shirt: It reflects a lot of light but does not produce its own light.
- A luminous firefly: It produces its own light, even if that light is not very bright.
- The Sun: It is both bright and luminous.
- 
Also, bright can mean intelligent (a bright student), while luminous can describe something exceptionally clear or illuminating (a luminous explanation).

## Easy memory trick

- Bright → Brightness → How much light?
- Luminous → Luminescence → Giving off light.
