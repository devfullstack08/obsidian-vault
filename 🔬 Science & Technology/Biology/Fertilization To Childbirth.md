### **Important Facts**

- **Ovulation:** Usually occurs approximately 14 days before the next menstrual period (around Day 14 in a 28-day cycle).
    
- **Sperm survival:** Up to 5 days in favourable conditions in the female reproductive tract.
    
- **Ovum survival:** Approximately 12–24 hours after ovulation.
    
- **Fertilization:** Usually occurs at the ampullary–isthmic junction of the fallopian tube.
    
- **Selection:** Millions of sperm begin the journey, but relatively few reach the fertilization site. Only one sperm normally fertilizes the ovum.
