
**Science:** The word “science” comes from the Latin word scientia, meaning “knowledge.”

**Science branches:** physics, astronomy, biology, geology, and chemistry.

**Nature of science:** by nature, I'm naturally natural.

**Science fair**: engages students and ordinary people, encouraging them to learn and ask questions.

**Scientific method:** It's typically presented in a linear set of steps. (there isn't just one, universal scientific method that all scientists use). -> Observation - Question - Hypothesis - Procedure - Analysis - Conclusion  

**Scientific learning:** include scientific theories and scientific laws.
