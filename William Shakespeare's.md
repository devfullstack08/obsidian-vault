Hamlet
 phrase: **Caviar to the General**
 - Caviar: An expensive delicacy made from fish eggs.
	 - luxury food (Caviar is considered one of the world's most luxurious foods because it comes from the eggs of sturgeon fish, which can take many years to mature, and the eggs are difficult and expensive to produce.)
 - The general: Ordinary people or the general public.
 (caviar to the general public ) - Something that is too refined, sophisticated, or excellent for ordinary people to appreciate or understand.
