**Homographs** are words that have the **same spelling** but have **different meanings**.

They may have:

- the same pronunciation, or
    
- different pronunciation.
    

The word _homograph_ comes from:

- **homo** = same
    
- **graph** = writing
    

So:

> **Homographs = Same spelling, different meaning.**

### Examples With the Same Pronunciation

#### Bank

- **bank** = financial institution
    
- **bank** = side of a river
    

> I deposited money in the **bank**.

> We sat on the river **bank**.

#### Bat

- **bat** = flying animal
    
- **bat** = sports equipment
    

> A **bat** flew out of the cave.

> He bought a new cricket **bat**.

#### Match

- **match** = a sports competition
    
- **match** = a stick used to create fire
    

> We watched the football **match**.

> He lit the candle with a **match**.


---

### Homographs With Different Pronunciations

Some homographs are spelled the same but pronounced differently.

These are also called **heteronyms**.

## Examples

### Lead

**lead** /liːd/ = to guide

> She will **lead** the team.

**lead** /led/ = a metal

> The pipe contains **lead**.

---

### Tear

**tear** /tɪə(r)/ = liquid from the eye

> A **tear** rolled down her cheek.

**tear** /teə(r)/ = to rip something

> Do not **tear** the paper.

---

### Bow

**bow** /baʊ/ = bend forward

> The actor took a **bow**.

**bow** /bəʊ/ = weapon used with arrows

> He carried a **bow** and arrow.

---

### Wind

**wind** /wɪnd/ = moving air

> The **wind** is very strong.

**wind** /waɪnd/ = turn or twist

> **Wind** the clock carefully.
