A **heteronym** is a special type of homograph.

It has:

- the same spelling
    
- different pronunciation
    
- different meaning
    

Examples:

|Word|Pronunciation/Meaning 1|Pronunciation/Meaning 2|
|---|---|---|
|lead|/liːd/ = guide|/led/ = metal|
|tear|/tɪə(r)/ = eye liquid|/teə(r)/ = rip|
|wind|/wɪnd/ = moving air|/waɪnd/ = twist|
|bow|/bəʊ/ = weapon|/baʊ/ = bend|
|object|**OB**ject = thing|ob**JECT** = oppose|
|present|**PRE**sent = gift/current|pre**SENT** = introduce|
