## Passive syntax

|          | Present                    | Past                      | Future                               |
| -------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| simple   | am/is/are + v3             | was/were + v3             | shall/will be + v3                   |
| continue | am/is/are + **being** + v3 | was/were + **being** + v3 | not possible                         |
| perfect  | have/has + **been** + v3   | had + **been** + v3       | shall have/will have + **been** + v3 |

|Tense|Passive Structure|
|---|---|
|Present Simple|am/is/are + V3|
|Present Continuous|am/is/are + being + V3|
|Present Perfect|has/have + been + V3|
|Past Simple|was/were + V3|
|Past Continuous|was/were + being + V3|
|Past Perfect|had + been + V3|
|Future Simple|will + be + V3|
|Future Perfect|will + have been + V3|
|Modal|modal + be + V3|
|Modal Perfect|modal + have been + V3|

## Modal Verb Passive

Common modal verbs:

- can
- could
- may
- might
- must
- should
- would
- shall

### Active

`Subject + Modal + V1 + Object`

### Passive

`Object + Modal + be + V3`

Example:

> She can solve the problem.

Passive:

> The problem can be solved by her.


-----
## Negative Sentences

The negative word **not** stays after the helping verb.

Example:

> She does not write letters.

Passive:

> Letters are not written by her.

---

> He did not complete the work.

Passive:

> The work was not completed by him.

---

> They will not accept the request.

Passive:

> The request will not be accepted by them.


---

## Interrogative Sentences

### Present Simple

Active:

> Does he write letters?

Passive:

> Are letters written by him?

### Past Simple

Active:

> Did she complete the work?

Passive:

> Was the work completed by her?

### Future

Active:

> Will they announce the result?

Passive:

> Will the result be announced by them?
