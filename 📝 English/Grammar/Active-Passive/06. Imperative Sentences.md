Imperative sentences express:

- orders
- commands
- instructions
- requests

Traditional passive formula:

`Let + Object + be + V3`

Example:

> Open the door.

Passive:

> Let the door be opened.

---

> Complete the work.

Passive:

> Let the work be completed.

---

> Follow the rules.

Passive:

> Let the rules be followed.

---

## Negative Imperative

Active:

> Do not open the door.

Passive:

> Let the door not be opened.

Another natural version:

> The door should not be opened.
