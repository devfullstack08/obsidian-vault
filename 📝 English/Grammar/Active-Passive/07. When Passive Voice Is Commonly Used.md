
Passive voice is useful when the action is more important than the doer.
### Scientific writing

> The solution was heated to 100°C.

The scientist does not need to say:

> We heated the solution...

### News

> Three people were arrested yesterday.

### Official language

> Applications must be submitted before Monday.

### Instructions

> The password should be changed regularly.

### When the doer is unknown

> My wallet was stolen.

### When the doer is obvious

> The criminal was arrested.

Obviously, police probably arrested the criminal.

## Active vs Passive in Real English

Active voice is usually clearer and more direct.

Active:

> Our team fixed the bug.

Passive:

> The bug was fixed by our team.

Both are correct.

If the focus is the team:

> Our team fixed the bug.

If the focus is the bug:

> The bug was fixed.

As a software developer, you will often see passive voice in technical documentation:

> The user is authenticated.

> The request is validated.

> The data is stored in MongoDB.

> The token is generated automatically.

> The account has been created.

These sentences focus on the **process/result**, not necessarily who performs the action.
