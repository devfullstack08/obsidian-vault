
The WH word generally remains at the beginning.

Example:

> Why did he cancel the meeting?

Passive:

> Why was the meeting cancelled by him?

---

> When will they announce the result?

Passive:

> When will the result be announced by them?

---

> Where did she keep the documents?

Passive:

> Where were the documents kept by her?


---

## Who and Whom

Active:

> Who wrote this book?

Formal passive:

> By whom was this book written?

Natural modern English:

> Who was this book written by?

Another:

> Who helped you?

Passive:

> By whom were you helped?

For competitive exams, remember:

**Who → By whom**
