An **apostrophe** is mainly used for **possession** and **contractions**.

1. **To show possession**
    

- Rahul’s book = the book belongs to Rahul
    
- The girl’s bag = the bag belongs to the girl
    

For plural nouns ending in **s**, put the apostrophe after the **s**:

- the students’ classroom
    
- the teachers’ room
    

For irregular plurals not ending in **s**:

- children’s toys
    
- men’s shoes
    
- women’s clothes
    

2. **In contractions**  
    It shows missing letters:
    

- do not → **don’t**
    
- cannot → **can’t**
    
- I am → **I’m**
    
- we are → **we’re**
    
- it is → **it’s**
    

3. **Important difference: its vs it’s**
    

- **its** = possession  
    _The dog wagged its tail._
    
- **it’s** = it is / it has  
    _It’s raining._
    

4. **Do not use apostrophes for normal plurals**
    

- apple’s ❌
    
- apples ✅
    
- book’s ❌
    
- books ✅
    

### Easy memory

**Apostrophe = ownership or missing letters.**

Examples:

- **John’s car** → ownership
    
- **John’s coming** → “John is coming”
