### 5. Confusion with Inverted comas ".".
- AME: At the end of a sentence in American English it always comes before the closing inverted coma.
	- He said, "Come back.".
- BE: In British English it depends whether you have a full sentence said inside the inverted comas or if you are ending the full sentence outside the inverted comas.
	- He said, "come back .
- If the inverted comas are in the middle of a sentence we always use a coma at the end, even if it a full sentence.
	- He said, "Please arrive at 10 o'clock," and then he told me that it didn't matter if I was a bit late.

### 6. Confusion with brackets
put the full stop inside the brackets when the word in brackets make a full sentence. put the full stop outside the bracets when the word in brackets are part of a sentece.
