
### “A” before a consonant sound

> a university  
> a European  
> a one-eyed man

### “An” before a vowel sound

> an hour  
> an honest person  
> an MLA

### “The” for something specific or unique

> the Sun  
> the Earth  
> the Ganga  
> the Himalayas

### No article with languages and subjects

> She speaks English.  
> He studies mathematics.
