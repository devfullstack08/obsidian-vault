**Hardly/Scarcely → when**  
**No sooner → than**.

- **Subject + had + hardly/scarcely + V3 + when + simple past**
	- I had hardly finished dinner when he called.
	-  He had hardly reached the station when the train left.


- **Subject + hardly/scarcely + verb**
	- He hardly speaks.  
    - She scarcely eats.

### Rule 1. Inversion with Hardly, Scarcely and No Sooner

When Hardly, Scarcely or No sooner begins a sentence, we use inversion: **the helping verb comes before the subject**.

- **Hardly/Scarcely + had + subject + V3 + when + simple past**
	- Hardly had I finished dinner when he called.
	- Hardly had he reached the station when the train left
- **No sooner + had + Subject + V3 + than + Simple Past**
	 - No sooner had I reached than the bell rang.
