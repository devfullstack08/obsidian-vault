**Hardly/Scarcely → when**  
**No sooner → than**.

- **Subject + had + hardly/scarcely + V3 + when + simple past**
    
    > I had hardly finished dinner when he called.
    
- **Hardly/Scarcely + had + subject + V3 + when + simple past**
    
    > Hardly had I finished dinner when he called.
    
- **Subject + hardly/scarcely + verb**
    
    > He hardly speaks.  
    > She scarcely eats.
    

- **Scarcely had I entered... when...** ✅
- **Hardly had he arrived... when...** ✅
- **No sooner had he arrived... than...** ✅
