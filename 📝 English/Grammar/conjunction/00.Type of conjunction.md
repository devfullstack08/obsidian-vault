
| Type of conjunction           | What it does                                      | Common words                                            | Example                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Coordinating conjunction**  | Joins words or clauses of equal importance        | **for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so**                     | _I was tired, **but** I continued working._  |
| **Subordinating conjunction** | Joins a dependent clause to an independent clause | **because, although, if, when, while, since, unless**   | _I stayed home **because** it was raining._  |
| **Correlative conjunction**   | Works in pairs to join equal parts                | **either…or, neither…nor, both…and, not only…but also** | _She is **both** smart **and** hardworking._ |

A simple way to remember them:

**Coordinating = equal + equal**  
_Ram **and** Shyam are friends._

**Subordinating = main idea + dependent idea**  
_I went to bed **because I was tired**._

**Correlative = pair of conjunctions**  
_**Either** study **or** go to sleep._

Also remember **FANBOYS** for coordinating conjunctions:

**F**or, **A**nd, **N**or, **B**ut, **O**r, **Y**et, **S**o.
