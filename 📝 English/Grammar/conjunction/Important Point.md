
### Rule 1. Since / For

Since = starting point / points yo a starting point. 
For = duration / tells you how long.

| Incorrect                               | Correct                               |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| I have lived here **since** five years. | I have lived here **for** five years. |
| She has been ill **for** Monday         | She has been ill **since** Monday     |
