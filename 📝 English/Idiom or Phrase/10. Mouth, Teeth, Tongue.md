| Mealy Mouthed<br><br>Mealy-mouthed = indirect / evasive / unwilling to speak plainly<br>(ગોળગોળ બોલનાર / સ્પષ્ટ ન બોલનાર / સીધી વાત ટાળનાર)                                                             | Not speaking clearly or directly; avoiding saying exactly what you mean, often because you are afraid of offending someone or taking a firm position.<br><br>1. Stop being **mealy-mouthed** and tell me what you really think.<br>2. The politician gave a **mealy-mouthed answer**.<br>3. His response was so **mealy-mouthed** that nobody knew his real opinion.                                                                                |
| Put words into someone's mouth<br><br>falsely claim that someone said or meant something.<br>(કોઈએ ન કહેલી વાત તેના નામે કહેવી / કોઈની વાતનો ખોટો અર્થ કાઢવો)                                           | To say or suggest that someone said or meant something **when they actually did not**.<br><br>1. Don’t **put words into my mouth**. I never said that.<br>2. She accused him of **putting words into her mouth**.<br>3. You’re **putting words into my mouth**; that’s not what I meant.                                                                                                                                                            |

| Put your money where your mouth<br>(Talk is not enough → prove it with action)<br><br>(કહેવાથી નહીં, કામ કરીને સાબિત કરવું / પોતાની વાતને ક્રિયામાં સાબિત કરવી)<br><br><br>let the bet<br>show me truth | Prove that you really believe what you say by taking action, especially by risking money, effort, or commitment.<br><br>1. He keeps saying the project is excellent, but now he needs to **put his money where his mouth is**.<br>3. | Kiss and Tell<br><br>reveal private romantic details<br>(પ્રેમસંબંધની ખાનગી વાતો જાહેર કરવી / અંગત સંબંધોની વાતો બીજાને કહેવી)                                                                          | To reveal private or intimate details about a romantic or sexual relationship, especially to other people or the media.<br><br>1. He promised he would never **kiss and tell**.<br>2. The actor was angry because his former partner **kissed and told**.<br>3. She refused to **kiss and tell** about her relationship.                                                                                                                            |


## Teeth

| Idiom / Phrase                                                                                                                                       | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Teething Troubles<br><br>(early problem)<br>(શરૂઆતની મુશ્કેલીઓ / નવી વ્યવસ્થા કે પ્રોજેક્ટની શરૂઆતની સમસ્યાઓ)                                        | Small problems or difficulties that happen in the **early stages of something new**.<br><br>Examples:<br><br>1. The new software is having some **teething troubles**.<br>2. Every new business has a few **teething troubles** at the beginning.<br>3. The project faced some **teething troubles**, but things are improving now.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Take the bit between your teeth<br><br>take control and act determinedly<br>(દૃઢ નિશ્ચય સાથે કામ હાથમાં લેવું / પરિસ્થિતિનો કાબૂ પોતાના હાથમાં લેવો) | To take control of a situation and act with determination, especially when you decide to do something yourself.<br><br>1. She **took the bit between her teeth** and completed the project herself.<br>2. Once he **took the bit between his teeth**, nobody could stop him.<br>3. The team finally **took the bit between their teeth** and started making decisions quickly.<br><br><br>It comes from horse riding: when a horse takes the **bit** firmly in its teeth, it may become difficult for the rider to control. |
| Bite the Bullet<br><br>face something difficult bravely<br>(મુશ્કેલ અથવા અપ્રિય વાત હિંમતથી સ્વીકારવી / કઠિન કામ કરવાની હિંમત કરવી)                  | To force yourself to do or accept something **difficult, unpleasant, or painful** because it cannot be avoided.<br><br>1. I finally **bit the bullet** and told him the truth.<br>2. We have to **bite the bullet** and pay for the repairs.<br>3. She **bit the bullet** and went to the dentist.<br><br>> You don’t want to do it, but you know you must do it.                                                                                                                                                           |
