
| Idiom / Phrase                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Albatross(sea birds) Around My Neck(ગળે બાંધી દીધું)<br><br>(મોટો બોજ / સતત મુશ્કેલી / પીછો ન છોડતી જવાબદારી)                                                                                                            | A serious burden, problem, guilt, or responsibility that is difficult to get rid of.<br><br>1. That huge debt became **an albatross around his neck**.<br>2. The failed project was **an albatross around the company’s neck**.<br>3. His old mistake remained **an albatross around his neck** for years.              |
| Stick Your Neck Out<br><br>take a personal risk / expose yourself to possible trouble.<br>(જોખમ લેવું / કોઈના માટે કે કોઈ વાત માટે પોતાને જોખમમાં મૂકવું)                                                                | To take a risk by supporting someone, giving an opinion, or doing something that could cause trouble for you.<br><br>1. I **stuck my neck out** for him, but he didn’t support me later.<br>2. She **stuck her neck out** and defended the new employee.<br>3. He is unwilling to **stick his neck out** for this idea. |
| Breathing down my neck<br><br>closely watching or pressuring them.<br>(સતત નજર રાખવી / પાછળ પડીને દબાણ કરવું / ખૂબ નજીકથી દેખરેખ રાખવી)                                                                                  | To watch or pressure someone very closely, especially in an annoying or stressful way.<br><br>1. My manager is **breathing down my neck** about the deadline.<br>2. I can’t work properly with someone **breathing down my neck**.<br>3. The police were **breathing down his neck**.                                   |
| Nip and Tuck<br><br>nip - ચૂસવું<br>Tuck- ખોસી દેવું અથવા દૂર મૂકવું<br><br><br>(બરાબરીની ટક્કર / બહુ નજીકની સ્પર્ધા / કોણ જીતશે તે નક્કી ન હોય એવી સ્થિતિ)<br><br>Nip and tuck = very close competition / neck and neck | A competition or situation that is **very close**, where either side could win.<br><br>1. The race was **nip and tuck** until the final few metres.<br>2. It was **nip and tuck** between the two teams.<br>3. The election remained **nip and tuck** until the last votes were counted.                                |
