| Lip service<br>(only saying something verbally, without actually doing it)<br><br>(મોઢે સમર્થન આપવું, પણ હકીકતમાં કશું ન કરવું)         | saying that you support, believe in, or care about something **without actually doing anything about it**.<br><br>1. | Read my Lips<br><br>listen carefully and believe what I am saying.<br><br>(મારી વાત ધ્યાનથી સાંભળો / હું જે કહું છું તેને ગંભીરતાથી લો) | Listen very carefully to what I am saying, because I mean it seriously and clearly.<br><br>- **Read my lips:** I am not changing my decision.<br>- **Read my lips:** this is your last warning.<br>- She said, **“Read my lips, I won’t do it again.”**<br><br>-> It is often used for **strong emphasis**, especially when the speaker wants to make a firm point. |
