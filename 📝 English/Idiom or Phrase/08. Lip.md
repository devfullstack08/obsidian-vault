
| Idiom / Phrase                                                                                                                             | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lip service<br>(only saying something verbally, without actually doing it)<br><br>(મોઢે સમર્થન આપવું, પણ હકીકતમાં કશું ન કરવું)            | saying that you support, believe in, or care about something **without actually doing anything about it**.<br><br>1. The company pays **lip service** to employee welfare but does very little to improve working conditions.<br>2. Politicians often **pay lip service to** reform without taking real action.                                                     |
| Read my Lips<br><br>listen carefully and believe what I am saying.<br><br>(મારી વાત ધ્યાનથી સાંભળો / હું જે કહું છું તેને ગંભીરતાથી લો)    | Listen very carefully to what I am saying, because I mean it seriously and clearly.<br><br>- **Read my lips:** I am not changing my decision.<br>- **Read my lips:** this is your last warning.<br>- She said, **“Read my lips, I won’t do it again.”**<br><br>-> It is often used for **strong emphasis**, especially when the speaker wants to make a firm point. |
| Curl Your Lip<br><br>show contempt or strong dislike with your facial expression<br><br>(તિરસ્કારથી હોઠ વાળવો / નાપસંદગી કે અવગણના બતાવવી) | To raise or twist your upper lip to show **dislike, contempt, scorn, or disapproval**.<br><br>1. She **curled her lip** when she saw the dirty room.<br>2. He **curled his lip in contempt**.<br>3. Don’t **curl your lip** at people just because you disagree with them.                                                                                          |
| On Everyone's Lips<br><br>(બધાની ચર્ચામાં હોવું / દરેકની જીભ પર નામ હોવું / ખૂબ ચર્ચિત હોવું)                                              | Being talked about by **many people**; a person, topic, event, or piece of news is very popular or widely discussed.<br><br>1. The new movie is **on everyone’s lips**.<br>2. Her name was **on everyone’s lips** after the competition.<br>3. The scandal has been **on everyone’s lips** all week.<br>                                                            |
| Give somebody lip<br><br>talk back rudely / answer disrespectfully.<br>(કોઈને ઉદ્ધતાઈથી જવાબ આપવો / બેઅદબીથી બોલવું / સામે જવાબ આપવો)      | To speak to someone in a rude, disrespectful, or cheeky way.<br><br>1. Don’t **give me any lip**.<br>2. The student got in trouble for **giving the teacher lip**.<br>3. He was punished because he **gave his father lip**.                                                                                                                                        |
