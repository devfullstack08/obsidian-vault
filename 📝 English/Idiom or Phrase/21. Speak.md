| Idiom / Phrase                                                                                                                                        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Raise Your voice<br><br><br>speak louder<br>(અવાજ ઊંચો કરવો / જોરથી બોલવું)                                                                           | To speak **more loudly than normal**, often because you are angry, excited, or trying to be heard.<br><br>1. Don’t **raise your voice** at me.<br>2. He **raised his voice** during the argument.<br>3. You don’t need to **raise your voice**; I can hear you.                                                                            |
| Speak Volumes<br><br>show or reveal a lot without saying much.<br><br>(ઘણું બધું કહી દેવું / બહુ અર્થ વ્યક્ત કરવો / શબ્દો વગર પણ ઘણું બતાવવું)        | To reveal or communicate **a lot of information, meaning, or emotion without needing many words**.<br><br>1. Her silence **spoke volumes**.<br>2. His expression **spoke volumes** about how disappointed he was.<br>3. The company’s actions **speak volumes** about its priorities.                                                      |
| Talk out of Turn<br><br>speak at the wrong time or inappropriately.<br>(અયોગ્ય સમયે બોલવું / પોતાની વારી વગર બોલવું / ન બોલવું જોઈએ એવી વાત કહી દેવી) | To speak when it is **not appropriate**, especially when you were not asked to speak or when you say something you should not.<br><br>1. I’m sorry if I **spoke out of turn**.<br>2. He **talked out of turn** during the meeting.<br>3. She was criticized for **speaking out of turn**.                                                  |
| Speak too Soon<br><br>Say something prematurely and later be proved wrong.<br>(વહેલું કહી દેવું / સંપૂર્ણ માહિતી પહેલા જ નિષ્કર્ષ કાઢી દેવું)         | To say something **before you have enough information**, and then later discover that what you said was wrong or premature.<br><br>1. I thought the problem was solved, but I **spoke too soon**.<br>2. She said the weather would stay sunny, but she **spoke too soon**.<br>3. “This exam is easy.”  <br>    “Don’t **speak too soon**.” |
