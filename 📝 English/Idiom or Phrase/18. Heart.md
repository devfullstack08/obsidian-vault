| Heart in the Right Place<br><br>(ઇરાદા સારા હોવા / દિલથી સારો હોવો / સારા હેતુથી કામ કરવું)                                                                        | To have **good intentions**, even if your actions or decisions are not always perfect.<br><br>1. He made a mistake, but his **heart was in the right place**.<br>2. She can be strict, but her **heart is in the right place**.<br>3. I know he was trying to help; his **heart was in the right place**.                                                |
| Cockles of your heart<br><br>Make you feel very happy, pleased, or emotionally warm.<br>(હૃદયની ઊંડાઈ / દિલની અંદર સુધી ખુશી કે ઉષ્મા અનુભવવી)<br>                 | The deepest part of your feelings; usually used when something makes you feel **warm, happy, touched, or emotionally pleased**.<br>1. Seeing the children reunited **warmed the cockles of my heart**.<br>2. Her kind message really **warmed the cockles of his heart**.<br>3. It **warms the cockles of your heart** to see people helping each other. |
| Heart Sinks<br>(દિલ ધગધગ કરે છે - My heart is pounding)<br><br>Sink : ડુબવું, ખાળકૂંડી <br>sinks - sank - sunk<br>(અચાનક મન ઉદાસ થઈ જવું / નિરાશ થવું / ચિંતા થવી) | To suddenly feel **sad, disappointed, worried, or discouraged**.<br><br>1. My **heart sank** when I saw the exam result.<br>2. Her **heart sank** when she heard the bad news.<br>3. His **heart sank** at the thought of starting all over again.                                                                                                       |
| Pour your heart out<br><br>openly share your deepest feelings.<br>(દિલની બધી વાત ખુલ્લેઆમ કહી દેવી / મનની લાગણીઓ વ્યક્ત કરવી)                                      | To tell someone your deepest feelings, worries, problems, or emotions very openly.<br><br>1. She **poured her heart out** to her best friend.<br>2. He **poured his heart out** about his family problems.<br>3. Sometimes it helps to **pour your heart out** to someone you trust.                                                                     |
