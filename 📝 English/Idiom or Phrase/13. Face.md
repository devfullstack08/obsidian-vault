| Face value<br><br>(દેખીતી કિંમત / ઉપરથી દેખાતો અર્થ / લખેલી કિંમત)                              | The **apparent or stated value/meaning** of something, without looking for a hidden meaning or deeper truth.<br><br>1. Don’t take his words **at face value**.  <br> 2. I accepted her explanation **at face value**.  <br>3. The **face value** of this note is ₹500.  <br><br>**Take something at face value** = believe/accept it without questioning.                                                                                                                                |
| On the face Of it <br><br>(પ્રથમ નજરે / ઉપરથી જોતા / દેખીતી રીતે)                               | Judging only by the first appearance or the information immediately available; **apparently**.<br><br>1. **On the face of it**, the plan seems good.<br>2. **On the face of it**, he appears to be innocent.<br>3. **On the face of it**, the deal looks profitable, but we should check the details.<br><br>> **At face value** = accept something exactly as it appears, without questioning it.  <br>> **On the face of it** = based on first appearance, something seems to be true. |
| In the face of<br><br>despite / when confronted with<br><br>(મુશ્કેલી / વિરોધ / જોખમ હોવા છતાં) | Despite a difficult situation, danger, opposition, or problem.<br><br>1. She remained calm **in the face of danger**.<br>2. He continued working **in the face of criticism**.<br>3. The company succeeded **in the face of strong competition**.<br><br>Common collocations:<br><br>- **in the face of danger**<br>- **in the face of opposition**<br>- **in the face of criticism**<br>- **in the face of adversity**<br>- **in the face of competition**                              |
