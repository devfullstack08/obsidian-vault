
| Phrase       | Structure                   | Use                           | Example                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **too much** | too much + uncountable noun | More than needed              | There is **too much water**.    |
| **too much** | verb + too much             | An action happens excessively | He **talks too much**.          |
| **much too** | much too + adjective        | Adjective is excessive        | This bag is **much too heavy**. |
| **much too** | much too + adverb           | Adverb is excessive           | He drives **much too fast**.    |
### 1. Too much

Use **too much** with an **uncountable noun**.

Examples:

- I drink **too much coffee**.
- She has **too much work**.
- There is **too much sugar** in the tea.
- You spend **too much money**.

You can also use it after a verb:

- He talks **too much**.
- I ate **too much**.
- She worries **too much**.

Think:

> **too much + noun**  
> or  
> **verb + too much**

### 2. Much too

Use **much too** before an **adjective or adverb**.

Examples:

- The box is **much too heavy**.
- This question is **much too difficult**.
- The room is **much too hot**.
- He drives **much too quickly**.
- You are walking **much too slowly**.

Here, **much** makes **too** stronger.

Compare:

- This shirt is **too expensive**. = more expensive than acceptable.
- This shirt is **much too expensive**. = far more expensive than acceptable.

### Easy trick

Look at the word after the phrase:

- **Noun → too much**
- **Adjective/adverb → much too**

Examples:

- **too much water** ✅ — water = noun
- **too much noise** ✅ — noise = noun
- **much too hot** ✅ — hot = adjective
- **much too expensive** ✅ — expensive = adjective
- **much too slowly** ✅ — slowly = adverb

One more important point: for **countable plural nouns**, use **too many**, not _too much_.

- Too much water ✅
- Too much money ✅
- **Too many books** ✅
- **Too many people** ✅

So the core rule is:

> **Too much + uncountable noun**  
> **Too many + plural countable noun**  
> **Much too + adjective/adverb**
