- **Glass half full** → **positive / optimistic** thinking. You focus on what you have.
- **Glass half empty** → **negative / pessimistic** thinking. You focus on what is missing.
- **Glass completely full** → can suggest being very satisfied, abundant, or highly positive.
- **Glass completely empty** → can suggest having nothing left, disappointment, or a negative situation.

The main expression is: **“half full = positive” and “half empty = negative.”**
