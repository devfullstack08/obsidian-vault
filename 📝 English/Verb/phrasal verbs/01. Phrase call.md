
| Phrase                 | Meaning                                                   | Example                                             |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **call**               | phone someone / shout their name                          | I will **call you** tonight.                        |
| **call back**          | return a phone call                                       | I’m busy now. I’ll **call you back**.               |
| **call up**            | phone someone                                             | I’ll **call him up** tomorrow.                      |
| **call on**            | visit someone / ask someone to speak or answer            | The teacher **called on** me.                       |
| **call at**            | visit a place briefly                                     | The train **calls at** Mumbai Central.              |
| **call in**            | ask someone to come and help                              | We **called in** an electrician.                    |
| **call for**           | require / demand / go and collect someone                 | This job **calls for** experience.                  |
| **call out**           | shout / publicly criticize / ask someone to come for help | She **called out** his name.                        |
| **call off**           | cancel                                                    | They **called off** the meeting.                    |
| **call over**          | ask someone to come to you                                | She **called me over**.                             |
| **call around**        | phone several people/places                               | I **called around** to find a cheaper hotel.        |
| **call by**            | visit briefly                                             | I’ll **call by** your office tomorrow.              |
| **call in on**         | visit someone briefly                                     | I **called in on** my friend yesterday.             |
| **call upon**          | formally ask/request                                      | The government **called upon** people to cooperate. |
| **call away**          | make someone leave because they are needed elsewhere      | The doctor was **called away**.                     |
| **call forth**         | produce or cause something                                | His speech **called forth** strong reactions.       |
| **call into question** | make something seem doubtful                              | The mistake **called his judgment into question**.  |

**call someone** → phone them  
**call back** → return a phone call  
**call out** → shout / criticize publicly  
**call in** → ask someone to come help  
**call for** → require/demand  
**call on** → ask someone to answer/do something  
**call off** → cancel  
**call over** → ask someone to come toward you  
**call around** → phone multiple places

For example:

> The match was **called off** because of rain.  
> The teacher **called on** me.  
> This situation **calls for** patience.  
> We **called in** an expert.  
> She **called him out for** lying.  
> I'll **call you back** later.
