These verbs do **not** change in past or V3. Only **V4** changes.

**V1 = V2 = V3**  
**Only V4 adds -ing**

| Verb | V1 | V2 | V3 | V4 | Example |
| ---- | -- | -- | -- | -- | ------- |
| **cut** | cut | cut | cut | cutting | He cut the paper. |
| **put** | put | put | put | putting | She put the book there. |
| **set** | set | set | set | setting | Set the table. |
| **hit** | hit | hit | hit | hitting | He hit the ball. |
| **let** | let | let | let | letting | Let him go. |
| **shut** | shut | shut | shut | shutting | Shut the door. |
| **hurt** | hurt | hurt | hurt | hurting | He hurt his leg. |
| **cost** | cost | cost | cost | costing | This cost too much. |
| **spread** | spread | spread | spread | spreading | She spread the butter. |
| **read** | read | read | read | reading | I read the book yesterday. |
| **burst** | burst | burst | burst | bursting | The balloon burst. |
| **cast** | cast | cast | cast | casting | He cast a vote. |
| **broadcast** | broadcast | broadcast | broadcast | broadcasting | They broadcast the news. |
| **forecast** | forecast | forecast | forecast | forecasting | They forecast rain. |
| **upset** | upset | upset | upset | upsetting | The news upset her. |
| **quit** | quit | quit | quit | quitting | He quit the job. |
| **rid** | rid | rid | rid | ridding | Rid the house of rats. |
| **shed** | shed | shed | shed | shedding | The tree shed its leaves. |
| **slit** | slit | slit | slit | slitting | He slit the envelope. |
| **split** | split | split | split | splitting | They split the bill. |
| **thrust** | thrust | thrust | thrust | thrusting | He thrust the key in. |
| **bet** | bet | bet | bet | betting | He bet on the match. |
| **bid** | bid | bid | bid | bidding | She bid a high price. |

**read** is spelled the same, but the sound changes:

- V1 **read** = /riːd/  
- V2 / V3 **read** = /red/

Incorrect: He **cutted** the paper. / She **putted** the book.  
Correct: He **cut** the paper. / She **put** the book.
