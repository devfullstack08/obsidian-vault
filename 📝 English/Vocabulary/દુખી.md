
Sad — adjective
Sadly — adverb
Sadness — noun
  

Sorrowful  — adjective = દુઃખી / શોકગ્રસ્ત - deep sadness
Sorrowfully — adverb = દુઃખપૂર્વક
Sorrow — noun = દુઃખ / શોક

Unhappy

**Melancholy** = a **deep, quiet, thoughtful sadness**, often lasting for some time.
- **Melancholy** — noun/adjective
- **Melancholic** — adjective, less common

**Dejected** (rejection/failure + sad)= feeling **sad and discouraged because of failure, disappointment, or rejection**.
- **Deject** — verb = હતાશ કરવું
- **Dejected** — adjective = હતાશ
- **Dejection** — noun = હતાશા
- **Dejectedly** — adverb = હતાશાપૂર્વક


**Despondent** = feeling **very unhappy and without hope**, especially after disappointment or failure.
- **Despondency** — noun = નિરાશા / હતાશા
- **Despondent** — adjective = નિરાશ / આશાહીન
- **Despondently** — adverb = નિરાશાપૂર્વક

	Sad < Dejected < Despondent

**Gloomy** = **sad, dark, depressing, or without hope.**
- **Gloom** — noun = ઉદાસીનતા / અંધકાર
- **Gloomy** — adjective = ઉદાસ / અંધકારમય
- **Gloomily** — adverb = ઉદાસ રીતે

**Dismal(Bleak - અંધકારમય)** = very bad, gloomy, depressing, or causing sadness.
- **Dismal** — adjective
- **Dismally** — adverb
- very bad + depressing.

**Downcast(adj)** = **sad, discouraged, or lacking confidence**, often shown by someone’s facial expression or lowered eyes.

**Miserable** = very unhappy, uncomfortable, or suffering badly.
- **Misery** — noun = દુઃખ / કષ્ટ
- **Miserable** — adjective = ખૂબ દુઃખી
- **Miserably** — adverb = દુઃખદ રીતે

	Mesmerise - મંત્રમુગ્ધ કરો

**Poignant(Touching/Moving)** - causing a **strong feeling of sadness, emotion, or sympathy**, often in a touching way.
- **Poignant** — adjective
- **Poignancy** — noun
- **Poignantly** — adverb


- **Sad** = unhappy
- **Dejected** = sad because of disappointment/failure
- **Downcast** = visibly sad and discouraged
- **Despondent** = sad + almost no 
- **Melancholy** = deep, quiet, reflective sadness
- **Gloomy** = sad, dark, depressing
- **Dismal** = depressing **and often very poor/bad**
- **Poignant** = strong feeling of sadness
