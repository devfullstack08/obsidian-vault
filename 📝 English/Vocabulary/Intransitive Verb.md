Verbs of Light/Shining

| Verb          | Meaning                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Coruscate** | to sparkle or flash brightly         |
| **Glitter**   | to shine with small flashes          |
| **Gleam**     | to shine softly                      |
| **Glisten**   | to shine because of wetness          |
| **Shimmer**   | to shine with a soft moving light    |
| **Twinkle**   | to shine with repeated small flashes |
| **Sparkle**   | to shine brightly with flashes       |
| **Radiate**   | to send out light or heat            |
