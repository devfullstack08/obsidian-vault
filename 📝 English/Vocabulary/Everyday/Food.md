## Important  One-Word Vocabulary

**One who eats too much** → **Glutton**
**One who enjoys fine food** → **Epicure / Gourmet**
**A large formal meal** → **Banquet**
**Food fit to be eaten** → **Edible**
**Food unfit to be eaten** → **Inedible**
**A special or rare food** → **Delicacy**
**A strong desire for food** → **Craving**
**Extremely hungry** → **Famished / Ravenous**
**Food left after a meal** → **Leftovers**
**Food that forms the main part of a diet** → **Staple food**

### Best memory groups

**Eat quickly:** devour, gobble, gulp  
**Eat slowly/small amounts:** nibble, sip, savour  
**Very hungry:** starving, famished, ravenous  
**Bad food:** stale, rotten, spoiled, rancid  
**Good taste:** tasty, delicious, delectable, appetizing, palatable  
**No taste:** bland, tasteless  
**Too oily:** greasy  
**Not cooked:** raw  
**Too much eating:** gorge, binge, glutton
