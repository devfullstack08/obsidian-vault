## Types of work

| Word (Gujarati Meaning)             | Type             | Meaning / Small Description                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Portrait (મુખચિત્ર)                 | Noun             | A picture of a person, especially the face.                      |

| Still life (નિશ્ચલ વસ્તુઓનું ચિત્ર) | Noun             | A picture of objects such as fruit, flowers, or pots.            |

| Silhouette (પડછાયાકૃતિ)             | Noun             | A dark outline of a person or object against a light background. |

| Masterpiece (શ્રેષ્ઠ કૃતિ)          | Noun             | An artist’s greatest work.                                       |
| Etching (એચિંગ)                     | Noun             | A print made from a metal plate with acid-cut lines.             |
| Engraving (નકશી)                    | Noun             | A design cut into a hard surface, often used for prints.         |

---

Easy exam pairs:

- **Sketch** = quick drawing. **Portrait** = picture of a person.
- **Landscape** = nature scene. **Still life** = objects that do not move.
- **Mural** = on a wall. **Fresco** = on wet plaster.
- **Caricature** = funny exaggeration. **Silhouette** = dark outline.
- **Sculptor** = statues. **Painter** = paintings.
- **Abstract** = not realistic. **Realistic** = like real life.
