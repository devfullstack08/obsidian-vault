Mystique(noun) રહસ્યમયતા
It means a **special quality of mystery, charm, or fascination that makes a person, place, or thing seem interesting or attractive**.

- **Mystery** = something unexplained or unknown
- **Mystique** = the attractive aura or charm created by mystery
  
**Mystery = રહસ્ય**  
**Mystique = રહસ્યમય આકર્ષણ**.
