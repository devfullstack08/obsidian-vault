## Main classes

**Mammal**: A mammal (from Latin mamma 'breast') is a vertebrate animal of the class Mammalia. Mammals
characterized :
- Milk-producing : Gives milk to the young
The study of mammals is **mammalogy**.
Examples: cow, lion, whale, bat, human

**Bird**: Has feathers, lays eggs
Examples: crow, eagle, hen, sparrow

**Reptile**: Cold-blooded, dry scales
Examples: snake, lizard, crocodile, turtle

**Amphibian**(ઉભયજીવી): Lives on land and in water
Examples: frog, toad, salamander

**Fish**(ઉભયજીવી): Lives in water, has gills
Examples: shark, rohu, goldfish

**Insect**(ઉભયજીવી):Six legs, often wings
Examples: ant, bee, mosquito, butterfly

**Arachnid**(ઉભયજીવી): Eight legs
Examples: spider, scorpion, tick

**Crustacean**(ઉભયજીવી): Hard shell, mostly water
Examples: crab, lobster, prawn

**Mollusc**(ઉભયજીવી): Soft body, often a shell
Examples: snail, oyster, octopus


1. A **carnivorous mammal**:  is a **mammal that eats mainly meat**.

2. **Primate mammals** are mammals belonging to the biological order **Primates**.
   Common features:
	- relatively large brains
	- forward-facing eyes
	- flexible hands
	- usually five fingers/toes
	- good ability to grasp objects

3. **Hoofed mammals**:  are mammals that have **hooves** instead of ordinary feet or paws.

4. **Rodents mammals** :are mammals in the group **Rodentia**, usually recognized by their **continuously growing front teeth (incisors)** used for gnawing.

5. **Cetacean mammals** are **aquatic mammals** belonging to the group **Cetacea**
	Common features 
	- They live in water.
	- They breathe **air with lungs**, not gills.
	- They give birth to live young.
	- Mothers feed their babies with **milk**.
	- They must come to the surface to breathe.
- So even though they live in water, they are **mammals, not fish**.

6. **Bats are mammals**, and they are the **only mammals capable of true sustained flight**.
   Main features:
	- They have **hair/fur**
	- They breathe with **lungs**
	- They give birth to young ones
	- Mothers feed babies with **milk**
	- Their forelimbs are modified into **wings**
- **Bat = flying mammal, not a bird.**
-  Fruit bat
- Flying fox
- Vampire bat
- Microbat

7. **Marsupial mammals** are mammals whose babies are born **very early and underdeveloped**, then usually continue developing inside a **pouch** on the mother’s body.
- Marsupial = pouch mammal
-  **Kangaroo**
- **Koala**
- **Wallaby**
- **Wombat**
- **Opossum**
- **Tasmanian devil**

### Mammals by group

- **Carnivores** — lion, tiger, dog, coyote
- **Primates** — humans, monkeys
- **Rodents** — rat, squirrel, beaver
- **Hoofed mammals** — cow, deer, horse
- **Cetaceans** — whale, dolphin
- **Bats** — flying mammals
- **Marsupials** — kangaroo, koala

### How they eat

| Word            | Meaning                   | Example                |
| --------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Carnivore**   | Eats flesh                | lion, tiger            |
| **Herbivore**   | Eats plants               | cow, deer, goat        |
| **Omnivore**    | Eats plants and flesh     | bear, crow, human      |
| **Insectivore** | Eats insects              | frog, pangolin         |
| **Scavenger**   | Eats dead animals         | vulture, hyena, jackal |
| **Predator**    | Hunts other animals       | lion, eagle            |
| **Prey**        | The animal that is hunted | deer, rabbit           |
