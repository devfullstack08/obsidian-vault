### 5. Great Egret/S-necked(એક જાતનું લાંબાં પીંછાવાળું બગલું)
- A heron with mainly white plumage(પીંછાં), having long plumes in the breeding season.
- the male in full breeding **plumage**
- This long-legged, S-necked white bird is found throughout the Americas and around much of the world. It is typically the largest white egret occurring anywhere in its range (only the white-colored form of the great blue heron is larger).
- Diet: Carnivore
- Scientific Name: Ardea alba
- **Male/Female:** Male egret / Female egret
- **Young One:** Chick
- **Group:** Colony, heronry, or rookery
- **Home:** Nest, usually in trees or shrubs near water
- **Sound:** Croak, squawk, or harsh call
