Ostrich, Emu, Kiwi, Cassowary, Penguin

### 1.Ostrich(શાહમૃગ)
- A flightless swift-running African bird with a long neck, long legs, and two toes on each foot. 
- It is the largest living bird, with males reaching an average height of 8 feet (2.5 m).
- Male/Female: Cock - Hen
- Young One: Chick
- Group: Flock 
- Home: Nest
- Sound: Boom

### 2.Emu(વિશાળકાય ઑસ્ટ્રેલિયન પક્ષી (ઊડવાની શક્તિ વગરનું))
- A large flightless fast-running Australian bird resembling the ostrich, with shaggy gray or brown plumage, bare blue skin on the head and neck, and three-toed feet.
- Male/Female: Cock - Hen
- Young One: Chick
- Group: Mob 
- Home: Nest
- Sound: Boom/Drum
### 3. Kiwi (kiwi fruit)
- A flightless New Zealand bird with hairlike feathers, having a long down-curved bill with sensitive nostrils at the tip.
- Male/Female: Cock - Hen
- Young One: Chick
- Group: Mob 
- Home: Nest
- Sound: Boom/Drum
