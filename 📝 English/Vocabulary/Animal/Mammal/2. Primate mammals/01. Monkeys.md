### 1.Monkeys
- They are broadly categorized into **Old World monkeys** (Africa and Asia), which have narrow noses and sitting pads, and **New World monkeys** (Central and South America), which often feature flat noses and prehensile tails.
- Monkeys typically live in troops, exhibit stereoscopic vision for navigating trees, and are known for their dexterity and problem-solving abilities.
