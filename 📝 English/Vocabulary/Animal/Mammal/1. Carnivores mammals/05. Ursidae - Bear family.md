Brown Bear, Polar Bear, Black Bear, Sloth Bear
### 1. Brown Bear
- Europe, Asia, North America
- Large brown bear; includes the **grizzly bear**
- Male/Female: Boar - Sow
- Young One: Cub
- Group: Sleuth/Sloth 
- Home: Den
- Sound: Growl   
### 2. Polar Bear 
- Arctic
- White coat, excellent swimmer, mainly eats seals
### 3. Black Bear 
- Mainly North America and Asia
- Usually smaller than brown bears; color can vary
### 4. Sloth Bear 
- Indian subcontinent
- Shaggy black coat, long snout; feeds heavily on termites and ants

**Giant panda → Ursidae → bear family**  
**Red panda → Ailuridae → separate family**
### 5. Giant Panda 
- Black-and-white bamboo-eating bear of China
- Male/Female: Boar - Sow
- Young One: Cub
- Group: - 
- Home: -
- Sound: Bleat/Bark
