Spotted Hyena, Striped Hyena

### 1. Spotted Hyena(ઝરખ)
- the larger, more powerful hyena, with dark spots on its coat. 
- It mainly lives in sub-Saharan Africa and often hunts in groups called clans.
- Male/Female: Hyena - Hyena
- Young One: Cub  
- Group: Cackle/Clan
- Home: Den
- Sound: Laugh/Cackle 
### 2. Striped Hyena 
- smaller, with dark stripes on a lighter coat. 
- It is found across parts of Africa, the Middle East, and South Asia, including India. It is generally more solitary and scavenges more often.
