### 2.Wolf (વરુ,સ્ત્રીઓની પાછળ પડનાર માણસ)
- Native to both Eurasia and North America, but has been widely exterminated.
-  The wolf is nonetheless related closely enough to smaller _Canis_ species, such as the **coyote** and the golden jackal to produce fertile **hybrids** with them.
-  A carnivorethe wolf is an **apex predator** that feeds on large wild **hooved mammals** as well as smaller animals, livestock, **carrion(સડેલું માંસ)**, and garbage.
- Male/Female: Wolf - She-wolf
- Young One: Pup/Cub 
- Group: Pack
- Home: Den/Lair
- Sound:Howl/Cry 
- Idiom
	1. Cry Wolf = To raise a false alarm

### 6.Dingo(Australia)
- A wild or half-domesticated dog with a sandy-colored coat, found in Australia.
- The dingo is the largest mammalian carnivore in Australia (excluding humans!).
- Dingoes tend to howl rather than bark. They often howl at night to gather the pack and deter intruders. They also communicate via scent, defecating and urinating to mark their territory.
