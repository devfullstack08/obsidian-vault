Squirrel, Mouse, rat, beaver, porcupine
### 1. Squirrel(ખિસકોલી)
- An agile tree-dwelling rodent with a bushy tail, typically feeding on nuts and seeds.
- Scientific Name: Sciuridae
- Diet: Omnivore
- Male/Female: Buck - Doe
- Young One: Kit/Pup
- Group: Scurry, dray
- Home: Drey
- Sound: Chatter
- Size relative to a teacup: Five to 36 inches

### 2. Mouse
- Male/Female: Buck - Doe
- Young One: Pup/Pinky
- Group: Mischef/ Nest
- Home: Nest/Hole
- Sound: Squeak

### 3. Rat
- Male/Female: Buck - Doe
- Young One: Pup/Colony
- Group: Mischef/ Nest
- Home: Burrow/Nest
- Sound: Squeak

### 4. Beaver(કીંમતી રૂંવાદાર પ્રાણી)
- A large semiaquatic broad-tailed rodent that is native to North America and northern Eurasia. 
- It is noted for its habit of gnawing through tree trunks to fell the trees in order to feed on the bark and build dams.
- Scientific Name: Castoridae
- Male/Female: Boar - Sow
- Young One: Kit/ 
- Group: Colony  
- Home: Lodge 
- Sound: -  

- Some points: 
	- Beaver sculpture over entrance to the Canadian Parliament Building.
	- English verb "To beaver" means working with great effort and being "as busy as a beaver"; a "beaver intellect" refers to a way of thinking that is slow and honest.

### 5. Porcupine(શાહુડી)
- A large rodent with spiky outer fur for protection against predators.
- Diet: Herbivore
- Scientific Name: Hystricidae or Erethizontidae
- Male/Female: Boar - Sow
- Young One: Porcupette
- Group: Prickle
- Home: Den 
- Sound:Grunt
- Average Life Span In The Wild: 5 to 7 years
