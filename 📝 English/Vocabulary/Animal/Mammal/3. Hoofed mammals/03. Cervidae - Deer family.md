### 1. Deer(હરણ)
-  Male/Female: Buck/Stag - Doe/hind(full grown female deee)
- Young One: Fawn(baby)
- Group: Herd
- Home: -
- Sound:Bell/Bellow
### 2. Reindeer(શીત પ્રદેશનું હરણ, જેને ગાડીમાં જોડવામાં આવે છે)
- A deer of the tundra and subarctic regions of Eurasia and North America, both sexes of which have large branching antlers. 
- Most Eurasian reindeer are domesticated and used for drawing sleds and as a source of milk, flesh, and hide.
### 3. Elk (એક જાતનું મોટું હરણ)
- A large deer with palmate antlers, a sloping back, and a growth of skin hanging from the neck. 
- It is native to northern Eurasia and northern North America.
- Male/Female: Bull - Cow
- Young One: Calf
- Group: Gang/Heard 
- Home: -
- Sound:Bugle
### 4. Moose(એક જાતનું હરણ)
- A large deer with palmate antlers, a sloping back, and a growth of skin hanging from the neck. 
- It is native to northern Eurasia and northern North America.-  Male/Female: Buck/Stag - Doe/hind(full grown female deee)
- Male/Female: Bull - Cow
- Young One: Calf
- Group: Gang/Heard 
- Home: -
- Sound:Bugle
