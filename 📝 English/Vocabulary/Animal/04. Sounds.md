### Extra from your old notes

| Word | Type | Meaning |
| ---- | ---- | ------- |
| **Neigh** | Noun/Verb | Horse’s high-pitched cry. |
| **Whinny** | Noun/Verb | A **soft / gentle** neigh. |
| **Bray** | Noun/Verb | Cry of a donkey or mule. |
| **Bleat** | Noun/Verb | Cry of a sheep, goat, or calf. Also: speak weakly or foolishly. |

Easy memory:

**neigh / whinny** → horse  
**bray** → donkey  
**bleat** → sheep, goat  
**low / moo** → cow  
**trumpet** → elephant  
**hoot** → owl  
**caw** → crow  
**chirp** → small bird
