
| Word                       | Type | Meaning                                                                 |
| :------------------------- | :--- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Abode** (રહેઠાણ)         | Noun | A place where someone lives.                                            |
| **Residence** (નિવાસ)      | Noun | A person’s home or place of living.                                     |
| **Dwelling** (નિવાસસ્થાન)  | Noun | A house or place where people live.                                     |
| **Hometown** (વતન)         | Noun | The town where a person was born or grew up.                            |
| **Native Place** (મૂળ વતન) | Noun | The place where a person originally belongs.                            |
| **Village** (ગામ)          | Noun | A small settlement in a rural area.                                     |
| **Town** (નગર)             | Noun | A place larger than a village but smaller than a city.                  |
| **City** (શહેર)            | Noun | A large and important town.                                             |
| **Metropolis** (મહાનગર)    | Noun | A very large and important city.                                        |
| **Capital** (પાટનગર)       | Noun | The main city of a country or state where government is located.        |
| **Suburb** (ઉપનગર)         | Noun | A residential area outside a city.                                      |
| **Slum** (ગંદી વસાહત)      | Noun | A poor and crowded area of a city.                                      |
| **Hamlet** (નાનું ગામડું)  | Noun | A very small village.                                                   |
| **Province** (પ્રાંત)      | Noun | A region or administrative area of a country.                           |
| **District** (જિલ્લો)      | Noun | An administrative division of a state or country.                       |
| **State** (રાજ્ય)          | Noun | A political unit within a country.                                      |
| **Territory** (પ્રદેશ)     | Noun | An area of land under a government or ruler.                            |
| **Nation** (રાષ્ટ્ર)       | Noun | A large group of people with a common identity, culture, or government. |
| **Country** (દેશ)          | Noun | A nation with its own government and territory.                         |
| **Continent** (ખંડ)        | Noun | One of the large landmasses of the Earth.                               |
| **Desert** (રણ)            | Noun | A dry area with very little rainfall.                                   |
| **Oasis** (મરુદ્યાન)       | Noun | A fertile place in a desert where water is found.                       |
| **Coast** (દરિયાકાંઠો)     | Noun | Land beside the sea.                                                    |
