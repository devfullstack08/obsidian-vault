
## Meaning of Lake

A lake is a large body of water surrounded by land.

Lakes may contain freshwater, saltwater, or brackish water.

## Types of Lakes in India

| Type of Lake        | Meaning                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Freshwater Lake     | Lake with fresh water                       |
| Saltwater Lake      | Lake with salty water                       |
| Brackish Water Lake | Mixture of freshwater and saltwater         |
| Lagoon Lake         | Coastal lake separated from sea by sandbars |
| Glacial Lake        | Formed by melting glaciers                  |
| Tectonic Lake       | Formed due to earth movements               |
| Oxbow Lake          | Formed from a curved river meander          |
| Artificial Lake     | Man-made lake or reservoir                  |
|                     |                                             |
