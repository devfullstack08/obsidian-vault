# 5. Explaining Something Difficult

These are very useful vocabulary words.

| Word                                    | Type      | Meaning                       |
| --------------------------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Lucid (સ્પષ્ટ / સરળતાથી સમજાય એવું)** | Adjective | Clear and easy to understand. |

### Clarify

Make something clearer.

> Please **clarify** your question.

### Elucidate

Make a difficult idea easier to understand.

> The professor **elucidated** the theory.

### Explicate

Explain something carefully and thoroughly.

> The author **explicates** the meaning of the poem.

### Simplify

Make something easier to understand.

> Can you **simplify** this explanation?

### Break down

Explain something by dividing it into smaller parts.

> Let me **break down** this grammar rule.

# 6. Explaining Step by Step

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**walk someone through**|explain step by step|
|**talk someone through**|verbally guide someone through something|
|**break something down**|divide it into simple parts|
|**go through**|examine/explain each part|
|**go over**|review or explain|
|**guide**|help someone understand or complete something|
|**instruct**|tell someone how to do something|

Example:

> Can you **walk me through** the installation process?

Meaning:

> Can you explain the installation process step by step?
