 Reckon(verb) = મને લાગે છે, ઓળખાય છે , આશા રાખવી 
1. . To think or believe something 
	- e.g I reckon it's going to rain later today. 
2. To calculate or estimate something
    - He reckon that the trip will take about two hours.
3. To consider or regard in a specified way 
	- She is reckoned to be one of the best tennis players. 
4. To expect or anticipate 
	- I reckon on your support in this matter. 
5. Reckoning : serious consideration, judgment, or settling of accounts
	  - A reckoning is a time when you have to face the consequences of your actions, often involving a confrontation or realization that come after a period of avoidance or suspension. 
	  - e.g After years of ignoring his problems , the day of reckoning finally arrived when he had to take responsibility .
