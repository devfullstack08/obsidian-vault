Uncountable nouns take singular verbs

Examples:

- furniture
- information
- advice
- luggage    
- equipment
- machinery
- knowledge
- bread
- scenery

> The scenery of Kashmir is beautiful.
> The furniture **is** expensive.  
> This information **is** useful.  
> His luggage **was** lost.

Never write:

> The furnitures are ❌  
> The informations are ❌
