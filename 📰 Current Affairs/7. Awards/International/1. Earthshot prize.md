The Earthshot Prize is an international environmental award established in 2020 by Prince William of the United kingdom.

It's main objective is to discover, support and scale innovative solutions to the world's biggest environmental problems by 2030.

Key facts 

| Particular        | Information                                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Established       | 2020                                            |
| Founder           | Prince William                                  |
| First awards      | 2021, London                                    |
| Main objective    | Repair and restore the planet                   |
| Traget year       | 2030                                            |
| Frequency         | Annually                                        |
| Number of winners | 5 each year                                     |
| Prize money       | £1 million per winner                           |
| Inspiration       | US President John F.Kennedy's Moonshot ambition |

### Five Earthshot Prize categories

**1. Protect and Restore Nature** — Protect forests, biodiversity and natural ecosystems.

**2. Clean Our Air** — Reduce air pollution and improve air quality.

**3. Revive Our Oceans** — Protect marine ecosystems and ocean life.

**4. Build a Waste-Free World** — Reduce pollution, plastic waste and unnecessary resource consumption.

**5. Fix Our Climate** — Develop solutions to address climate change.
