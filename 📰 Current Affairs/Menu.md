## **I Monetary Policy — Repo Rate**

Category: Economy & Banking · High priority

**1. Why in the News?**

Whenever the RBI announces a monetary policy decision, record the decision, its effective date and the official source.

**2. Key Facts**

- RBI = Reserve Bank of India.
    
- Repo rate = Rate at which RBI provides short-term liquidity to banks against eligible securities through repurchase transactions.
    
- MPC = Monetary Policy Committee.
    
- MPC has six members.
    
- The inflation target framework has a CPI target of 4%, with a tolerance band of ±2 percentage points, under the framework applicable through March 2026; verify any subsequent framework before recording it as current.

**Answer:** RBI's Monetary Policy Committee.
