## ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations)

- Established: 8 August 1967
- Founded at: Bangkok, Thailand
- Founding Declaration: Bangkok Declaration
- Headquarters: Jakarta, Indonesia
- Total Members: 11
- Founding Members: 5
  - Indonesia
  - Malaysia
  - Philippines
  - Singapore
  - Thailand
- Latest Member: Timor-Leste (26 October 2025)
- Official Working Language: English
- ASEAN Day: 8 August
- Motto: One Vision, One Identity, One Community
- Objective: Regional peace, economic growth
  and cooperation
- ASEAN Charter: Signed on 20 November 2007
- Decision-making: Consultation and consensus
- India is not a member of ASEAN.
- India became a Comprehensive Strategic
  Partner in 2022.

### Three Pillars
- Political-Security Community (APSC)
- Economic Community (AEC)
- Socio-Cultural Community (ASCC)

### Important Terms
- AFTA: ASEAN Free Trade Area
- ARF: ASEAN Regional Forum
- ASEAN+3: ASEAN + China, Japan, South Korea
