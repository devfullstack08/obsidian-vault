**ILO — International Labour Organization**

Is a specialised agency of the **United Nations** that promotes workers' rights, decent employment, social protection and social justice worldwide.

| Particular            | Information                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Full form             | International Labour Organization          |
| Established           | **1919**                                   |
| Headquarters          | **Geneva, Switzerland**                    |
| Origin                | Treaty of Versailles, after World War I    |
| UN specialised agency | Since 1946                                 |
| Members               | 187 member states                          |
| Unique feature        | Tripartite structure                       |
| Nobel Peace Prize     | **1969**                                   |
| Major report          | World Employment and Social Outlook (WESO) |

### **2. What makes ILO unique?**

The ILO is the only tripartite UN specialised agency, meaning three groups participate in developing international labour standards.

**ILO** (Tripartite Structure)
1. Government
2. Employer
3. Worker 
Government + Employer + Worker representatives participate in ILO decision-making. 

### **3. ILO and India**

- India is a **founding member** of the ILO (1919).
- India has been a permanent member of its Governing Body since 1922.
- ILO standards are relevant to labour laws, workplace safety, child labour, forced labour and social security.
- The ILO contributes to **SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth**.
