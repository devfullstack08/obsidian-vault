### India's First Indigenous H5N1 Vaccine (2026)

- **Date:** 20 November 2026
- **Developed by:** ICAR–National Institute of High Security Animal Diseases (NIHSAD)
- **Location:** Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh
- **Dedicated by:** Shivraj Singh Chouhan
- **Disease:** Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (Bird Flu)
- **Virus:** Influenza A (H5N1), Clade 2.3.2.1a
- **Type:** Inactivated vaccine
- **Target:** Chickens (not humans)
- **Announced price:** ₹1–2 per dose
- **Significance:** Indigenous veterinary biotechnology, poultry protection and reduced economic losses
- **Additional announcement:** ₹300-crore animal disease research laboratory in Bhopal

### Static GK Connection

- First reported H5N1 outbreak in India: 2006, Navapur (Maharashtra)
- HPAI: Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza
- H5N1: Subtype of Influenza A virus
