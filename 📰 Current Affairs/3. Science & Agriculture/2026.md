Historic milestone · 18 November 2026: **Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth

Distance = **25.9 billion km**
One-way signal time = **24 hours**

Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to reach the distance light travels in one full day.

That means a radio signal travelling at light speed takes about **24 hours to reach Voyager 1**, and a response takes another 24 hours to reach Earth.
