
Generational names classify people born during specific periods who share similar social, cultural, technological, and economic experiences.
## Timeline

| Generation          | Birth Years \| | Key Characteristics                                 |
| ------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Baby Boomers        | 1946–1964      | Post-WWII generation                                |
| Generation X        | 1965–1980      | Grew up before the Internet                         |
| Millennials (Gen Y) | 1981–1996      | Internet generation                                 |
| Generation Z        | 1997–2012      | Digital natives                                     |
| Generation Alpha    | 2013–2024      | Born into AI & smart devices                        |
| Generation Beta     | 2025 onwards   | Expected to grow up with advanced AI and automation |

## Important Points

- Gen Z grew up with smartphones and social media.
- Gen Alpha is the first generation born entirely in the 21st century.
- Gen Beta is expected to live in an AI-driven world.
