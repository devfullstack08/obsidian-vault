
| Year | Important event                                | Why it mattered                                                                                                              |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1802 | Treaty of Bassein                              | Increased British influence over the Marathas                                                                                |
| 1813 | Charter Act (2)                                | Ended most of the Company's Indian trade monopoly; opened greater scope for missionary activity                              |
| 1828 | Brahmo Sabha established by Raja Rammohan Roy  | Major development in social and religious reform                                                                             |
| 1829 | Sati abolished under Lord William Bentinck     | Landmark social legislation                                                                                                  |
| 1833 | Charter Act (3)                                | Ended the Company's commercial activities and strengthened centralization                                                    |
| 1835 | Macaulay's Minute and English education policy | Strengthened colonial promotion of English-language higher education                                                         |
| 1853 | First passenger railway (Bombay–Thane)         | Beginning of passenger rail transport in India                                                                               |
|      | Charter Act (4)                                | Last Charter Act; separated legislative and executive functions and paved the way for competitive civil service recruitment. |
| 1856 | Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act                   | Significant social reform legislation                                                                                        |
| 1857 | Revolt of 1857                                 | Major uprising against Company rule                                                                                          |
| 1858 | Government of India Act                        | Company rule ended; British Crown took direct control                                                                        |
| 1861 | Indian Councils Act                            | Reorganized legislative councils with limited Indian participation                                                           |
| 1878 | Vernacular Press Act                           | Restricted freedom of sections of the Indian-language press                                                                  |
| 1885 | Indian National Congress founded               | Beginning of an enduring all-India nationalist political organization                                                        |
| 1892 | Indian Councils Act                            | Expanded representation and limited discussion rights                                                                        |
