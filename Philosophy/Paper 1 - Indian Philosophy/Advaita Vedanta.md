# Advaita Vedanta

Advaita Vedanta is a school of orthodox (*Astika*) Indian philosophy, chief-proponed by **Adi Shankaracharya**. It is a system of absolute non-dualism (monism), which asserts that Brahman (the ultimate reality) and Atman (the individual self) are one and the same.

---

## 1. Conception of Reality (*Sat*)

Shankaracharya defines and compares reality using the following foundational Sanskrit dictum:

> **"Trikala-abadhitvam lakshanam sat"** (ત્રિકાલ અબાધિતત્વં લક્ષણં સત્ / त्रिकालाबाधितत्वं लक्षणं सत्)
> 
> * **Literal Meaning:** *"Reality is that which remains uncontradicted and unsubrated in all three divisions of time (past, present, and future)."*

### Key Analysis of the Definition:
* Under this strict comparison of reality, if something changes or is contradicted at any point in time, it cannot be called *absolute reality*.
* Based on this, Shankaracharya classifies reality into **Three Levels of Existence (Satta-traya / સત્તા-ત્રય)**:

1. **Paramarthika Satta (Absolute Reality):**
   * Only **Brahman** is absolutely real. Brahman is eternal, unchanging, and never contradicted (*abadhita*) by any higher experience.
2. **Vyavaharika Satta (Empirical / Practical Reality):**
   * The physical world we live in. It is practically real for daily activities, but it is **relative**. It is contradicted/subrated once a person achieves ultimate self-realization (*Brahma-jnana*).
3. **Pratibhasika Satta (Apparent / Subjective Reality):**
   * Illusions, dreams, or mistaking a rope for a snake. It is real only as long as the illusion lasts. It is contradicted immediately upon waking up or gaining correct perception.

---

## 2. Main Pillars of Advaita Vedanta

Shankaracharya's philosophy is summarized in the famous verse:
> *"Brahma Satyam Jagan Mithya, Jivo Brahmaiva Naparah"*
> 
> (Brahman is real, the world is unreal/illusory, and the individual soul is none other than Brahman).

### A. Brahman (Ultimate Reality)
* Brahman is the only ultimate, non-dual reality (*Sat*).
* It is *Nirguna* (without attributes), *Nirakara* (without form), and is described as **Sat-Chit-Ananda** (Absolute Existence-Consciousness-Bliss).

### B. Jiva (Individual Self)
* Jiva is the individual self. Due to ignorance (*Avidya*), the Jiva falsely identifies itself with the physical body and mind.
* In essence, the Jiva is identical to Brahman.

### C. Maya (The Principle of Illusion)
* Maya is the power that projects the changing world and hides the true nature of Brahman.
* It is *Anirvachaniya* (indescribable as either real or unreal).

---

## 3. Liberation (*Moksha*)

* **Cause of Bondage:** Ignorance (*Avidya*).
* **Path to Liberation:** Right Knowledge (*Jnana Yoga*). 
* **Moksha:** The realization of the absolute identity between Atman and Brahman. Advaita accepts **Jivanmukti** (liberation while still living in the physical body).
