# The 24 Tirthankaras of Jainism (૨૪ તીર્થંકર)

In Jainism, a **Tirthankara** (તીર્થંકર - "Ford-maker") is an ordinary human soul who attains absolute knowledge (*Kevala-jnana*) through intense penance and teaches the path to liberation.

## 2. In-Depth Details of Key Tirthankaras

### A. Rishabhadeva (1st Tirthankara)
* **Other Names:** Adinatha, Yugadideva.
* **Vedic Reference:** Mentioned in the *Rigveda* (which lists his name with high reverence(આદર)) and the *Bhagavata Purana* (where he is considered an incarnation of Vishnu).
* **Contributions:** Jains believe he taught humanity the arts of agriculture, cooking, writing (*Brahmi script*, named after his daughter Brahmi), and mathematics.
* **Family:** His elder son was **King Bharata** (after whom India is named *Bharata-varsha*), and his second son was **Bahubali** (celebrated with the colossal statue at Shravanabelagola).

### B. Mallinatha (19th Tirthankara)
* **The Gender Controversy:**
  * **Shvetambara Sect:** Argues that Mallinatha was a **woman** (Princess Malli of Mithila).
  * **Digambara Sect:** Argues that women cannot attain liberation directly (they must be reborn as men first). Therefore, they maintain Mallinatha was a **man**.

### C. Neminatha (22nd Tirthankara)
* **Krishna Connection:** Jains believe he was the cousin of Lord Krishna (son of Samudravijaya, brother of Krishna's father Vasudeva).
* **Girnar Connection:** His renunciation, penance, and ultimate liberation occurred on **Mount Girnar (Junagadh, Gujarat)**, making it a highly sacred site (*Tirth*).

### D. Parshvanatha (23rd Tirthankara)
* **Historicity:** Universally accepted by modern historians as a historical figure who lived around 250 years before Mahavira (circa 8th-7th century BCE).
* **Teachings:** He introduced the **Chaturyama Dharma** (Four-fold path of restraint):
  1. *Ahimsa* (Non-violence)
  2. *Satya* (Truthfulness)
  3. *Asteya* (Non-stealing)
  4. *Aparigraha* (Non-possession)

### E. Mahavira (24th Tirthankara)
* **Life Details:** Born in Kundagrama (near Vaishali, Bihar) into the Jnatrika clan. Attained Keval-Jnana under a Sal tree on the banks of River Rijupalika.
* **Teachings:** He accepted Parshvanatha's 4 vows and added the 5th vow: **Brahmacharya** (Chastity). He also popularized **Anekantavada** and **Syadvada**.
* **Nirvana:** Attained Moksha at **Pawapuri** (Bihar) at the age of 72.

## 3. Spiritual Significance of Sammed Shikharji (Jharkhand)

* **Location:** Located on **Parasnath Hill** in the Giridih district of **Jharkhand**, India. 
* **Elevation:** It is the highest peak in Jharkhand (1,350 meters).
* **The "Moksha-Bhumi" (Land of Liberation):**
  * **20 out of the 24 Tirthankaras** achieved Nirvana (liberation) at this single mountain.
  * The mountain is named "Parasnath" after the 23rd Tirthankara, **Lord Parshvanatha**, who also attained liberation here.
  * The only 4 Tirthankaras who did **not** attain Nirvana here are:
    1. **Rishabhadeva (1st)** — Mount Ashtapada (Kailash)
    2. **Vasupujya (12th)** — Champapuri (Bihar)
    3. **Neminatha (22nd)** — Mount Girnar (Gujarat)
    4. **Mahavira (24th)** — Pawapuri (Bihar)
