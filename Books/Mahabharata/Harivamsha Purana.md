**Harivamsha Purana** is commonly called **“Harivamsha”** or **“Harivamsha Parva.”**

It is **not usually counted as one of the 18 Maha Puranas** or the 18 Upa Puranas. Instead, it is traditionally considered a **khila** — an **appendix or supplement to the Mahabharata**.

**Meaning of Harivamsha:**

**Hari** = Lord Vishnu / Krishna  
**Vamsha** = lineage or family line

So **Harivamsha** means **“the lineage of Hari,”** especially the family history and divine story of **Lord Krishna**.

It mainly describes:

1. The genealogy of Vishnu/Krishna
2. The Yadava dynasty
3. The birth and childhood stories of Krishna
4. Krishna’s life before the main Mahabharata events
5. Creation stories and cosmic teachings

In simple words:

**Harivamsha Purana is the “family lineage/story of Lord Hari,” especially Lord Krishna. It is considered a supplement to the Mahabharata, not one of the main 18 Puranas.**
