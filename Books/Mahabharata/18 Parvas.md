The Mahabharata consists of 18 Parvas, beginning with Adi Parva and ending with Svargarohana Parva.
**Parva** means **book/section**.

| No. | Parva                           | Short description                                                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Adi Parva**                   | Beginning of the story; origin of the Kuru dynasty, birth of Pandavas and Kauravas.         |
| 2   | **Sabha Parva**                 | Yudhishthira’s royal assembly, dice game, and Draupadi’s humiliation.                       |
| 3   | **Vana Parva or Aranya parav ** | Pandavas’ 12-year exile in the forest.                                                      |
| 4   | **Virata Parva**                | Pandavas live secretly in King Virata’s kingdom during the 13th year.                       |
| 5   | **Udyoga Parva**                | Efforts for peace before the war; Krishna’s peace mission.                                  |
| 6   | **Bhishma Parva**               | Beginning of the Kurukshetra war; Bhishma as commander; **Bhagavad Gita** is included here. |
| 7   | **Drona Parva**                 | Drona becomes commander; Abhimanyu is killed in the Chakravyuha.                            |
| 8   | **Karna Parva**                 | Karna becomes commander; battle between Arjuna and Karna.                                   |
| 9   | **Shalya Parva**                | Shalya becomes commander; Duryodhana is defeated by Bhima.                                  |
| 10  | **Sauptika Parva**              | Ashwatthama attacks the sleeping Pandava camp at night.                                     |
| 11  | **Stri Parva**                  | Lamentation(વિલાપ) of women after the war; Gandhari and others mourn.                       |
| 12  | **Shanti Parva**                | Bhishma teaches Yudhishthira about kingship, duty, and peace.                               |
| 13  | **Anushasana Parva**            | Bhishma gives more teachings on dharma, charity, and conduct.                               |
| 14  | **Ashvamedhika Parva**          | Yudhishthira performs the Ashvamedha sacrifice.                                             |
| 15  | **Ashramavasika Parva**         | Dhritarashtra, Gandhari, and Kunti retire to the forest.                                    |
| 16  | **Mausala Parva**               | Destruction of the Yadava clan.                                                             |
| 17  | **Mahaprasthanika Parva**       | Pandavas leave their kingdom and begin their final journey.                                 |
| 18  | **Svargarohana Parva**          | Yudhishthira reaches heaven; conclusion of the Mahabharata.                                 |
