
| Principle              | Application                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Integrity              | Refuse to compromise your duties for personal advancement.                  |
| Impartiality           | Investigate without favouring or targeting anyone.                          |
| Objectivity            | Base findings on verified evidence rather than political allegations.       |
| Courage of conviction  | Uphold your duties despite intimidation.                                    |
| Accountability         | Ensure public funds are traceable and wrongdoing is addressed.              |
| Compassion and empathy | Protect the poor families whose benefits were diverted.                     |
| Rule of law            | Respect legal procedures and the accused officer's right to a fair process. |

> A good civil servant is not merely someone who refuses corruption, but someone who protects public interest through lawful, fair and effective action.
