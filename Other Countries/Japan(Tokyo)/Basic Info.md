## Physical geography
- **Mount Fuji:** Highest mountain of Japan — approximately **3,776 metres**.
- **Japanese Alps:** Mountain ranges in central Honshu.
- **Shinano River:** Longest river in Japan.
- **Lake Biwa:** Largest freshwater lake by surface area in Japan, located on Honshu.
- **Kanto Plain:** Japan's largest plain; includes Tokyo and surrounding areas.=
- **Volcanoes:** Japan contains numerous active volcanoes.(fujiyama - Pacific Ring of Fire)
